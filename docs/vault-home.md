# Crafta Docs

Index of repo documentation. The rules stay in their own files; the project `CLAUDE.md` imports the writing docs so every session loads them. The pages under Overview and Content are synthesis: they summarise and link to the rule files, and lose any disagreement with them. [[writing/editorial-framework]] wins over everything here.

## Overview

- [[about-crafta]]: what Crafta is, the owner's design purpose (including the active climbing activewear brief), how readers meet a post, current counts and gaps.

## Content

- [[content/topics-and-categories]]: the six categories in practice, recurring subjects, the owner's taste, what is out of scope, how ideas become posts.
- [[content/sources-philosophy]]: what Crafta trusts and why, publications by use, weak evidence, anchor claims and labelled analysis.
- [[content/post-anatomy]]: post generations and lengths, exemplar posts, older posts that are not style models, vault note conventions.

## Writing

- [[writing/editorial-framework]]: the three lenses (Observation, Transformation, Stance), Narrative, voice, structure, quality essentials. Source of truth for how Crafta posts are written.
- [[writing/lessons]]: running log of corrections, recurring patterns and the drafting process. Loses any disagreement with the framework.

## Elsewhere in the repo

- `AGENTS.md` (repo root): how to work with the owner. Modes of work, writing gates, scope filter, subagent briefs, where corrections get written. Imported by `CLAUDE.md`.
- `.claude/research-protocol.md`: research methodology, source tiers, footnoting.
- `.claude/feeds.md`: RSS feeds, domains, sitemaps.
- `.claude/skills/`: `generate-posts`, `discover`, `mood-board`, `triage`, `source`, `idea-bank`, `editorial-calendar`, `manage-feeds`.
- `bank/`: the Obsidian content vault (ideas, research, posts, sources).
