## In One Line
A Swedish climbing and outdoor maker built around repair and long life; it is the clearest repair-first brand in the function-driven thread, though the vault holds only its own account.

## Lineage
The maker says it was founded by "a tightly-knit band of local climbers in the city of Umeå in 1975" as a do-it-yourself workshop [^1]. The page footer lists Åre as the current base [^1]. This is a maker claim from a web summary, and it is T1 for what the brand says about itself.

## Making and Material
The About page states an approach to "repair equipment until it falls apart and to continually strive to improve constructions" [^1]. It gives no production country, no cloth names and no weights, so Making and Material cannot be scored above the basics. The maker claims the first outdoor brand with a fully fluorocarbon-free collection in September 2017; that is a maker claim and was not checked [^1].

## Patina and Care
The stated goal is "the most durable and long-lived equipment imaginable", and a recycling system started in 2009 [^1]. The page gives no explicit repair terms or guarantee, so the repair offer is an intention in the vault, not a documented service.

## Today
No product list, price or retailer is in the vault. Research in [[Research/Workwear and Sportswear - Function and Ontology]] records that Patagonia and Klättermusen both state repair-first philosophies and that no climbing pant with a modular replaceable panel was found.
