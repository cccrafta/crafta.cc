## In One Line
A London label whose menswear and sport footwear borrow from uniforms, workwear and 1970s running shoes; the ASICS split-toe collaboration is the part that touches Crafta's function-driven thread.

## Lineage
Kiko Kostadinov showed a debut collection in January 2017, so the label has no long production history [^1]. Wikipedia describes the early brief as "modern-day uniform and contemporary workwear" and lists the early partners: Stussy in 2015, Camper in 2018, Levi's in 2024 and a Fox Racing capsule in 2025 [^1]. He was creative director of Mackintosh 0001 from November 2017 and no longer is [^1]. This lead is a single T4 source and was not confirmed in the London brands research.

## Making and Material
The vault does not say where the main line is produced; Wikipedia gives no production countries [^1]. The one construction description is for a single shoe: the ASICS LYASA FF Tabi is a slip-on with a soft elastic upper and a rubber and EVA sole, drawn from the 1970s ASICS Tabi marathon shoes [^2]. That page is a model summary, so the detail is second-hand.

## Patina and Care
No source in the vault.

## Today
ASICS footwear began with SS2018 and a Novalis line was announced in 2023 [^1]. The LYASA FF Tabi released on 11 April 2026 as the second instalment of a four-part collaboration first shown at the womenswear FW2025 Paris show [^2]. Its metallic graphic nods to Herb Lubalin's late-1980s ASICS logo proposals [^2].

## Design
The pieces pair sport references with uniform cut, and the ASICS Tabi quotes a real running shoe rather than inventing a form. Taste score: the eye is distinct and the reference to a documented 1970s shoe suits the owner's interest in sport and work lineage [unsourced].
