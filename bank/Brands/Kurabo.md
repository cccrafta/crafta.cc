## Making and Material
Blackhorse Lane Ateliers says its denim comes from Japanese selvedge, Italian and Turkish mills, and names Kurabo (Japan) on its channels.[^1] That tells us Kurabo supplies selvedge cloth to a London jeans maker; weights, looms and cotton are not stated.
