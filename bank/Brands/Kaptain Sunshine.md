## In One Line
Japanese menswear label designed by Keita Kojima. Its safari jackets are one of the Japanese reinterpretations that the Safari Jacket research treats as craft rather than costume.

## Lineage
Designer Keita Kojima; founding year `[unsourced]`. The Safari Jacket research cites Heddels and Hypebeast for the claims below (footnote 12 there); there is no Reference note in the vault.

## Making and Material
The Belted Safari Light Jacket is in Japanese-made Giza cotton and is said to take "the fuss out of traditional safari jackets"; the Safari Shirt Jacket is silk, linen and cotton; the design draws on the American P41 military jacket in cotton and linen blends. Fit is said to be one "that cannot be replicated elsewhere" (maker framing as quoted).
