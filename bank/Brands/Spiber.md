## In One Line
Japanese biotech firm that ferments plant sugar into a structural protein fibre, Brewed Protein, and sells it into outdoor and sportswear labels. A material case: the fibre is real, the scale claims are mixed.

## Lineage
Joint development with Goldwin began in 2015, and the first product was The North Face Planetary Equilibrium Tee in August 2019, sold by lottery.[^4] The company's own founding date is not in the vault. [unsourced]

## Making and Material
The fibre is made by fermentation, then processed into staple, tops or sliver, and spun yarn, with a silk-like sheen.[^1] The Nuptse outer in the 2023 collection is 60% Brewed Protein, 25% wool and 15% nylon, with a PTFE laminate on the reverse.[^4] Spiber announced a Rayong, Thailand plant on 10 July 2019 with "several hundred tonnes per year" and production expected in 2021.[^3] Goldwin says polymer mass production began in early 2022.[^4] A 500 t/yr figure appears only in secondary coverage; the dates and figure conflict and are left unresolved.[^5]

## Patina and Care
No source in the vault on how the fibre ages in wear. End-of-life breakdown is shown at lab scale only.[^1]

## Today
The first mass-produced collection launched 29 September 2023 with 17 products from four labels: The North Face, Goldwin, nanamica and Woolrich; Goldwin's March 2023 release names five brands.[^4] Goldwin's goal is 10% of newly developed products by 2030.[^4] By August 2025 Spiber reported 45 brands, 193 products and more than 80 companies.[^1] Land Cruiser Prado seat covers were announced in October 2025.[^2]

## Design
The cloth is the design. Judged on the vault evidence, the fibre keeps its own sheen and is blended with wool and nylon, so its role is an ingredient more than a visible fabric.[^4]
