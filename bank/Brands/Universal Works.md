## In One Line
A British menswear label launched in 2008 that produces in the UK, Portugal and India; whether it is a London or a Nottingham brand is in conflict in the vault, so the city is left blank.

## Lineage
A Peggs and Son retailer page says David Keyte and Stephanie Porritt launched Universal Works in 2008 [^1]. That page points to Nottingham origins, while the London Brands research lists it under "Not London: Universal Works, Sunspel (Nottingham)" [unsourced]. Permanent Style's London shopping guide lists the Coal Drops Yard shop as worth a visit [^2]. A shop in London does not decide where the brand is based, and the conflict is kept open.

## Making and Material
The retailer says production runs across the UK, Portugal and India [^1]. That makes it a UK brand with part of its production in India, so it is not excluded from scope. A Harris Tweed range from Kenneth Mackenzie appears in a search summary of the retailer page and is not confirmed in the page text [^1]. No mill, weight or construction detail is documented.
