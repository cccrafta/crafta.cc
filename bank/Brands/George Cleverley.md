## In One Line
A Mayfair bespoke shoemaker, established 1958 and still run by the family that took it on. It is known for the chisel-toed Cleverley shape.

## Lineage
George Cleverley was born on 10 August 1898 and spent 38 years at Tuczek on Clifford Street before founding G.J. Cleverley on Cork Street, Mayfair, in 1958 [^1]. In 1978 he chose two pupils, John Carnera and George Glasgow, to take over; he died in 1991, almost 93, still working [^1]. The business is now at 13 The Royal Arcade, 28 Old Bond Street, led by George Glasgow Jr as CEO and creative director [^1].

## Making and Material
The signature is the chisel-toed Cleverley shape [^1]. The maker's page does not say where shoes are made; a Royal Arcade workshop appears only in a search summary [single-source] [^1]. Permanent Style records three bespoke pairs over a 13-part series, with fit problems at the outside joints and the little toe, and notes that the author keeps the black pair because they are beautifully made [^2]. No leathers, lasts or construction method are named in the vault.

## Patina and Care
No maker source on resoling or repair. The one owner account is the author who kept the black pair [^2].

## Today
Still a family business at the Royal Arcade [^1]. Permanent Style's 2026 survey of bespoke shoemakers covers it among 20 makers the author has known [^2].

## Design
A chisel-toed shape with a recognisable outline [^1]. Permanent Style's fit problems at the outside joints and little toe show the shape is not a guarantee of a comfortable first fitting [^2].

## Depth
