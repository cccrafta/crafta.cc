## In One Line
London handsewn shoemaker run by two shoemaking teachers. Both made and taught at the same studio, with a mixed verdict from Permanent Style on its boots.

## Lineage
Deborah Carre and James Ducker met as handsewn-shoemaking apprentices under master shoemaker Paul Wilson and set up Carreducker in December 2004 [^1]. James apprenticed at John Lobb, St James's, and taught at Cordwainers College; Deborah won a QEST scholarship [^1].

## Making and Material
The studio is at Unit 1.20, Oxo Tower Wharf, Bargehouse Street, London SE1 9PH [^1]. It offers classes in shoemaking, closing and pattern cutting plus made-to-order boots and clogs [^1]. Permanent Style says the boots come by remote fitting with some machine stitching, "pretty good" but not top-maker finishing [^2]. Leather and lasts are not named in the vault.

## Patina and Care
No repair or resoling service described on the founders' page [^1]. Handsewn construction suits resoling, but the vault does not show the brand offering it `[unsourced]`.

## Today
Classes and made-to-order work from the Oxo Tower studio [^1]. London Brands rates it a Tier 2 London maker, "bespoke made in Oxo Tower studio; founders Cordwainers teachers".

## Design
Boots and clogs in a workshop-led, plain idiom; no product images are in the vault, so taste is judged from description only.

## Depth
A teaching workshop with named training lines (Paul Wilson, John Lobb, Cordwainers) is a good primary source on handsewn shoemaking technique; a repair offer would raise it.
