## In One Line
Brand of Toyo Enterprise, the Japanese reproduction group that also owns Buzz Rickson's. Evidence is scattered across research notes, so scoring leans on those notes and one Heddels profile.

## Lineage
Toyo Enterprise, parent of Buzz Rickson's, Sugar Cane, Sun Surf, Tailor Toyo and Headlight, traces to Kosho & Co. in the 1940s, which made garments for American servicemen in Japan.[^1] The Sugar Cane '2025' cut recreates a transitional 1930s design.[^2]

## Making and Material
The Okinawa sugarcane-fibre denim is a 50/50 cotton blend at 11oz, as used in the Mister Freedom FW2024 CPO.[^3] A moleskin French work coat was sewn on vintage Union Special machines, per a 2019 note.[^4] Headlight, a sister label, makes a 10oz herringbone twill reproduction.[^5]

## Today
Active across several labels, with current collaborations in the research notes.[^2][^5]

## Design
Reproduction of period cuts at the level of detail Heddels describes for Buzz Rickson's: the group copies, then adjusts only where cloth or sewing demands it.[^1]

## Depth
One Heddels profile plus five research mentions across denim, moleskin and HBT cases.
