## In One Line
A label owned by the Tokyo retailer Nepenthes, sold next to Engineered Garments. The vault knows it as a house line and a track-wear line, with almost nothing on its own making.

## Lineage
Nepenthes was conceived in Tokyo in 1988 by Keizo Shimizu, began with US-made goods, moved to collaborations and then to its own lines.[^2] Needles is one of those own lines; its founding year is not in the vault.[^1][^2] [unsourced] It is known for track-style garments.

## Making and Material
The London store range is mostly Nepenthes' own labels, Engineered Garments and Needles.[^1] Fabrics, mills and factories are not stated. The Nepenthes LA feed lists a Needles SS26 Track Series of track jackets and pants (a product drop, not a source of method).

## Patina and Care
No source in the vault.

## Today
Needles was already distributed to about 40 European stores when Nepenthes London opened at 8 Woburn Walk in 2019; the second overseas Nepenthes store after New York.[^1] Shimizu: "We do not intend to grow too large in scale."[^1]
