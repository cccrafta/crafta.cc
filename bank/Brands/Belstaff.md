## In One Line
British waxed-cotton motorcycle and outdoor clothing label; the vault holds one collector's mention and the London Brands research files it under cachet or no lineage evidence.

## Lineage
Founded in the 1920s in Staffordshire for motorcycle clothing [unsourced]. The only sourced fact is a dead-stock 1970s Belstaff parka and mitten set designed with the mountaineer Chris Bonington, named by the writer as a favourite piece [^1]. That points to a mountaineering line in the 1970s; the vault has no maker account of it.

## Making and Material
