## In One Line
American denim maker, third of the old "Big Three" with Levi's and Lee, known in the vault for the 1964 13MWZ broken twill and a recent selvedge collaboration. Dates conflict.

## Lineage
Ideas/Broken Twill says the Wrangler 13MWZ, from 1964, was the first commercial broken-twill denim.[^1] Ideas/Denim - From Serge de Nimes to the Rivet War describes a 1920s "Big Three" market split with Lee in the Midwest, Levi's in the West and Wrangler in the South and West, which conflicts with the 1964 data; Wrangler's own start date is not sourced in the vault.[^2] [unsourced]

## Making and Material
The broken twill reverses the twill direction to reduce leg twist in the finished jeans.[^1] A recent three-drop collaboration with imogene + willie used deadstock Cone Mills White Oak selvedge, then broken-twill denim from Mount Vernon Mills.[^3]
