## In One Line
Pacific Northwest wool maker that the vault knows through one garment: its CPO-style Heston shirt jacket, milled and made in the US. A material-led case.

## Lineage
Faherty's CPO history lists Pendleton, L.L. Bean and Eddie Bauer among the brands that moved the CPO silhouette into civilian outerwear.[^2] Founding date and mill history are not in the vault. [unsourced]

## Making and Material
The Pendleton Heston is 100% virgin wool at 23oz, milled and constructed in Oregon or Washington; Denimhunters compares it with Filson at 26oz and Dehen at 14oz.[^1] A Heddels note on Dehen's varsity jackets records Pendleton 24oz melton, though that article was not read in full.

## Today
Still sold as a heavyweight wool shirt jacket, per the Denimhunters comparison.[^1]

## Design
A civilian CPO shape in dense wool, close to the garment's military source. Judged from one description.[^1]
