## In One Line
Paris ready-to-wear label known for raw denim; in the vault only as the partner in Barbour's 2026 denim collection.

## Lineage
Founded in Paris in 1987 by Jean Touitou [unsourced]. The vault holds no lineage source.

## Making and Material
The Barbour x A.P.C. Trucker Denim Jacket (style MCA1168IN71) is 100% cotton by Barbour's description; no denim weight or mill is given [^1]. A.P.C. denim is made in Japan [unsourced].

## Patina and Care
No source in the vault. Raw denim from the label is sold to be worn unwashed [unsourced].

## Today
The collection is described by Barbour as combining "Barbour's wax with A.P.C.'s denim" and as the first large collection between the two brands, at about EUR 390 [^1].

## Design
Plain, minimal cuts without visible branding [unsourced]. The jacket borrows Barbour's wax-and-corduroy language more than A.P.C.'s own codes, but no source describes the pieces in detail [^1].
