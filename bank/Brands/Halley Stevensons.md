## Making and Material
The Canvas post says Tom Bonamici's Handy project in Portland makes roll-top bags, in runs rarely above fifty, from 22oz Scottish waxed twill by Halley Stevensons, stitched with mil-spec English cotton webbing and brass hardware (see In the Vault).
