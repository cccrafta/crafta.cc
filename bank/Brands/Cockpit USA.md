## In One Line
A US maker whose 2025 CPO shirt is cut in 20-22oz wool melton, close to the weight of the military original, which makes it the vault's reference for a CPO that keeps the cloth.

## Lineage
No source in the vault gives the founding or history. [unsourced] It is a US flight-jacket maker with roots in the 1970s. Heddels' varsity jacket history lists Cockpit USA ($790) among makers of that jacket [^2].

## Making and Material
The CPO shirt is 85/15 wool/nylon melton at 20-22oz, made in the USA, in navy and olive [^1]. It has two chest pockets, anchor buttons, a curved hem, a silk-lined yoke and collar, and a removable CPO anchor pin, at $325 [^1]. Heddels states that "few, if any, brands are making authentic CPO shirts in the USA" and calls it "a three-season beast" [^1]. The vault's CPO research finds its weight closer to the original range than Freenote or Schott [^3].

## Design
A faithful CPO: a wool shirt with the silhouette of a shirt and the cloth of a peacoat. The silk-lined yoke is the one detail Heddels notes beyond the standard pockets and hem [^1].
