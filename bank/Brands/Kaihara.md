## In One Line
A Hiroshima denim mill that blends TENCEL lyocell and recycled stretch into denim, documented in the vault almost entirely through its fibre suppliers' press.

## Lineage
No source gives the founding date. [unsourced] It is a long-established Hiroshima denim mill. Lenzing says TENCEL has been in Kaihara's collections since the 1990s [^1]. The Textile Innovations research says Kaihara is "widely reported to make about half of Japanese denim"; that rests on search summaries only and is indicative, not sourced.

## Making and Material
The REMOD collection (Kingpins Amsterdam, 14-15 October 2026) uses three TENCEL Lyocell fibres (Standard, HV100 with Variocut, dtex 2.2), woven at Kaihara's integrated mill in Hiroshima, in heavyweight jackets, wide-leg and stretch jeans and cotton-free shirting [^1]. These are vendor claims with no ratios, weights or wear data [^1]. The SAISEI collection (October 2024) combines Lenzing ECOVERO with REFIBRA and Roica EF; the body says "up to 20%" while the footnote says "a minimum of 20%", a conflict kept as found [^2][^3]. Roica EF carries a 30% lower CO2 claim from Asahi Kasei [^2]. The collection has three fabrications including a low-stretch selvedge [^3]. The vault found nothing supporting Kaihara as supplier of the Barbour x Levi's collection, so it is not stated here.
