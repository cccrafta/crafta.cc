## In One Line
Osaka denim maker, one of the "Osaka Five", also named by Heddels as a loopwheel sweatshirt specialist. The vault has category-level coverage, not a brand history.

## Lineage
Counted with Warehouse, Fullcount, Denime and Evisu as the Osaka Five, the group of 1990s reproduction denim makers.[^3] Its own founding date is not in the vault. [unsourced]

## Making and Material
Heddels lists Studio D'Artisan with Strike Gold, Merz b. Schwanen and Momotaro as makers of loopwheeled goods, and says loopwheeled items cost about twice as much as machine-knit equivalents.[^1] The slow circular loopwheel knits fabric with little tension, which is why Heddels calls it "the selvedge of knitwear".[^1] Denim weights and looms are not in the vault.

## Patina and Care
No source in the vault.

## Today
Heddels places Studio D'Artisan among end-level sweatshirt makers, with prices from about $175.[^2]

## Design
Close reproductions of mid-century American work and sport garments, with a preference for loopwheel construction. Judged from the Heddels category pieces only.[^1][^2]

## Depth
Two Heddels articles at category level plus the Osaka Five mentions in the research. No piece on the brand itself.
