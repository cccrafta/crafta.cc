## In One Line
A 1966 London menswear shop whose white voile frock for Mick Jagger became a cultural marker; the vault holds one profile, so the brand is known through that garment and not its workroom.

## Lineage
Michael Fish (1940 to 2016) opened the shop Mr Fish in 1966 with partner Barry Sainsbury, per The Vintage Showroom [^1]. It is known for kipper ties, kaftans and dresses for men [^1]. Garments were also worn by David Bowie [^1]. It is a different brand from Mister Freedom in Los Angeles and from Mr Freedom, the London shop.

## Making and Material
The Hyde Park frock for the Rolling Stones concert of 5 July 1969 was white cotton voile, bishop-sleeved and loosely cut to mid-thigh over white trousers, inspired by the fustanella, a Greek ceremonial kilt [^1]. The concert took place 48 hours after Brian Jones died and drew about 250,000 people [^1]. The voile choice is explored in the Crafta research on cotton voile. No tailor, workroom or construction detail is documented.
