## In One Line
A Turkish denim mill that showed a gold-rush-inspired workwear fabric line and a plant-based stretch yarn for FW26-27. The evidence is a single trade article.

## Lineage
No founding date or history is recorded in the vault. The mill is Turkish and appears among European and Turkish mills in the Working Craft research [^1].

## Making and Material
Sourcing Journal's FW26-27 mills piece describes a "Roots Age" concept inspired by the gold rush, with ultra-nappy textures, and a "Worktive" range of workwear-inspired durable pieces [^1]. It also describes Bio Form, a plant-based elastane replacing petroleum-derived stretch [^1]. These are the mill's presentation as relayed by a trade outlet. No weights, looms, dye method or capacity figures are recorded.

## Patina and Care
No source on how the cloth fades. "Ultra-nappy" suggests a raised surface; how it wears is not stated [inference].

## Today
The vault's Working Craft post groups Calik with Candiani, Bossa and Evlox as mills answering a return to heritage and practical denim [^1]. No customers or garments are named for the mill.

## Design
Concept-led cloth: the stories are gold rush and work. Without a swatch or garment in the vault, the design reading rests on the names of the lines, not on handling.
