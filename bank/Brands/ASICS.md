## In One Line
Japanese sports footwear maker. In the vault it appears as the partner of Kiko Kostadinov and as the origin of a 1970s split-toe marathon shoe that today's tabi sneakers cite.

## Lineage
Company founding and history are `[unsourced]`. The only lineage claim is that the Kostadinov LYASA FF Tabi is inspired by "the original ASICS Tabi marathon shoes developed in the 1970s" (Hypebeast, T3) [^1]. Kostadinov has designed ASICS footwear since SS2018, with the Novalis line announced in 2023 (Wikipedia, low reliability) [^3].

## Making and Material
The LYASA FF Tabi is a slip-on with an elastic upper and a rubber and EVA sole [^1]. Factories and materials beyond that are `[unsourced]`.

## Patina and Care
No source.

## Today
The LYASA FF Tabi was released on 11 April 2026 as the second instalment of a four-part collaboration [^1]. Who What Wear lists it in a 2026 tabi sneaker round-up (T3, low reliability) [^2].

## Design
The split toe on a technical shoe is the interesting detail. The idea note records that maker claims about balance and grip from the split toe are untested. Design score rests on the Kostadinov collaboration only.

## Depth
