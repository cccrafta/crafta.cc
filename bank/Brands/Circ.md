## In One Line
A Danville, Virginia recycler that separates polyester and cellulose from polycotton waste and sells the cellulose pulp for lyocell. It is a different company from Circulose.

## Lineage
No founding date or history is recorded. The maker's press release of 18 February 2026 identifies it as based in Danville, Virginia [^1].

## Making and Material
Circ separates polyester and cellulose from polycotton; the cellulose pulp goes to Lenzing, which makes TENCEL | Circ lyocell with REFIBRA, containing 30% Circ pulp from recycled polycotton waste, and Linz Textil spins the yarn [^1]. The first industrial-scale facility is described as underway in France and is not yet operating [^1]. Textile Focus lists Circ with Ambercycle, Circulose and Syre as developers and says scale needs investment, feedstock, demand and policy [^2].

## Patina and Care
No source. The output is a fibre input; no garment ageing data exists.

## Today
The Fiber Club pools demand across brands to overcome minimum-order and pricing barriers. The second cohort, announced in February 2026, is Madewell, Reformation and C&A, with Lenzing and Linz Textil as suppliers; the first cohort of January 2025 was Bestseller, Eileen Fisher, Everlane and Zalando, with Arvind, Foshan Chicley and one Indian cellulose maker as supply partners [^1]. The Indian partner is out of scope and is not developed here.
