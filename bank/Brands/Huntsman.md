## In One Line
A Savile Row bespoke house built around shooting and field wear, with structured, military-influenced cutting and a documented safari-suit link to *Mogambo* (1953). For Crafta it is the tailoring house closest to the safari jacket and to inherited-garment repair.

## Lineage
The house says it has been bespoke tailors since 1849.[^2] It made safari suits for Clark Gable, Ava Gardner and Grace Kelly for *Mogambo* in 1953; the source is Huntsman's own page, so it is maker-attested.[^1] Creative Director Campbell Carey designs the current collection from the heritage.[^1]

## Making and Material
Bespoke garments are handmade at 11 Savile Row, with at least three fittings and commissions from GBP 7,500 for a two-piece suit; the cutter's name stays inside the jacket and clients meet the makers.[^2] Huntsman says it is the only Savile Row tailor with its own handloom and the only house where clients can design a signature tweed, sampled in-house; both are maker claims.[^2] Permanent Style places the cut as more padded than Henry Poole's.[^3] The ready-to-wear Seagreen Linen Herringbone Safari Jacket uses softer construction: thinner shoulder pads, lighter canvassing, soft sleeve head.[^1] Cloth weights are not given in the vault.

## Patina and Care
Huntsman offers an Inherited Garment Service for altering inherited Huntsman pieces; it is not offered for other makers' garments, and the terms rest on a search summary [single-source].[^2] No source describes repair beyond alteration.

## Today
Current offerings include herringbone and navy seersucker safari jackets.[^1] Specialities listed are shooting and field-sports wear and morning wear.[^2] A Savile Row house with bespoke pricing, so it is a study object rather than a wardrobe option.

## Design
Structured, military-influenced silhouettes, held to the heritage rather than softened the way many contemporary safari jackets are (the vault's Huntsman publication note says so). Scored on taste: construction-led tailoring with a field-wear brief, which fits the owner's valued tailoring houses.
