## In One Line
One of the Osaka Five, the 1990s Japanese brands that reproduced American workwear and denim. The vault's idea note says each of the five took a different approach and Fullcount "went technical".

## Lineage
Research/Working Craft lists Fullcount among the Osaka Five (Warehouse, Fullcount, Denime, Evisu, Studio D'Artisan) who began reproducing American workwear in the 1990s. Founding year and founders are `[unsourced]`.
