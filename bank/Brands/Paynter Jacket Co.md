## In One Line
A jacket brand designed in East London and made in Guimaraes, Portugal, in limited batches; it matters to Crafta for its plainly stated production and a recut French chore coat, and not for a London workshop.

## Lineage
The maker says the jackets are "designed by us in East London" and made in a small family-run factory in northern Portugal belonging to Sergio's family [^1]. Founders Huw Thomas and Becky Okell met in 2018 at a London workshop; no founding date is given [^1]. Essential Journal describes Batch No.1 as a nod to the classic French chore coat, recut as a tailored shape instead of the "boxy, baggy" vintage originals [^2]. It is not made in London, which matters because the brand sits near the London label.

## Making and Material
The cloth comes from Berto, a mill near Venice making cloth since the 1850s, chosen after the founders were unhappy with the factory's usual suppliers [^1]. Essential Journal says the reverse bull denim was made bespoke for the brand, that jackets are assembled by hand in a mountainside factory, and that corozo buttons are handmade in the UK from Ecuadorian nuts [^2]. A garment dyer about 20 km from the factory dyes to Pantone references, where the first jackets used natural indigo [^1].

## Today
Batches are limited. The maker says "just four times a year"; Essential Journal in 2019 reported three times a year and 300 pieces per batch, so the batch count conflicts and is kept as stated [^1] [^2]. Batch No.1 sold out in 14 minutes [^2].

## Design
