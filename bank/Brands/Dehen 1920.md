## In One Line
A Portland, Oregon maker of varsity jackets and CPO shirt jackets in UK-milled melton. In the vault it marks the light end of the melton CPO range.

## Lineage
The name carries 1920 and the maker is in Portland, Oregon; no founding history is recorded in the vault [unsourced].

## Making and Material
The Denimhunters CPO comparison lists the Dehen 1920 Crissman as 14 oz melton wool with a 100% cotton accent liner, milled in the UK and made in the USA [^1]. The CPO research note calls it the lightest melton interpretation that still uses melton, against Filson at 26 oz and Pendleton at 23 oz [^1]. The cache lists an unread Heddels piece on Dehen team varsity jackets with Pendleton 24 oz melton and bison sleeves; that stays a snippet.

## Patina and Care
No source on how the melton or the cotton liner age.

## Today
Heddels' varsity history prices the maker's varsity jackets at $625-$645, against $790 for Cockpit USA and $505-$525 for Falcon Garments [^2]. Those figures place Dehen mid-range among American makers.

## Design
A restrained CPO: the shirt silhouette in wool with a plain cotton liner, at a weight meant to be worn as a shirt rather than as a coat [^1]. The designer's choice is the weight, not an added detail.

## Depth
