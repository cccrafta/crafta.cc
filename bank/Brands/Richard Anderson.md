## In One Line
A Savile Row bespoke tailor known in the vault through one Permanent Style construction analysis of its tuxedo. Founding date and history are not in the vault.

## Lineage
[unsourced] A Savile Row house; the vault gives no founding date or founder. The Tuxedo research note lists it among Savile Row tailors as understated with a narrow shoulder.[^1]

## Making and Material
Permanent Style's analysis of the tuxedo records a body canvas running the length of the jacket, horsehair at the top and domette over it, with two darts on either side for waist shape.[^1] The shoulder is narrow, finishing before the outer edge of the deltoid; the grosgrain facing is attached by hand, and the buttonholes are slim, neat and handmade.[^1] Cloth, weight and mill are not recorded.
