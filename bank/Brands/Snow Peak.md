## In One Line
Japanese camping maker from Niigata whose Takibi line puts flame-retardant fabric into workwear-shaped jackets for use beside a fire. The vault holds the maker's own account of it.

## Lineage
Snow Peak is a camping-equipment company founded in 1958 in Sanjo, Niigata, by Yukio Yamai. [unsourced] Its apparel began in 2015, and the Takibi (campfire) line was set in 2016.[^1]

## Making and Material
The Takibi cloth blends flame-retardant aramid and Kanecaron modacrylic with cotton and linen, developed with Teijin and tested in Niigata.[^1] The maker says the styles are "inspired by classic workwear", and that the flame resistance lasts through washing.[^1] The ripstop jacket's removable liner is not flame-retardant, a limit the maker's own page states.[^1]
