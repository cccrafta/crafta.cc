## In One Line
Japanese reproduction maker whose military and workwear pieces are built to the original specification and sewn in-house; the vault holds its own product page and five trusted mentions, which is why it is one of the better-documented Japanese makers here.

## Lineage
The Coat Man's Combat Tropical page presents a reproduction of a US Army tropical jacket and says it follows the early models, which is the maker's own claim.[^1] The CPO research records a Real McCoy's CPO in 100% melton wool, with a curved-wing pocket flap, anchor buttons and a Naval Clothing Factory label, made in Japan.[^2] The Crafta CPO post places it at the same standard of fidelity as Buzz Rickson's.[^7] No founding date or founder appears in the vault. [unsourced] The company is based in Japan and works from vintage garments.

## Making and Material
The Combat Tropical (MJ22006) is cut in poplin with urea buttons and is sewn in-house in Japan; the page gives no mill.[^1] The CPO is 100% melton, the same dense compressed wool a peacoat uses, not a wool-blend shirting.[^2] Heddels lists the maker among the end-tier loopwheel sweatshirt makers and quotes a 10 oz parka at USD 255.[^3][^4] That Heddels list is a ranking of price and fidelity, not a record of the maker's process.

## Patina and Care
No source in the vault. [unsourced] Melton wool and poplin cotton soften at fold lines and elbows with wear.

## Today
Put This On lists The Real McCoy's in the heritage tier of henleys, best paired with leather jackets and field coats.[^6] Permanent Style's Clutch Cafe guide names it among faithful-recreation brands alongside Buzz Rickson's.[^5] The Denimhunters CPO guide lists the 100% melton shirt as current.[^2] Pieces are sold at the high end of the price range for each category.

## Design
The design logic is the original garment's: a curved flap, a label, a specified weight, copied from a documented model. The maker's reasoning in its own words was not found; the Combat Tropical note says the same of Buzz Rickson's, RRL, Kapital, visvim and Fullcount.[^1] Design is a taste score: reproduction with construction fidelity is the owner's profile.

## Depth
Six sources cover the maker: a product page, a Denimhunters guide, two Heddels lists, and two list mentions at Permanent Style and Put This On. A post would need the maker's account of its archive and fabric sources.
