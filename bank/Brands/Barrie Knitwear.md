## In One Line
Scottish Borders cashmere knitter, owned by Chanel since 2012; the vault's example of a craft maker absorbed into a house.

## Lineage
Barrie Knitwear was founded in 1903 in the Scottish Borders and is one of three key Hawick-area survivors, with William Lockie (1874) and Johnstons of Elgin (1797) [^1]. Chanel acquired it in 2012 through its Métiers d'Art initiative [^1].

## Making and Material
Cashmere knitwear [unsourced]. The vault holds the Hawick context: Joseph Dawson's de-hairing machine made Hawick the centre of global cashmere knitwear, and mills such as Caerlee (Ballantyne) have since closed [^1]. No source describes Barrie's own gauge, yarn spinner or production site.
