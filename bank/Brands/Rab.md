## Making and Material
Trek & Mountain, 25 March 2024, lists Rab among the brands with ePE products in the UK; ePE is GORE-TEX's expanded polyethylene membrane without a fluoropolymer [^1]. The field test in that article was on a Mountain Equipment jacket and not on a Rab one, so the article says nothing about a Rab garment's making or performance [^1]. It also notes DWR contamination and breathability caveats for the new membrane [^1]. A GORE-TEX 2026 line page in the vault is a stub with a failed fetch and is not relied on.
