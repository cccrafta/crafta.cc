## In One Line
Ralph Lauren's archival workwear line; the vault records it in lists and one Heddels-sourced product item, with no maker account of how it is made.

## Lineage
No source in the vault gives its founding. [unsourced] A sub-brand of Ralph Lauren, started in the early 1990s. The April 2026 weekly briefing notes the Lot 271 Trucker in Worn In Black as sanforized black denim modelled on vintage Lee Riders, with RRL "leaning harder into archival silhouettes".[^2]

## Making and Material
The Lot 271 Trucker is sanforized black denim.[^2] Put This On lists RRL among indigo-dyed henley options, with Orslow, Pure Blue Japan and Kapital.[^1] No factories or mills are named; Barbour's denim jungle jacket research found no denim jungle jacket from RRL.[^4]

## Patina and Care
No source in the vault. [unsourced] Indigo and denim garments fade with wear; no repair offer is documented.

## Today
Permanent Style's overshirt guide, as recorded in the CPO research, recommends Filson and RRL; that note says Permanent Style was paywalled and not fully read.[^3] The April briefing flags the Lot 271 as a current product.[^2]

## Design
The documented design logic is reproduction of a named Lee model and its cut, which is the right instinct. Design is a taste score: the main house's cachet weighs on it and the line is only partly documented.

## Depth
Three passing mentions across vault sources. A post would need a maker's account of the archive, the mills and the factories.
