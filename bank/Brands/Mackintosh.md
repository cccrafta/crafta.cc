## In One Line
British raincoat maker that appears in the vault only through other brands: Margaret Howell names its part hand-made raincoats as a tradition she works with, and Kiko Kostadinov directed its Mackintosh 0001 line. Scored mostly from general knowledge; treat as provisional.

## Lineage
Margaret Howell cites "Mackintosh's part hand-made raincoats" among the British traditions she works with.[^1] The brand descends from the rubberised-cloth raincoat that gave the garment its name. [unsourced]

## Making and Material
The vault has no factory, cloth or construction detail. The coat is made from bonded or rubberised cotton with taped or bonded seams. [unsourced]

## Today
Kiko Kostadinov was creative director of Mackintosh 0001 from November 2017 and is no longer in the role.[^2]
