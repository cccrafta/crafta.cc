## Making and Material
The Trilobal Yarn idea note says Stone Island has used trilobal nylon since 2005, with a "Tinto Capo" process that dyes the finished garment, not the yarn, so the colour settles into the structure and shifts as the fabric moves (research claim, no source note).[^1] The Nylon Metal debut is dated 2005 in that note.[^1] The 25 April 2026 weekly briefing reports a Nylon Metal-TC documentary and a version using ECONYL recycled nylon, with Japanese coverage presenting it as material-science storytelling.[^2]
