## In One Line
A Paris-founded French workwear maker, now made in Portugal. The vault places it in the revival of vintage French moleskin workwear, with one Heddels mention and two research-note lines.

## Lineage
The Bleu de Travail idea note lists Vetra as established in 1927 in Paris, now made in Portugal; this is the vault's own note, not a source note.[^2] Heddels names Vetra among the brands in the modern revival of vintage French workwear, with Le Laboureur and Le Mont St. Michel.[^1]

## Making and Material
Moleskin is a fustian, tightly woven in a satin weave with warp and weft the same colour, and one side shorn and brushed in a process called peaching.[^1] The Moleskin research note lists Vetra for workwear jackets in moleskin.[^2] The note that production is now in Portugal is the idea note's wording; no maker source states it.[^2] Weights and mills are not in the vault.

## Patina and Care
No source in the vault. Heddels describes moleskin as warm, durable and wind-resistant for farmers and hunters, which says nothing on how a given jacket ages.[^1]

## Today
Heddels counts Vetra among the brands behind the modern revival of French workwear.[^1] [unsourced] Nothing on its current range or stockists.

## Design
Workwear jackets in moleskin, in the French bleu de travail tradition the idea note describes.[^2] Scored on taste: plain French workwear, material-led, a good fit.
