## In One Line
An Austrian fibre producer whose lyocell and recycled-content fibres are woven into Japanese denim by Kaihara; the vault holds the maker's releases and one trade report, but no independent verdict from denim specialists.

## Lineage
Lenzing is an Austrian cellulosic fibre producer [unsourced]; no founding date is documented in the vault. Its ties to Japanese denim are recorded instead: TENCEL has appeared in Kaihara collections since the 1990s, according to a trade channel that repeats vendor statements [^2].

## Making and Material
The SAISEI collection, announced 16 October 2024 for Kingpins Amsterdam on 23-24 October, is Kaihara denim woven with LENZING ECOVERO fibres with REFIBRA technology and ROICA EF recycled stretch yarn, in three fabrications including a low-stretch selvedge [^1]. The release footnote says ECOVERO with REFIBRA contains "a minimum of 20% recycled material" from pre- and post-consumer cotton waste under the Recycled Claim Standard, while its body text says "up to 20% of post-consumer textile waste"; these conflict and are kept as stated [^1]. Just Style restates the "up to 20% post-consumer" wording [^3]. The REMOD collection, for Kingpins on 14-15 October 2026, uses three TENCEL Lyocell fibres (Standard, HV100, dtex 2.2) woven at Kaihara's integrated Hiroshima mill; HV100 Variocut gives a matte, cotton-like surface [^2]. These are vendor claims with no blend ratios or wear data. Lenzing is also a supplier for TENCEL | Circ, with 30% Circ pulp from recycled polycotton [^4].
