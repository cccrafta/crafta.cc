## In One Line
A Finnish fibre developer that spins cellulose pulp without dissolving it; the vault holds a single press-release report, so its claims are unverified.

## Lineage
Spinnova works from Jyväskylä [^1]. It trialled Circulose pulp in 2023 [^1]. No founding date or earlier history is in the vault, and the company is young compared with the mills in the Crafta library.

## Making and Material
Per the 3 March 2026 release summarised by Textile World, Spinnova uses a mechanical fibre process that needs no harmful chemicals and no dissolving during spinning [^1]. Circulose pulp can be used at 100%, and the CEO says the pulp becomes cotton-like fibres, not viscose [^1]. The report gives no capacity, volume, terms or timeline, and the vault holds only this one source. Spinnova capacity figures in [[Research/Textile Innovations 2026]] are unsourced.
