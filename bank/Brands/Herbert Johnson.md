## In One Line
London hatmaker founded 1889 and owned by Swaine Adeney Brigg since 1996. Where its hats are made is the open question.

## Lineage
Founded in 1889 as a hatmaker; a royal warrant from Edward VII followed in 1901; Swaine Adeney Brigg acquired it in 1996 [^1].

## Making and Material
Swaine London's page says "Made in the UK" and describes a workshop and atelier on New Bond Street with servicing and restoration offered, but it does not say where each product is made [^1]. Research/London Brands says Herbert Johnson is "not London-made", with civilian hats from the Midlands. The vault ran a search on where the hats are made (cache 2026-11-06 London Brands B) with no firm answer.

## Patina and Care
Servicing and restoration are offered [^1].

## Today
Sold as part of the Swaine London group [^1].

## Design
Not seen; hats judged only by description.
