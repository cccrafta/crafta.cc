## In One Line
Paris tailor known for a soft, roped shoulder and the 6x1 double-breasted jacket; the vault holds one cashmere review and tuxedo research citing The Rake and Permanent Style.

## Lineage
The vault's Tuxedo research credits the roped shoulder to the founder's grandfather and records the 6x1 double-breasted cut, from The Rake and Permanent Style, in its footnote 20 [^2]. The post The Tuxedo names Lorenzo Cifonelli as the current head. The 1880 founding year is [unsourced].

## Making and Material
Permanent Style's Simon Crompton wore a Cifonelli cashmere overcoat for nine years [^1]. No source in the vault describes the shoulder construction, canvas or workroom beyond the Tuxedo research line.

## Patina and Care
After nine years the Cifonelli cashmere overcoat showed no balding but remained "frustratingly soft" and rarely held a clean line [^1]. This is one wearer's report on one coat and does not generalise to the house's tailoring.

## Today
The house continues bespoke work in Paris and has a Paris-Tokyo-based following [unsourced]. The vault records its influence on tailoring through press mentions only.

## Design
A soft shoulder with a roped sleeve head and a long, flowing double-breasted front [unsourced for detail; roped shoulder per research note [^2]]. The style is a clear point of view among European tailors, which supports a taste score of 4.

## Depth
One extended wear report and two research mentions. A fuller note would need the house's own history, a workroom account and a clear explanation of the 6x1 cut.
