## In One Line
A Paris-founded maker of the Breton top, one of the three principal French manufacturers with Saint James and Armor Lux. The maker's own history page is the main source, and it no longer loads.

## Lineage
Orcival was founded in Paris in 1939, one year after Armor Lux began operations.[^1][^2] Its knitting factory is now in Lyon.[^1] The French Navy introduced the Breton shirt in 1858, with 21 white stripes of 20 mm and 21 blue stripes of 10 mm on the torso, so Orcival is a later maker of an older naval pattern, not its originator.[^2]

## Today
In 2017 MoMA chose the Orcival Breton top to represent the genuine Breton top in its "Is Fashion Modern?" exhibition; this is from search results, not the museum's own page.[^1] [unsourced] The vault does not say whether production remains in Lyon.

## Design
The garment is the striped knit top with a boat neck, whose naval specification is documented.[^2] Scored on taste: a plain, utilitarian pattern with real naval lineage, but no vault note describes Orcival's own cut or fabric.
