## In One Line
An Italian luxury house known for cashmere and fine wool cloth. The vault mentions it only in the cashmere research, so this score is mostly general knowledge.

## Lineage
[unsourced] A family textile firm from northern Italy that moved from wool trading into fine cloth and later luxury garments; now part of a luxury group. No vault source gives dates or ownership.

## Making and Material
The Cashmere research note says Loro Piana uses longer, stable cashmere to reduce pilling (see In the Vault); the note attributes this generally and no source note holds the claim. [unsourced] The house is known for vertical control of fibre sourcing, spinning and weaving.

## Patina and Care
The Cashmere note explains that cashmere pills at first and then settles, and that a longer, stable fibre pills less; it does not give Loro Piana's own care or repair offer. [unsourced] No repair service known.

## Today
[unsourced] Sold at luxury price points through its own boutiques. The cashmere note names it next to Brunello Cucinelli as a quality reference.

## Design
[unsourced] Quiet, logo-free luxury knitwear and outerwear. Scored low-middle on taste: the owner values construction over cachet, and this house sells largely on cachet.

## Depth
The cashmere research note holds a fibre-level comparison that could grow into a material note on staple length and pilling. Nothing on the house's own archive.
