## In One Line
British waxing house behind much UK waxed cotton, including Barbour's. The vault has it only through the Canvas research note.

## Lineage
Founding and history are `[unsourced]`. Research/Canvas calls it "the British equivalent" of Martexin (a beeswax blend since 1838), "used by Barbour and many bag makers", and "the waxing house behind Barbour and most UK waxed cotton".

## Making and Material
Wax is applied after weaving: paraffin, Martexin or British Millerain treatment. Waxing fills the spaces between yarns for water resistance while air and vapour pass through (Research/Canvas). Bleu de Chauffe uses 18oz wax canvas from British Millerain. The research footnotes for these claims point at sources not yet resolved in the vault, so treat them as unverified.

## Patina and Care
Waxed cloth can be re-waxed and repaired as it ages; that property belongs to the finish, and the vault has no British Millerain-specific care claim `[unsourced]`.

## Today
Supplies Barbour and bag makers per Research/Canvas. London Brands found no confirmed London user.

Not applicable to a cloth supplier in the usual sense; the cloth itself is a proven functional waxed cotton, judged only on reputation within the research note.
