## In One Line
East London label (2010) that says plainly most of its clothes are made abroad and keeps a small Made in England capsule. The London research files it under "story louder than lineage".

## Lineage
Founded in East London in 2010, with the first store in Soho.[^1] No earlier production history is documented.

## Making and Material
Percival states "our garments are made across the world with skilled craftspeople we know and trust".[^1] Cotton is organic from Portugal, blends recycled from Italy, and specialist wool from the UK.[^1] The Made in England capsule has outerwear cut and sewn at a 150-year-old Manchester factory, 6-ply lambswool knitwear from Leicestershire and military Derby shoes from Northamptonshire, in runs of 100 or fewer; the Manchester factory is not named.[^1]

Trading in London, with the capsule as the England-made part of the range.[^1]
