## In One Line
Hawick knitwear maker, established 1874 and still manufacturing in the town. The vault has one heritage source and one post line on the cashmere it buys.

## Lineage
William Lockie was established in Hawick in 1874 and is named as a survivor, with Johnstons of Elgin and Barrie, of the Scottish cashmere trade.[^1][^2]

## Making and Material
The Hawick heritage page says Lockie still manufactures in the town.[^1] The post Cashmere - What One Goat Produces in a Year says it pays $135/kg for Inner Mongolian cashmere bought at auction in Canton; that claim comes from a Put This On article not read in full, so it is single-source and second-hand.[^3]
