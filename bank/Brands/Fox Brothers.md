## In One Line
Somerset flannel and worsted mill; the vault holds a single-source claim that Margaret Howell has worked with it for about 40 years and nothing confirmed beyond that.

## Lineage
Founded in Wellington, Somerset, in 1772 and known for flannel [unsourced]. The only vault statement is the Margaret Howell interview, where the designer says her house has worked with Fox Brothers for about 40 years and calls it "the most important supplier" [^1].

## Making and Material
Flannel, worsted and woollen cloths for tailoring are the mill's trade [unsourced]. No source in the vault gives weights, fibres or finishing methods.

## Patina and Care
No source in the vault.

## Today
The mill still supplies tailors and designers [unsourced]. The same interview adds that the Howell workroom makes 4,000 shirts a year, a fact about Howell and not about Fox [^1]. The London Brands research records "No confirmed London user of ... Fox Brothers", meaning no corroboration was found for the claim.

## Design
