## In One Line
Newcastle designer who treats a 4,000-garment archive of military, expedition, workwear and sportswear as primary sources and rebuilds them; not a London brand, but the clearest case in the vault of one maker holding workwear and sport lines together.

## Lineage
Born in 1949 in Peterlee, County Durham, Cabourn studied art and industrial design in Newcastle from 1967 and started the menswear label Cricket in 1969. Cricket was liquidated in 1984 and relaunched under his own name.[^1] A trade fair in Paris in 1979 produced a GBP 250,000 order from the Japanese wholesaler Sam Sugure, which began a long Japan relationship; the Army Gym flagship opened in Tokyo in 2009, and at the peak the company had 26 stores, 16 of them in Japan.[^1] Carryology's article, dated 19 June 2026, reports his death; the vault's research notes carry an earlier, unconfirmed date of 11 June 2026, so the date is in conflict until a second source confirms it.[^1]

## Making and Material
The Jesmond (Newcastle) studio archive holds more than 4,000 garments and about 3,000 books; a 1943 flight suit was rebuilt in Harris Tweed.[^1] Ventile and Harris Tweed recur, and the signature Everest Parka was listed at GBP 3,100.[^1] Lybro is the workwear line, from British industrial clothing; Army Gym draws on vintage sportswear and military training kit.[^1] Production sites are not named in the vault. Marubeni has held the Japanese licence since January 2025.[^1]

## Today
The Cabourn Utility Jacket is "based on the Jungle Fatigue" with four gusseted pockets, tailored lapels and Ventile.[^2] A Cabourn x Lybro CPO runs in heavy wool with elbow patches, bartacking and custom metal buttons at USD 280.[^3] A Lee x Cabourn "OX'ed Silver" launched in the US on 8 October 2026.[^4] Collaborators include Alpha Industries, Converse, Filson, Fred Perry, Karrimor and Red Wing.[^1]

## Design
The method is concrete: take a primary garment, keep its function, rebuild it in a cloth the original lacked. The CPO adds bartacks at stress points; the jungle jacket gets a Ventile body. Design is a taste score at the top of the range because the work sits on every thread the owner follows.

## Depth
A named archive, two lines that pair workwear and sport, and a thirty-year Japan story. Research notes already treat the Utility Jacket against Real McCoy's and Engineered Garments.
