## In One Line
A London hatter that acquired Christys' in 2026, with a stated plan to bring production back to London, and the vault's case of a heritage hat name changing hands.

## Lineage
Christys' set up its hat business at Whitehart Court, London, on 1 March 1773 [^1]. The Christys took over the Stockport felt works in 1824, and Bermondsey became one of the largest bowler makers [^1]. In the 1990s Stockport closed and operations moved to Witney, and in 2011 Liberty acquired Christy & Co and Compton Webb [^1]. The 2026 Christys' page says Laird Hatters acquired Christys', "bringing production back to London", without detail [^1]. FashionUnited (March 2026) reports Christy & Co entering voluntary liquidation after 253 years, with Laird Hatters buying Christys' and owner Alex Torun-Shaw saying the firm was already insolvent when approached in October 2025 [^2]. That article gives no date in its text and does not mention Witney. The liquidation date is single-source. Laird's own history is not in the vault.
