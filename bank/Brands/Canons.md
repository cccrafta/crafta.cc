## In One Line
A bespoke shoemaker in Islington that inherited the Foster & Son business, rebuilt it around a small workshop and a nine-month delivery time, and is Permanent Style's top recommendation among bespoke shoemakers.

## Lineage
Foster & Son made shoes in London's West End from 1840; the Jermyn St lease ended in 2020 [^1]. A 2018 Northampton factory backed by Japanese investors failed because shoes were priced at £800 when they should have been £2,000+ [^1]. In 2022 managing director Simon Bolzoni bought the company with his own money and renamed it Canons, launched in 2023 [^1]. Bolzoni trained under Terry Moore, the last Foster maker [^1]. Permanent Style now calls Foster & Son the "old business", its name used for accessories and Northampton ready-made shoes [^2].

## Making and Material
The workshop is at 6 Northampton Street, Islington, with about 20 staff plus outworkers [^1]. Output is twice the Foster Jermyn St level, and delivery has been cut from about 18 months to 9 [^1]. Bespoke starts at £4,750 + VAT [^1]. The house colours its own leathers and develops suede colours with tanneries, and brought despatch-box production in-house in 2018 [^1].
