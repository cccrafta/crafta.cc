## In One Line
A Japanese workwear maker that Heddels uses for heavy waffle henleys and places in the end tier of sweatshirts; the vault holds no maker page, so founding and factory are undocumented.

## Lineage
No founding date, founder or factory is documented in the vault. Heddels groups UES among the Japanese makers that lead quality in waffle knits [^1].

## Making and Material
The UES Big Waffle henley is a 13 oz Japanese waffle weave that Heddels presents as a garment for all seasons [^1]. In the end tier of sweatshirts, from $175 up, the guide places UES with other specialist makers [^2]. Two Crafta posts cite UES shoulder bags made from vintage machine-woven duck and over-dyed after construction; this rests on the post text and is thin [unsourced].

## Patina and Care
Over-dyed duck bags are said to pick up uneven colour, as recorded in the Canvas post [unsourced]. No source covers how the waffle knit ages.

## Today
UES appears in current Heddels guides as a reference maker for waffle and sweat tiers [^1] [^2]. Its current line, prices and factory are not documented.

## Design
A heavy waffle grid and plain henley placket, described as a single garment type; no other piece is examined [^1].

## Depth
