## In One Line
Sneaker brand in the vault through its 2021 Maison Margiela Tabi collaboration. Evidence is one partner article, so this note records the brand as a platform for someone else's design.

## Lineage
The Classic Leather was introduced in 1983.[^1] Company founding and origins are not in the vault. [unsourced]

## Making and Material
The Classic Leather Tabi Bianchetto uses black nappa leather hand-painted white.[^1] No factory, tannery or construction information for Reebok's own range is in the vault.

## Patina and Care
The i-D partner piece says the white coating "intentionally chips off over time".[^1] That behaviour belongs to the Margiela finish, not to Reebok's ordinary leather, and rests on partner content.

## Today
Still collaborates with luxury houses. The earlier Tabi Instapump Fury was first seen on the Artisanal SS20 runway.[^1]

## Design
The vault records Reebok's silhouette as the host for a Margiela split-toe, not an original design statement.[^1]

## Depth
