## In One Line
Japanese sportswear company, licensee of The North Face in Japan and Spiber's development partner. In the vault it matters as the bridge between technical outerwear and everyday clothes (nanamica's founder came from Goldwin).

## Lineage
Founding year and early history are `[unsourced]`. Goldwin is the licensee of The North Face in Japan per its Publication note. Joint development with Spiber began in 2015 [^1].

## Making and Material
Goldwin and Spiber launched Fall/Winter 2023 products with Brewed Protein fibre across five brands: The North Face, Goldwin, nanamica, The North Face Purple Label and Woolrich [^1]. The North Face Nuptse listing: outer 60% Brewed Protein fibre, 25% wool, 15% nylon, with a PTFE waterproof and breathable laminate on the reverse layer [^1]. Polymer mass production at Spiber's Thailand plant began in early 2022 [^1].

## Today
The first Brewed Protein product was The North Face's Planetary Equilibrium Tee (August 2019), earlier items sold in limited lottery runs; Goldwin's stated target is shifting 10% of newly developed products to Brewed Protein by 2030 [^1]. nanamica's founder Eiichiro Homma is a former Goldwin technical-outerwear designer [^3].

## Design
Technical outerwear and licensed lines; the vault has no direct design verdict. The nanamica connection suggests performance-wear logic carried into daily clothes [^3].
