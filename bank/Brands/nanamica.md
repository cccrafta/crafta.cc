## In One Line
A Tokyo label that carries technical-outerwear logic into everyday clothing. Founder Eiichiro Homma worked at Goldwin for more than 18 years, and the brand sits closer to sport than to workwear.

## Lineage
Homma says he spent more than 18 years at Goldwin designing technical outerwear.[^1] Search results (secondary) put the founding at 2003 with Takashi Imaki, the same year Goldwin and Homma launched The North Face Purple Label.[^1] The interview does not mention workwear or military references; the lineage is performance wear, not workwear.[^1]

## Making and Material
Homma's stated method is to "transform modern technology into real life garments", pairing natural with technical and soft with durable, with cotton GORE-TEX as his example.[^1] For Fall/Winter 2023 nanamica was one of five Goldwin-group brands to ship products with Brewed Protein fibre, developed with Spiber since 2015; mass production of the polymer began at Spiber's Thailand plant in early 2022.[^2] Spiber's release lists nanamica among four labels in a 17-product collection on 29 September 2023.[^3] Where the clothes are sewn is not stated in the vault.

## Today
Active in the Brewed Protein programme, which Goldwin ties to a goal of moving 10% of newly developed products to the fibre by 2030.[^2] An interview dated 7 July 2026 shows Homma still describes the brand as "a hybrid of fashion and function".[^1]

## Design
Homma: "When talking about style, we don't mean decoration", and "the main character must always be the human being".[^1] Scored on taste: function-first Japanese outdoor-derived clothing with no decoration, a close match for the owner's function-across-work-and-sport thread.
