## In One Line
Los Angeles maker of period-tailored workwear and military pieces, in the vault for its FW2024 "Submariner" collection with Sugar Cane. Not the same brand as Mr Freedom, the London shop of Tommy Roberts (1969).

## Lineage
Founding date and founder are not in the vault; Christophe Loiron is the usual name given for the brand. [unsourced] The vault's evidence is one 2024 collection page, cited in the CPO shirt research.[^1]

## Making and Material
The FW2024 "Submariner" collection produced a CPO shirt, the "Bubblehead", in Sugar Cane's Okinawa fibre denim: 50% cotton, 50% sugarcane fibre, 11oz, with US Navy fouled-anchor plastic buttons, a scalloped cuff pattern, chin strap and concealed chest pocket.[^1] The research note reads this as a material departure from melton wool that keeps the 1930s-40s tailoring.[^1] Where it sews is not in the vault.

No source in the vault.

## Today
Active in 2024 with Japanese mill and brand partners.[^1] A Heddels piece on a Mister Freedom "Watertown" shirt blending French sportswear and workwear was found but only an excerpt was read, so nothing is drawn from it.

## Design
Exact period detail applied to a new cloth, which is the move the owner rates in the Japanese reproduction makers. Judged from one garment.[^1]
