## In One Line
Italian denim and fashion brand owned by OTB. The vault knows it through business news of Glenn Martens leaving as creative director, not through its garments.

## Lineage
Founder is Renzo Rosso, OTB chairman, per the trade press [^1]. Founding date is `[unsourced]`. Glenn Martens joined in October 2020 after the 2018 Red Tag capsule and left after the SS27 Milan show on 22 September 2026, announced 18 September; he stays at Margiela [^1][^2]. Working Craft notes 1980s designer brands (Diesel, Valentino) produced high-end jeans, which marks the point denim entered luxury.

## Making and Material
Production, factories and mills are `[unsourced]`. The trade press describes business results, not making.

## Patina and Care

## Today
Rosso said Martens "transformed the perception of the brand" [^1]. No successor named [^1]. Andrea Rigogliosi became CEO in January 2026, and OTB reported 2025 revenue of EUR 1.7bn with Diesel at "the highest profitability level of the last 10 years" [^4][^5]. The Fashionista note is a stub (HTTP 403), so it is not cited for claims [^3].

## Design
Under Martens the brand sat in the runway-led, trend-aware denim lane; the owner's taste prefers construction-led pieces, so score is low.

## Depth
The Martens exit is a business story; the denim-into-luxury thread in Working Craft is the more useful part.
