## In One Line
Osaka denim brand and one of the group the vault calls the Osaka Five; covered only as a name in research on 1990s Japanese reproduction denim.

## Lineage
The vault's Working Craft research names Denime among the Osaka Five (Warehouse, Fullcount, Denime, Evisu, Studio D'Artisan), brands that reproduced American workwear in the 1990s. The vault's Idea on the Osaka Five treats the group as the start of the Japanese denim scene. The 1988 founding year is [unsourced].

## Making and Material
Reproduction of 1940s-60s American jeans with Japanese selvedge cloth is the group's general approach [unsourced for Denime in particular]. The vault holds no source on Denime's mill, weight or dye.
