## In One Line
Jermyn Street shirtmaker, founded 1885, whose factory is in Gloucestershire. The vault holds it as a Bond voile-shirt maker and a name in Drake's history.

## Lineage
The London research cache gives 1885, a Gloucester factory and ownership by Ali Fayed; the Wikipedia source was not read.[^3] Permanent Style's Drake's history records that the Hill & Drake line was sold on to Turnbull & Asser.[^1]

## Making and Material
A Bond Suits article names Turnbull & Asser among the makers of the cotton voile dress shirt worn in the films.[^2] No construction, cloth source or factory detail is in a sourced note.
