## In One Line
A London house with a real invention behind it, gabardine, and a long record on mountains and terraces. Today it is better known for cachet, which the vault's London research files under brands with no lineage evidence.

## Lineage
Thomas Burberry founded the house in 1856, introduced gabardine in 1880 and trademarked the logo in 1900, per the Sport in History introduction [^1]. The Mallory replica project (2002-2006, led by Prof. Mary B. Rose) recorded the 1924 Everest jacket and breeches as Burberry gabardine over silk and wool [^2]. In the 1930s Baracuta's founders, the Miller brothers, made rain coats for Burberry and Aquascutum in Manchester [^6]. Casuals of the 1970s and 1980s wore Burberry and Aquascutum [^3].

## Making and Material
The Mountain Heritage Trust describes a Burberry-made replica of Mallory's gabardine jacket and breeches; Hoyland tested it on Everest at 5,150 m and 6,340 m and found it comfortable but probably not warm enough for a bivouac [^2]. The Rake reports that the family factories of Azam, owners of Grenfell, once made about 400 Burberry coats a week [^5]. The vault records no current mills, factories or cloth weights for Burberry's own lines.

## Patina and Care
No source on how gabardine ages or on repair. The Mallory testing says only how the replica performed, not how it wears.

## Today
A 2026 round-up of Harrington makers describes Burberry's version as cleaner in line with sharper fabrics and an occasional flash of check lining [^4]. Raeburn's studio in Hackney was reported in 2019 to occupy a former Burberry factory [^7]. The vault's London research lists the house among brands with cachet but no lineage evidence for current production.

## Design
Current output reads as refined and brand-led: sharper cloth, the check as a signature, the Harrington described by a retailer round-up in a few lines [^4]. The older gabardine garments were designed for rain and altitude; the modern design has moved to image.

## Depth
Seven source notes touch it, none a Burberry maker page. The strongest is the Mallory replica report; the weakest is the cachet claim, which rests on the vault's own research note.
