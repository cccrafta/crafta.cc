## In One Line
Osaka-area Japanese denim maker that appears in the vault in three passing mentions. Scored from those and from general knowledge; treat as provisional.

## Lineage
Founding and mill history are not in the vault; the brand is commonly placed among Osaka's reproduction denim makers. [unsourced]

## Making and Material
A Heddels 2024 piece, read only as a snippet, reports Samurai's natural-indigo-dyed ripstop jungle jacket and pants.[^1] A 2026 weekly feed note mentions a Samurai Kurozan belt in indigo-dyed Tochigi vegetable-tanned leather described as color-fast.[^2] Denim cloth, looms and sewing are not described.

## Patina and Care
No source in the vault.

## Today
Active in 2024 with the indigo ripstop jungle jacket.[^1]

## Design
A natural-indigo jungle jacket in ripstop rather than denim is the point the vault's idea note takes up: the weave, not the dye, decides weight and fade.[^1]

## Depth
Very thin. Heddels also lists Samurai among makers of coach jackets (with Warehouse and Real McCoy's) in its baseball-and-menswear piece.[^3]
