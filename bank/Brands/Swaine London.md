## In One Line
A London house dated to 1750 that began as a whip maker and grew into umbrellas, sticks and hats; the vault has only the maker's About page, so where each product is made is not confirmed.

## Lineage
Its About page says Swaine was founded in 1750 as a whip maker, added Brigg umbrellas and walking sticks from 1838, and brought in Herbert Johnson, whose own date is 1889 with a royal warrant in 1901 [^1]. Swaine Adeney Brigg acquired Herbert Johnson in 1996 [^1]. The page is promotional, so these are maker claims.

## Making and Material
The page has a workshop and atelier on New Bond Street and a footer reading "Made in the UK", but it does not say where each product is made [^1]. The London Brands research flags Brigg's or Swaine's place of making as unconfirmed [unsourced]. No material or construction detail is documented.

## Patina and Care
The About page lists servicing and restoration, which implies objects are meant to be repaired and kept [^1]. No source describes how its leather or cane ages.

## Today
The maker lists a shop and atelier on New Bond Street and says it offers servicing and restoration [^1]. Nothing in the vault records current range, prices or independent assessment.
