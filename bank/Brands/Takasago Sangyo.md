## In One Line
Hyogo shoe factory that SOU SOU names as its main jika-tabi maker; the only evidence is SOU SOU's own press story, so everything here is a client's account of the factory.

## Lineage
The PR TIMES story describes Takasago Sangyo as a running-shoe maker in Hyogo that first refused SOU SOU's jika-tabi project.[^1] Its chairman, Kako Akiharu, then backed it, and the factory remains SOU SOU's main producer.[^1] No founding date appears. [unsourced] The company is a long-established regional shoe manufacturer.

## Making and Material
The story gives about 20,000 pairs a year and says only three Japanese companies still make jika-tabi, of which Takasago is the main factory for SOU SOU.[^1] A running-shoe maker applying its rubber and sole skills to a split-toe work shoe is the process point.[^1] Cloth and rubber suppliers are not recorded.

No source in the vault. [unsourced] Sole and big-toe seam wear are the likely points; no repair offer is documented.

## Today
The Margiela tabi research lists Takasago in its account of where jika-tabi are made now, again sourced to SOU SOU's story.[^2] No independent coverage exists in the vault.
