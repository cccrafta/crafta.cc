## Today
The essay "From Sport to Staple" argues that durability, comfort and ease of movement carried athletic garments into everyday wardrobes, citing the rugby shirt, the Lacoste pique shirt and the terry sweatshirt.[^1] It makes no workwear comparison, and the Lacoste claim is single-retailer; Lacoste's own site dates the L.12.12 to 1933, not the 1920s.[^1] The essay calls the long relaxing of workplace dress "casualification".[^1]
