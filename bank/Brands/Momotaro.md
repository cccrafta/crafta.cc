## In One Line
A Japanese denim maker that specialist press uses as a reference point for Japanese jean construction; the vault holds no maker page, only mentions in comparisons and a price note.

## Lineage
No founding date, founder or mill is documented in the vault. It is named in the vault only through others: Heddels lists Momotaro among loopwheel specialists [^1], and two London reviews use Momotaro jeans as the Japanese benchmark that Blackhorse Lane Ateliers set itself against [^2] [^3].

## Making and Material
Permanent Style contrasts Blackhorse Lane's felled stitching with "the big yellow overlock of Momotaro-type jeans" [^2]. Denimhunters records Han Ates saying Japanese jeans often use overlock for speed, and that he studied Oni and Momotaro jeans before choosing tucked edges and a one-piece fly [^3]. These are a rival's descriptions, and no source from Momotaro describes its cloth, looms or dyeing. The research note on Working Craft records a kakishibu-dyed pair priced at $499 and a Redcast Heritage collaboration of 200 pairs, drawn from Heddels headlines that were not read in full [unsourced].
