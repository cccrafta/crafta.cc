## Angle
HBT (herringbone twill) was the US military's standard work fabric from the 1940s through the Vietnam era — worn as fatigue shirts, coveralls, and field trousers. The diagonal herringbone weave gave it durability beyond plain cotton twill. Japanese brands like Headlight are reviving exact period specs. Underwritten at Crafta.
