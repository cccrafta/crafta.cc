## Angle
EC0Tex (Bäumlin & Ernst with Empa, Switzerland) coats each filament with an organosilicon layer by dry plasma; H&B Materials (France) grafts plant fatty acids onto cellulose. Both are winners' claims from a fair organiser's release, not independent tests, and neither has wash-durability data in what was read. Both makers are in scope (Swiss, French). Look for Empa's own paper and any wash-test data.
