## Angle
Heddels (Feb 2024, snippet only) reports Samurai's natural-indigo-dyed ripstop jungle jacket and pants. Fujito also made a Ventile version (Heddels 2018, snippet). Compare with A Vontade's 9oz double-indigo denim. The weave (ripstop poplin vs twill) decides weight, drying and fade behaviour. Source notes for the Samurai and Fujito pieces were not created; read both pages first.
