## Angle
The shooting/hunting vest's game pocket — a large rear-access pouch for carrying birds — is one of the most specific pocket forms in workwear. Engineered Garments' SS26 vest series brings this construction into contemporary tailoring. The vest as a serious outer layer rather than a layering piece.
