## Angle
Green Book: gas flaps were imposed on all HBT clothing and "disliked intensely by men serving in the jungles". The 1963 first pattern carries an inner gas flap; the 3rd pattern (late 1967) drops it. Suzuki (Engineered Garments) kept the gas flap, side tabs and concealed buttons while removing epaulettes. A single detail traced through a design is a record of problems solved and then unsolved.
