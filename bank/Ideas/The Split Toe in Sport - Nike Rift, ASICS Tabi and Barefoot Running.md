## Angle
Kiko Kostadinov x ASICS draws on ASICS' 1970s Tabi marathon shoes; NikeSKIMS's Mesh Rift is based on the Nike Air Rift and its barefoot-running thinking; FDMTL x Vans puts a split toe on a skate shoe in boro denim; Marugo says its split-toe shoes help balance and grip and sells running and training versions. Function-driven lineage across work and sport, the owner's newest thread. Design hypothesis, tag `untested`: whether the split toe's balance and grip claims (all maker claims) hold for climbing or approach shoes. Needs a footwear-biomechanics source before any post.
