## Angle
Deep dive, 1000-2000 words. No tracked publication treats workwear and sportswear as one design lineage, and the scholarship calls the overlap "a huge area for further research". Risk: the workwear half rests mostly on Crafta's own earlier posts; the strongest new evidence is on the sport side. Do not assume one-way migration from function to fashion, since FIT and V&A both frame the influence as two-way.
