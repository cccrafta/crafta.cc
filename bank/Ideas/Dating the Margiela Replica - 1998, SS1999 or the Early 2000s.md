## Angle
When did Margiela first show the German Army Trainer, and what did "Artisanal" mean in 1999? Grailed and Truss draw on the same shoe-label story and may not be independent. The house's About page dates its first Artisanal collection to 2006 and does not mention the trainer, which suggests the SS1999 label is retroactive (inference). Needs a house archive entry, a show-notes scan, or a museum record (Galliera, MoMu). Fits the owner's interest in military-to-civilian lineage and in the paper trail behind a design.
