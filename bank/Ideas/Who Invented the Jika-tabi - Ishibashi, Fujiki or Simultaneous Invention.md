## Angle
Three accounts of the first rubber-soled work tabi. Marugo (Kurashiki): Fujiki Itaro sewed reshaped rickshaw tyres to tabi, c. 1917 or 1919. Japanese Wikipedia (T4, mostly unsourced): Ishibashi Tokujiro's glued-on sole, sold 1922 by Nippon Tabi, utility model no. 80594 registered October 1923. ABC: Shojiro Ishibashi (Bridgestone) in 1922, likely a conflation of the brothers. Marugo chairman: rubber soles appeared worldwide at once, Converse started rubber-soled sneakers in 1917. Primary job: the 1923 utility model record and a Kurume or Kurashiki archive. Anchor claim, needs 2+ independent sources.
