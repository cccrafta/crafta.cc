## Verification (2026-10-17)
- The split is stated by Martens himself in the SS27 show notes: Parisian ateliers "divided between the 'tailleur'... and the 'flou'" ([[Sources/References/Margiela SS27 Blossoming — FashionNetwork]]).
- La Galerie Dior, Dior's house museum, explains the same division and says Dior today keeps one atelier of each, about thirty people apiece ([[Sources/References/Flou and Tailleur Ateliers — La Galerie Dior]]).
- No Fédération de la Haute Couture et de la Mode, V&A, Palais Galliera or Met page found; the claim that Margiela "collapsed" the split rests on Martens' notes and two reviews (WWD, Design Scene). Keep that wording attributed.
