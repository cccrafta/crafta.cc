## Angle
Design hypothesis, `untested`. The Quartermaster textile section chose poplin because a tight, thin weave held the least water and dried fastest. That is a specification, not a style. Question for the workwear-inspired activewear line: does a 5-6oz poplin jungle-jacket pattern, with its pocket layout and adjusters, work for the body under load, or does the cloth need to change?
