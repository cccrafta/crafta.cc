## Angle
The Denim Institute and Museum opens in Downtown LA's Gerry Building in June 2026 — the first institution dedicated to denim preservation and education. Founded by Jonny Saven and Loren Cronk. What gets included, what gets left out, and what happens to a garment's meaning when it moves from a surplus store to a museum vitrine.
