## Angle
Marugo (Kurashiki) says Western popularity keeps the tradition alive; SOU SOU says half its clientele is overseas. In scope: Japanese craft.

Found so far (2026-10-17): Japanese maker-side sources now in the vault. Marugo's own history and METI profile show demand for jika-tabi tapering, and ASSABOOTS (Europe 2009, Japan 2012) launched in answer to overseas requests; SOU・SOU's press story says only 3 companies still make jika-tabi in Japan and about 98% sold are made abroad. Still needed: a maker interview or visit on construction and why makers closed.
