## Angle
Design cornerstone for a workwear-inspired climbing collection, and a possible companion post. The gusset is documented in martial arts and climbing, not in workwear, so an article must say so plainly. Hypotheses to test with climbers: abrasion on rock drives double knees, a replaceable knee panel is wanted, hemp canvas beats blends on drying and weight, gusset plus leg-hem cuff improves high-stepping.
