## Angle
Both independent testers found the membrane unremarkable and the DWR the weak point: Trek & Mountain saw it stop beading on cuffs within months. Compare with cloths that shed water by weave or wax, not by fluorine (inference, verify). Needs a repair/re-proofing source and owner first-hand notes; no multi-year wear evidence exists yet.
