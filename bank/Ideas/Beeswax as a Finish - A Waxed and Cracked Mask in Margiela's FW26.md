## Angle
WWD says a waxed and cracked laced mask started the Artisanal lineup and ties beeswax to its use as a binder in ancient China; Hypebeast says a 6 m Edwardian dress had its layers moulded together with beeswax. Compare with waxed cotton (Barbour) as a working finish: same material, opposite intent (cracking is the point).
