## Angle
Barbour dates the two-piece International for Captain Phillips of HMS Ursula to 1940; The Vintage Showroom dates the prototype to 1939 and a revised Royal Navy-wide version to 1941/42, with fewer than 20 prototype suits by the author's estimate. Lawrence Nedas & Co of Kent also built it. The Vintage Showroom author says the Ursula suit inspired the International; Barbour's own order is the reverse (International 1936, two-piece 1940). Needs the Barbour Archive or a Royal Navy record to settle.
