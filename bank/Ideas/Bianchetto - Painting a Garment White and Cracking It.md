## Angle
FW26 had an Edwardian tailcoat painted, baked and cracked (runway review only). Needs primary sources on method; patina angle: deliberate aging versus earned aging.

Found so far (2026-10-17): the house's own text gives the origin (1989, Salvation Army studio furniture overpainted white, extended to the Tabi the same year), a matt white applied "in the same way as walls are painted", visible brush strokes, intended cracking and fading, wash-inside-out care. Not found anywhere: paint chemistry, coats, any heat step. The method itself is the open question; an atelier visit or direct question to the house is the only route.
