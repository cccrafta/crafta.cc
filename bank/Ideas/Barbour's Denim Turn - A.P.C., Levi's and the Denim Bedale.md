## Angle
Barbour's usual cloth is 6oz Sylkoil waxed canvas built for weather. Denim resists weather less but needs no rewaxing, per Gear Patrol (opinion). Three denim releases in about two years: A.P.C. ("Barbour's wax with A.P.C.'s denim"), Levi's (30 Oct 2025), Denim Bedale (Japan-only first, $660 in the US). Open questions: mills, weights, raw vs washed, and where each is made, none found.
