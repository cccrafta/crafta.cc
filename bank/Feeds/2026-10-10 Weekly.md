## This Week in Craft & Fashion

### Heddels: Japanese Spinning, Selvedge Chino, Indian Weaving
Heddels published ten items between 2 and 9 October. Three matter for Crafta. "All About the Ishikawadai Spinning Machine" (7 Oct) is a primer on a Japanese yarn-spinning device. ONI made its "Just Right Straight" jeans from selvedge chino cloth (9 Oct), a khaki-toned twill instead of denim. Sethu (2 Oct) reworks American silhouettes such as chore coats in handwoven Indian cotton. The rest of the week was product coverage: Meermin's burgundy shell cordovan chukka, Red Wing's Pecos, Y'2 Leather's WJ-04 roughout western jacket, FAIZ T.S. at ten years, and a new denim label from Gordon Heffner called Hidden Forts.

### Long John: Kingpins Amsterdam Run-Up
Long John's feed is dominated by Kingpins Amsterdam (14–15 October, SugarCity): a show preview, Kohinoor's eco-dyeing and washes, Naveena's SS28 "Reading Club" fanzines, and Pioneer Denim's research shown on a Tokyo runway. One garment story stands out: Blue Blanket, an Italian brand, made its own Italian-made version of the Future Farmers of America corduroy jacket (5 Oct), with a history of the original.

### Nepenthes LA: Garment Deep Dives
Nepenthes ran two "Deep Dive" pieces in September. The Bedford Jacket (11 Sep) covers a barber-uniform origin, a Boston-street name and a workwear-tailoring hybrid construction. The Officer Pant (3 Sep) traces the wide-legged cut to WWII US Navy officers' khaki trousers, with modern changes such as a zipper fly and rear darts. Both are maker-side accounts of Engineered Garments pieces. The one item inside the last 14 days is Engineered Garments x Tricker's (7 Oct): Asymmetric Gibson and Multi Tone Brogue on the 4444 last with lightweight Vibram Morflex soles.

### Bryceland's
"A Spotlight on Made-to-Measure" (9 Oct) argues the wearer is the final judge of a tailored garment and the fitter's job is to guide the commission toward what suits the person. Earlier in the summer: "Ten Years of Bryceland's" (7 Aug) and "The Explorers" lookbook, Parts I and II.

### Web Search: Denim Industry Headlines
- Lee x Nigel Cabourn "OX'ed Silver" launched in the US on 8 October, drawing on archival American workwear and Japanese craftsmanship.
- Wrangler x imogene + willie runs a three-drop collaboration through 7 November. Drop one uses deadstock Cone Mills White Oak selvedge; later drops use broken twill from Mount Vernon Mills.
- Miu Miu teased a Miu Miu Upcycled collection with Wrangler at Paris Fashion Week.
- Paris SS27 denim leaned dark, with American workwear and Southwestern influences in tobacco-hued jeans and work pants.
