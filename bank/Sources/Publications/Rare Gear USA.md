## About
Vintage military retailer that publishes identification guides on its blog. Its Vietnam tropical combat jacket guide gives the pattern sequence, with dates that conflict across the guide and other collectors.

## Relation to Crafta
T3 retailer. Use it for what changed between patterns, not for dates or for claims about why (snagging explanations are retailer copy).
