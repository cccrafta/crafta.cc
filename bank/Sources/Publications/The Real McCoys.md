## About
Japanese maker of faithful reproductions of military and work clothing. Its MJ22006 combat tropical coat is described by the maker as poplin, with urea buttons, sewn in-house in Japan and copying the early models.

## Relation to Crafta
T1 as a maker's own account of its product, not a historical source; treat its statements as the maker's claims. Its reasoning behind the design was not found.
