## Summary
Heddels (1 Nov 2022) on A Vontade's Denim Jungle Fatigue jacket, a Tokyo-made jungle jacket in 9oz double-indigo denim. It is the best weight comparator found for a denim jungle jacket; the article says nothing about ageing. Fetch summary only.

## Key Claims
- A Vontade (Tokyo, made in Japan) makes a Denim Jungle Fatigue jacket in 9oz warp and weft yarn-dyed double-indigo denim.
- Details: concealed button front, four flap stow pockets, strap side adjusters, BDU-style elbow patches and sleeve gussets.
- It is based on 1960s-70s US subtropical jackets.
- It costs $486 at Sonder Supplies.
- Heddels called it "contemporary, but one we can certainly get behind".

## Notes
- The article says nothing about ageing.
- Agent arithmetic, not from the source: 9oz denim against the original 5.5oz poplin (per Nepenthes) is roughly 1.6 times heavier.
