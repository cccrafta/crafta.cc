## Summary
Mail-order catalogue read through its OCR text layer (the OCR is letter-spaced, so passages were read after removing spaces and some words are glued). Its work-pants and overalls pages show the standard 1922 construction claims, while its athletic pages sell wool tights with double knees, so both traditions use the same device in one book.
## Key Claims
- Work pants ("Big Buck" line): "All pockets, belt loops and points of strain are well reinforced with bar tacks. The seat and crotch are strongly sewed and taped." and "We guarantee these pants not to rip in the seams."
- Same pants: two side pockets, two hip pockets and a watch pocket; "Extra heavy weight" cloth.
- Overalls copy: "All Garments Are Triple Stitched, Full Cut, They Cannot Bind, Reinforced at All Strain Points"; a picture caption shows "how bar tacks strengthen pocket corners".
- Material guarantee: overalls are "2-20 White Back Indigo Blue Denim, firmly woven, heavy weight cotton".
- Overalls are "cut over extra large, roomy patterns, eliminating binding and insuring ease and comfort to the wearer".
- Overall lines include a Double Knee and Front Apron style; a Double Front and Double Seat style "extending below the knees" with "extra thickness of cloth forming double seat" reinforcing the back from waist to crotch; and an "Extra Strong" style with detachable suspenders with "elastic inserts, which allow play at the shoulders".
- Athletic section sells "Wrestling Tights With Double Knees", all-wool worsted knit, for "wrestling, hockey, skating or gymnasium", so double-knee reinforcement appears in both a work and a sport garment in the same 1922 catalogue.
- Catalogue is a retailer's marketing copy: it shows what was claimed and sold, not factory test data.
