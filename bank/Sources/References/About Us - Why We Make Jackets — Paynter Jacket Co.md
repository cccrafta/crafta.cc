## Summary
Maker's own account. T1 for where the jackets are designed and made and which mill supplies the cloth; commercial framing is the maker's own.

## Key Claims
- Jackets are 'designed by us in East London' and made in a small family-run factory in Northern Portugal; the factory belongs to Sergio's family in Guimaraes, just outside Porto.
- Founders Huw Thomas and Becky Okell met in 2018 at a London workshop; no exact founding date is given on the page.
- The fabric comes from Berto, a mill near Venice making cloth since the 1850s, chosen after the founders were unhappy with the factory's usual suppliers.
- First jackets were dyed with natural indigo; a garment dyer about 20 km from the factory now dyes to Pantone references.
- Jackets are made in limited batches 'just four times a year'; the Archive page lists everything made so far.
