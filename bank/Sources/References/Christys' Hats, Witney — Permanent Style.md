## Summary
2012 factory visit; a 2025 reader comment says felt hoods are now imported from Portugal (unconfirmed).

## Key Claims
- Christys made hats for Lock & Co and Bates, neither of which makes its own; staff fell from over 3,000 at the peak to about 30.
- Felting was done in Stockport (bought 1821 per the article) and blocking in Witney; one of about 10 makers worldwide doing both; some flanges over 150 years old.
- About a third of the business was military and police hats (the Metropolitan Police helmet has a plastic base with felt formed over it); 10,000 bowlers and toppers that year.
- Crompton: Lock hats differ from Christys' own mainly in felt thickness and finishing such as the ribbon band.
