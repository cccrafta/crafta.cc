## Summary
Textile Exchange, September 2025, covering 2024 data (T2 industry data body). Read from the PDF text layer.

## Key Claims
- Global fibre production reached a record 132 million tonnes in 2024, up from about 125 million in 2023, driven by virgin fossil-based synthetics.
- The recycled-fibre share stayed around 7.6% in 2024; most was recycled polyester from plastic bottles (6.9% of all fibre).
- "Overall, less than 1% of the global fiber market was from pre- and post-consumer recycled textiles."
- The report names the limits of textile-to-textile recycling as a threat to the industry's climate commitments.
