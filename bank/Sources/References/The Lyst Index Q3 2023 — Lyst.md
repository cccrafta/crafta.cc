## Summary
Lyst's own quarterly ranking (T2: primary publisher of the data, but a retailer-aggregator with a commercial interest). Read via WebFetch (a model summary of the page), so second-hand.

## Key Claims
- The Maison Margiela Tabi Mary Jane was #1 on the Q3 2023 hottest products list; searches for Tabis rose 342% in September 2023.
- The rise followed the viral "Tabi Swiper" TikTok story (a woman's Tabis stolen by a Tinder date), reported as followed by more than 178M TikTok users.
- Method: the products list filters more than eight million items by social-media mentions, searches, page views, interactions and sales on Lyst; colours of one style are merged and demand is weighed against stock.
- The page is internally inconsistent on the Tory Burch mules (text says third, list says sixth).
