## Summary
Maker's bespoke page: in-house making, handloom, cutters named in the jacket.

## Key Claims
- Bespoke garments are handmade at 11 Savile Row; 'bespoke tailors since 1849'; commissions from GBP 7,500 for a two-piece suit; at least three fittings.
- Huntsman says it is the only Savile Row tailor with its own handloom and the only house where clients can design a signature tweed, sampled in-house.
- The cutter's name stays inside the jacket; clients meet the artisans who make the commission.
- Specialities: shooting and field-sports wear, morning wear, and an Inherited Garment Service (alteration of inherited Huntsman pieces; not offered for other makers' garments, per the FAQ via search summary).
