## Summary
2020 interview. Gives lineage and the Japan production fallback; does not give studio location or construction detail.

## Key Claims
- Daley grew up in Leicester; father Jamaican-British, mother Scottish, parents ran a reggae club in Edinburgh in the late 1970s and early 1980s; his Jamaican grandfather was a shoemaker and his mother's family were knitters (the article names Edinburgh, not Dundee).
- He graduated from Central Saint Martins menswear in 2013; the graduate collection Culture Clash mixed Caribbean cool, Scottish craft and Japanese silhouettes.
- Sarah Mower praises his use of 'tweed and fabric from U.K. mills'; he works with British headwear makers and shoe manufacturers.
- When many UK mills closed in the pandemic he made much of the Stepping Razor collection in Japan with smaller suppliers outside Tokyo; Beams ordered pieces early on.
- Oversize baker boy hats are cut 'to fit a big fro or a lot of dreads'.
