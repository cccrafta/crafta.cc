## Summary
Business report (published 2026-09-19) on Martens leaving Diesel, read directly via curl. Gives dates the FashionNetwork piece omits.

## Key Claims
- Martens leaves Diesel after the SS27 show on 22 September 2026 and remains Creative Director of Maison Margiela; the show falls three days after the announcement (OTB announced on Friday 2026-09-18).
- Six-year tenure; joined Diesel in October 2020 after the 2018 Diesel Red Tag collaboration; no successor named.
- Appointed Margiela creative director in January 2025, succeeding John Galliano after his decade at the house; first Artisanal collection shown at Paris Haute Couture Week in July 2025.
- OTB 2025 results: turnover €1.7 billion, EBITDA €237.3 million, Margiela sales up 8.4%, Diesel's best profitability in a decade; Diesel appointed Andrea Rigogliosi CEO in January 2026.
