## Summary
Maker's page, partly garbled in fetch. Founding year here (2006) conflicts with a secondary 2002; mills are not named.

## Key Claims
- Founded by photographer Guy Hills and woven-textile designer Kirsty McDougall in a weave and design studio in East London; the page gives 2006.
- Cloth is woven in the UK in 'the best mills in the country'; technical yarns such as hi-vis cotton are used to update traditional tweed.
- Flagship opened in Mayfair in 2014 and moved to 47 Dorset Street, Marylebone, in 2018; the cloths were an instant success with Savile Row tailors.
- The brand frames tweed as Britain's original sportswear; fabrics are sold by the metre and jackets, trousers and coats are tailored.
