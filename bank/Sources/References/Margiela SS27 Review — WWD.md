## Summary
WWD review of "Hard and Soft" (published 2026-09-29). Read directly via curl (WebFetch is blocked with 451). Verdict: big, uneven, full of ideas.

## Key Claims
- Martens called the coed collection "Hard and Soft"; his aim was "to fuse structured garments and tailoring with dressmaking and draping."
- Sculptural coats and jackets, some looking "shaped from clay", were the highlight, including the tailored trench that opened the show with dramatic folds at the neck and an apron-like front.
- Jackets from dark denim to satin tuxedo carried stiff built-in corset panels at the front with soft, folded necklines and lapels.
- Corset panels also appeared in silky T-shirts, turtlenecks and long dresses; one white dress had a bodice of flattened silverware worn on the outside.
- Menswear: trousers with thick tuxedo stripes down the leg and deconstructed jackets and shirts with buttons folded and hidden inside.
- Criticism: too many ideas and not enough focus; flower prints on a shiny stiff pink skirt resembled "1970s tablecloths" on dresses, coats and boots.
- The review text does not use the words tailleur or flou; the atelier-split framing comes from FashionNetwork and Design Scene.
