## Summary
Charles McFarlane's article for Put This On (1 Feb 2019, T2) on how far Barbour jackets were really used in the 1982 Falklands campaign, and on later military-themed Barbour collaborations. Read via a fetch summary only.

## Key Claims
- Per Put This On: Barbour jackets reached the South Atlantic in small numbers in 1982, mostly the Durham and Solway Zipper on Royal Marines, largely as private purchases.
- Collector Paul Hannon calls their use "a little exaggerated"; Ian Gardner (The Yompers) says men bought Barbour jackets privately.
- Captain Mick Cotton's customised jacket is claimed by Barbour's archive, but the author dates the archive Durham to after 1987 (three Royal Warrants) and found no other customised Barbour from the conflict.
- Later military-themed collaborations and reissues, per Put This On: Tokihito Yoshida (F/W 2010), Engineered Garments (AW 2018), Adidas.
