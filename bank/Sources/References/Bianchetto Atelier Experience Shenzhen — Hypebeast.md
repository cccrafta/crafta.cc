## Summary
Event report (2026-04-13) on a hands-on Bianchetto session. Via WebFetch summary.

## Key Claims
- Bianchetto: a white paint layer covers a surface leaving brushstrokes visible; it cracks and fades, recording wear; debuted at the first show in 1989 (press framing; the house's own glossary dates it to 1989 via overpainted studio furniture, see [[Sources/References/Bianchetto Glossary — Maison Margiela]]).
- Event: April 11–12, 2026, Hairun Badminton Courts, Nanshan District, Shenzhen.
- Guests brought a personal wardrobe item to treat with the method under the Paris atelier team, and received a blouse blanche (the white work coat of Paris atelier staff).
- Part of MaisonMargiela/folders, an ongoing project showing internal workings through physical and digital archive files (public Dropbox folders).
- Follows the FW26 Shanghai show and continues through exhibitions and experiences across China.
