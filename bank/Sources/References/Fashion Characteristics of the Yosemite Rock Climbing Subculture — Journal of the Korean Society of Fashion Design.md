## Summary
Peer-reviewed article (Minho Choi and Sungeun Suh, Gachon University; 22(1):159-176, March 2022, DOI 10.18652/2022.22.1.9). Only the abstract was readable via a search summary because ResearchGate returned 403; enrich later if the full text can be obtained.
## Key Claims
- Method: the authors collected 250 images from the documentary Valley Uprising and related publications and analysed 23 that show the subcultural character of climbing.
- Finding: the Yosemite subculture formed through the 1950s Beat movement and expressed rebellion through climbing and fashion.
- Several styles coexisted and fused: beat, hippie, psychedelic, surfer and skateboard.
- A search summary (not confirmed in the abstract I could read) says the paper identifies 1970s climbers wearing workwear and sportswear such as painter pants, rugby shirts and running shorts.
