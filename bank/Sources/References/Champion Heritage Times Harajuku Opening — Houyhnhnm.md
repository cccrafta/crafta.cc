## Summary
Houyhnhnm report (by Kawahara Mine) on Champion's Tokyo archive store and its "True Archives" reissues. Publication year not stated on the page; 2019 is inferred from the 25 October Friday and the image path, so unverified. Useful for what Champion itself reissues and the Japanese-market role.

## Key Claims
- Store: Champion Heritage Times Harajuku, Newspot Harajuku, 4-28-25 Jingumae, Shibuya, opening Friday 25 October (year inferred 2019).
- Two lines: "True Archives" (full reissues) and "Homage Archives" (inspired by archive pieces).
- First Reverse Weave reissue (patented 1938, JPY 20,000 plus tax) has seamless one-piece shoulders and a V-shaped neck gusset.
- Second reissue (patented 1952, JPY 20,000 plus tax) was the first with vertical side gussets, called the "expansion gusset".
- RABART athletic shirt reissue (JPY 22,000 plus tax): Sam Friedland, developer of Reverse Weave, also patented it around 1938; the page says it was not strictly sold under the Champion name and is poorly documented.
- Homage Archives includes a tailored jacket (JPY 30,000) and trouser pants (JPY 24,000) with the expansion gusset detail, i.e. a sports construction detail moved into tailoring.
- A Made in USA Reverse Weave series is also stocked.
