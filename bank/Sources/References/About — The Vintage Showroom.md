## Summary

## Key Claims
- Established 2007 as a resource for designers and stylists; later added womenswear, denim and childrenswear.
- Showroom is at 17 Rosebery Avenue, Clerkenwell, EC1R 4SP, by appointment only (the 2009 Covent Garden shop is no longer given as the base).
- The digital Rental Catalogue covers 'over 2000 menswear garments'; clients hire garments for costume or buy high-resolution image packs.
- Services include virtual appointments, student research sessions and university workshops; Doug Gunn is named as co-owner.
