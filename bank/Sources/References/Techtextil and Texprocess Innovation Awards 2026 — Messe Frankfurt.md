## Summary
Messe Frankfurt press release (T1) for the fair organiser; ceremony 21 April 2026, Frankfurt. Read as page text via curl. Replaces the Fibre2Fashion stub as the source for the awards.

## Key Claims
- 17 international winners in ten categories across Techtextil and Texprocess Innovation Awards 2026.
- EC0Tex (Bäumlin & Ernst, Switzerland, with Empa, Lothos and Seilfabrik Ullmann), "New Concept": a dry-plasma process coats each filament with an ultra-thin organosilicon layer so inelastic and highly elastic yarns are permanently water-repellent and quick-drying without PFAS or fresh water; first PFAS-free swimming and water-sports products planned within twelve months.
- H&B Materials (France), "New Chemicals & Dyes": a PFAS-free water-repellent finish grafting plant-based fatty acids onto cellulose fibres, scoring 5 out of 5 in spray tests on cotton and blends (winner's claim); seed funding round planned for 2026.
- The release states that from January 2026 France is gradually banning the sale of apparel containing PFAS, with other textiles to follow.
- FormLig Knitted Wood (spek Design with DITF Denkendorf, Tecnaro, Buck): lignin-coated cellulose yarns knitted and heat-set into shapes.
