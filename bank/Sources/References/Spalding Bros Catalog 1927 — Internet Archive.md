## Summary
The 1927 Spalding catalogue (IA identifier spalding-bros-catalog-1927; price pages dated 5 July 1927). The OCR text is noisy, so only entries that read clearly are recorded. Shows American team jerseys sold in worsted and heavy cotton, and a sporting-goods house also supplying occupational (aviators') clothing. Use for corroboration of period fabrics, not for precise figures.

## Key Claims
- Football jerseys in "fashioned worsted" from about $5.50 to $9.00, and a heavy-weight cotton jersey (No. 31F) at $4.50 with worsted collar, elastic shoulder, worsted elastic sleeve ends and double-reinforced elbow, described as a "good serviceable jersey".
- Striped-sleeve worsted jersey No. 10PFX at $7.75; the catalogue says striping "usually alternat[es] two inches of same color as body and narrow stripes of some other color".
- No. 6F quarter-sleeve white cotton shirt at 75c "used by most foot ball players underneath jerseys".
- "Stick-Em" rubberised cloth sold for attaching to jersey sleeves for grip: a functional add-on to the garment.
- A cotton shirt, cotton-fleece-lined (No. TC), in gray, brown or white at $1.75, listed among athletic shirts; no item is named "sweatshirt" in the OCR text searched.
- A section headed "Spalding Aviators' Equipment" (page 79, July 5, 1927) lists winter flying suits with waterproof cloth and moleskin outer shells and fur linings: a sports house also sold occupational clothing in the same catalogue.
- OCR caveat: several price lines are garbled; verify against the scan before quoting.
