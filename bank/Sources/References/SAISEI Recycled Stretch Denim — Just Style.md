## Summary
Just Style report (October 2024) of the Lenzing / Kaihara Denim / Roica by Asahi Kasei SAISEI launch. Read as page text via curl. It restates the press release; the primary version is [[Sources/References/SAISEI Collection Launch — Lenzing]].

## Key Claims
- Saisei collection of premium stretch denim from recycled materials, a collaboration of Lenzing, Kaihara Denim and Roica by Asahi Kasei; debuted at Kingpins Amsterdam.
- Three fabrications: super stretch (Monster Stretch by Kaihara Denim), comfort stretch, and low stretch (selvedge denim).
- Lenzing Ecovero fibres with Refibra technology integrate "up to 20% post-consumer textile waste" (cellulose-rich materials and polyester-cotton blends).
- Roica EF yarn is made from pre-consumer recycled content and is stated to have 30% lower CO2 emissions than regular yarns.
- Not stated here: Kaihara's share of Japanese denim or export countries. Earlier text in this note giving "about half of Japanese denim" and "30 countries" came from a search summary, not this article, and was removed.
