## Summary
Museum's own about page. Exhibition venue rather than a deep archive.

## Key Claims
- Founded in 2003 by Dame Zandra Rhodes; run by Newham College since 2007; 83 Bermondsey Street, SE1 3XF, in a Ricardo Legorreta building.
- 'The only UK museum dedicated to showcasing contemporary fashion and textile design'; no permanent display, rotating exhibitions, over 30 major shows.
- Holds 'a small permanent collection' of 20th-century fashion and textiles used by its learning programmes.
