## Summary
Circ press release dated 18 February 2026 (posted 27 February 2026) (T1). Read as page text via curl. Circ (Danville, Virginia) is a different company from Circulose (Sundsvall).

## Key Claims
- Madewell (J.Crew Group), Reformation and C&A joined the second cohort of Circ's Fiber Club alongside supply partners Lenzing and Linz Textil.
- First cohort launched January 2025: Bestseller, Eileen Fisher, Everlane and Zalando, with supply partners Arvind, Birla Cellulose and Foshan Chicley.
- Each brand develops collections with TENCEL™ | Circ® with REFIBRA™ lyocell containing 30% Circ pulp from recycled polycotton waste; Circ supplies pulp, Lenzing makes the fibre, Linz Textil spins the yarn.
- The club is meant to pool demand to overcome minimum-order and pricing barriers that held back new materials.
- Circ's first industrial-scale facility is described as "underway in France" (not operating).
