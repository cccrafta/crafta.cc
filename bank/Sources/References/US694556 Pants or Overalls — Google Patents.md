## Summary
Patent for a separate cuff sewn inside the bottom of pant or overall legs so no inside hem is left to snag on the wearer's shoe and the hem edge resists wear. It documents leg-hem wear and snagging as a design problem for overalls in 1901-02. Full OCR text and three claims were readable.
## Key Claims
- Inventor Howard S. Lanier of Danville, Virginia; filed 13 August 1901, granted 4 March 1902.
- Purpose: "reinforcing-cuff for lower ends leg portions pants, overalls, like," so that "no inside hem is employed, edges legs strengthened held in shape".
- Snagging problem: "there is no interior hem or projection of any kind in leg a, which might catch in wearers shoe".
- Wear problem: the leg bottom is "apt to wrinkle and rot by absorbing moisture in walking" (OCR-garbled).
- Construction: cuff deeper at front and back than at the sides, sewn to the inside-out leg with a hidden seam, then turned right side out and folded over it, leaving four layers at the bottom edge with two rows of outer stitching.
- If the outer stitching wears through, the hidden seam still holds cuff and hem.
- Not about knees, seat, pockets or gussets.
