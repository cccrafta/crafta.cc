## Summary
Hypebeast's release report on the Barbour x Levi's collaboration, which launched on 30 Oct 2025 with denim and waxed versions of Barbour's jackets. It describes which Levi's and Barbour details were combined but gives no mill or place of manufacture.

## Key Claims
- The Barbour x Levi's collection launched on 30 Oct 2025.
- It includes denim and waxed versions of the men's Bedale and the Type II jacket.
- The collection uses Levi's trucker cuffs, back-cinch detail and arcuate pocket shapes alongside Barbour's waxed cotton and corduroy collars.
- Hypebeast says the pieces "honor the workwear roots of both brands" and pairs waxed cotton with denim that "personalizes over time" (Hypebeast's wording; a general line, not a tested observation).
- No mill or place of manufacture is given in the Hypebeast article.
