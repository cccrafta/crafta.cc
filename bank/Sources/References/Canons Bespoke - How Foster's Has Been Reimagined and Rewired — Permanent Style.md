## Summary
June 2025 profile of Canons, successor to Foster & Son bespoke.

## Key Claims
- Foster & Son made shoes in London's West End from 1840; Jermyn Street lease ended 2020; the Northampton factory (2018, Japanese investors) failed as shoes were priced at GBP 800 when they should have been over GBP 2,000.
- 2022: managing director Simon Bolzoni bought the company with his own money and renamed it Canons (launched 2023).
- Workshop at 6 Northampton Street, Islington; open-plan with customers' wooden lasts along the front half; about 20-strong team, the rest outworkers (normal for English shoemakers).
- Output is twice Foster's Jermyn Street level; delivery down from about 18 months to nine; bespoke from GBP 4,750 plus VAT.
- Colours its own leathers and develops suede colours with tanneries; brought despatch-box production in-house in 2018. Bolzoni trained significantly under last maker Terry Moore.
