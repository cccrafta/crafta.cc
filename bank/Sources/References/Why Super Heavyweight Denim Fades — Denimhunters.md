## Summary
Denimhunters explainer on how raw denim fades, including how weight changes the fade pattern, read as a summary. Together with two Heddels Fade Friday examples (snippet level), it gives what sources state about how raw selvedge denim jackets age.

## Key Claims
- Raw denim jackets fade more slowly than jeans (Heddels and Denimhunters both say this).
- Fade patterns appear where denim creases, bends and folds; elbows are the main fade area on jackets.
- Cuffs whiten at the edges.
- Lighter denim creases sharply; very heavy denim (20oz+) fades in 3D along rolled folds.
- Reactive-dyed denim does not fade; sulfur black breaks to grey.
- Heddels example (snippet level): a Burro jacket after 14 years shows elbow honeycombs and dark spots under the collar.
- Heddels example (snippet level): a Jean Shop jacket after 7+ years shows elbow combs (https://www.heddels.com/2015/01/fade-friday-jean-shop-denim-jacket-7-years-1-soak-0-washes/).

## Notes
- These are raw selvedge jackets, not Barbour's denim. Barbour's dye type (raw versus pre-washed) is unknown, so do NOT apply any of this to a Barbour jacket.
- Denimhunters read as summary; Heddels pieces snippets. A second Denimhunters piece (https://denimhunters.com/redline-rally/) is cited in the report but not extracted here.
