## Summary
ABC News Australia (2024-11-21) on the tabi, with quotes from Japanese makers. Via WebFetch summary.

## Key Claims
- Tabi thought to reach Japan from China in the 5th century as socks; 15th-century single-piece leather version; cotton later.
- 1922: Shojiro Ishibashi (later Bridgestone founder) reinforced soles with rubber and metal latches, making the jika-tabi.
- Jika-tabi worn by construction workers and at summer festivals with baggy trousers; wearers say they feel the wind and keep balance at height; work versions often steel-toed, about $50.
- Akira Namidome, Marugo (Kurashiki, over 100 years): fewer people take part in festivals; Western popularity helps keep the tradition alive; Marugo launched a fashion line.
- Maki Hashimoto, SOU SOU: half of clientele overseas; a dozen tabi makers once, now three major ones.
- Martin Margiela first saw tabi on construction workers in Japan; viral drivers: celebrities (Zendaya, Pedro Pascal, Dua Lipa), Vogue India "shoes of the year" 2024, TikTok story of $1,800 tabis stolen on a Tinder date; most popular style $1,430.
