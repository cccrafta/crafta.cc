## Summary
Maker's account. T1 for team, site and sourcing principles; gives no production volumes or named mills.

## Key Claims
- Han Ates, a second-generation Londoner of Turkish Kurdish ancestry, ran his own tailored-garment factory on Blackhorse Lane for 20+ years before returning to textiles in 2016.
- The atelier is in Blackhorse Lane, E17 Walthamstow, in the same building as Han's earlier factory; 20% of floor space is pledged to a charity that supports local craftsmen.
- The maker states no UK or London industry exists for spinning and weaving denim, so cloth is imported 'as locally as possible' and suppliers will be published.
- Named staff roles cover sampling, made-to-measure, repair and alterations (Wilbur is credited with denim repair and hemming); a wash R&D lab is being set up for designers and students.
- An allotment about 15 minutes' walk away runs community indigo workshops.
