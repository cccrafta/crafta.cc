## Summary
German Wikipedia (T4: useful for leads, not as sole source). Records the contract and factory; only some claims carry footnotes. Read via WebFetch (a model summary of the page), so second-hand.

## Key Claims
- "Adidas erhielt 1982 den Auftrag zur Produktion des Modells" (adidas received the contract to produce the indoor model in 1982); this sentence has no footnote of its own.
- The white indoor model ("Halle") resembles the adidas Samba; adidas had it made at the ZDA Partizanske plant in Czechoslovakia (footnoted to Hauke Sieverts, The Heritage Post no. 54, 2025).
- About half a million pairs were produced up to 1989 (footnoted to Luke Leitch, Wall Street Journal, 10 July 2015); since the 1990s large quantities were sold as surplus.
- A second, blue outdoor model ("Gelaende") resembles the Puma Easy Rider II (shared cylindrical rubber studs and spherical-cap dimples on the midsole); the article does not say Puma made it.
- Margiela released a luxury replica; adidas reissued the shoe as BW Army.
