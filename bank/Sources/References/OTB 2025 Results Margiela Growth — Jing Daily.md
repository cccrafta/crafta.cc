## Summary
Short results report (2026-02-18), read directly via curl. Independent second source for the FashionNetwork OTB numbers, and the only one found for the wholesale target.

## Key Claims
- OTB 2025 revenue €1.7 billion, down 4.8% at constant exchange rates; EBITDA €237.3 million, 15.1% margin on net sales.
- Margiela led brand performance with sales growth of 8.4%; Diesel reached its highest profitability in a decade.
- Wholesale declined nearly 15%; OTB "aims to reduce wholesale to 25-30% of total turnover over time."
- The group opened 49 stores, closed 58 and relocated others, investing €64 million in retail expansion and rationalisation; the Chinese market slowed while North America grew 5.9%.
