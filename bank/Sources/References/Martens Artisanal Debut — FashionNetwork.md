## Summary
Dissenting review of the July 2025 Artisanal debut, read directly via curl. The article carries no date in its text beyond "Wednesday night"; 9 July 2025 was a Wednesday and matches WWD's provisional couture schedule.

## Key Claims
- Martens' first Margiela Artisanal show took place on a Wednesday night in a quiet Paris couture season, staged in 104, a north Paris art and theatre space where Martin Margiela had also staged shows.
- Every model's head was covered by a mask, skullcap or copper pot; the reviewer found this "tired and repetitive", and models could barely move their legs in the heavier metallic dresses.
- Praised: feathered looks, tailored coats in Renaissance-style game-bird prints, crepe and silk evening dresses finished with small clouds of lace.
- Opening looks were gowns and masks in scrunched-up plastic; the house's signature painted jeans appeared covered in muddy plastic.
- Verdict: "a pale shadow" of Galliano's final couture show, though Martens "still seems a good fit for Margiela."
