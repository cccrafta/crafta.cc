## Key Claims
- Founded 1897 by David Nelken (a British entrepreneur) in Saxony; vulcanised fibreboard patented 1901 and still used in all cases; 'yet to be imitated successfully' (maker's claim).
- Production is handmade in England using some Victorian-era methods and machinery; page does not name the Hertfordshire factory or count processes.
- 1985: Malcolm Payton acquired the company after Henry Nelken's death.
- Cases carried by Queen Elizabeth II (1947 honeymoon), Hillary (1953); BOAC official case (1960).
