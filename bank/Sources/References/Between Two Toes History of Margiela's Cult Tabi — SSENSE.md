## Summary
SSENSE editorial by Arabelle Sicardi (undated on the page). Gives the most detail on the first Tabi and how it was made. Read via WebFetch (a model summary of the page), so second-hand.

## Key Claims
- The Tabi was on the first model of the debut show "in 1988", at Café de la Gare in Paris; the page calls the second show "Fall/Winter 1989", which implies the first show was the Spring/Summer 1989 season (inference; the page never names the first season).
- "No cobbler would take his tabi design on": the split toe was too radical for traditional workshops. Antwerp retailer Geert Bruloot introduced Margiela to an Italian craftsman, Mr. Zagato, who made the first boots.
- Margiela wanted "the illusion of a bare foot resting on a heel"; the heel is chunky from the side and narrow from the front; the clasps up the inside nod to the Japanese original.
- In the finale models wore white lab coats and shoes dipped in red paint; Margiela's reason: "I thought the audience should notice the new footwear. And what would be more evident than its footprint?"
- The painted runway cloth was reused for the next show as a waistcoat held together with brown scotch tape.
