## Summary
Heddels history of the German Army Trainer (Aug 2026, Kadeem Roper). Read via WebFetch summary of partly garbled text, so second-hand.

## Key Claims
- Known as Bundeswehr Sportschuh, Hallenschuh or BW Sport; classic: white leather upper, grey suede toe reinforcement, gum-rubber sole.
- In the 1980s hundreds of thousands of pairs were made, mainly in Slovakia.
- Designer is unresolved: Adidas says it made BW Sport trainers in the 1980s and 90s (sole resembles the Samba's); Bundeswehr Museum records reportedly point to a Puma prototype, which Puma denies; the Bundeswehr may have written the spec and used several contractors.
- Surplus pairs were cheap and carried little overt military baggage; by the 1990s a cult following existed across Europe.
- Margiela reportedly found BW Sports in Austria, bought dozens, and showed refurbished originals in the SS1999 Artisanal presentation; the house made its own Replica in the early-to-mid 2000s when vintage supply ran short.
- Contemporary alternatives: Adidas BW Army $115; Allevol GAT $475 (Horween Chromexcel); Adnitt (Crown Northampton) $327; Wallace Mercantile $289.
- Old surplus pairs can have unstable soles.
