## Summary
Grenfell's statements on its east London factory and the invention of Grenfell Cloth.

## Key Claims
- Garments are made in Grenfell's own factory in east London (Lea Bridge, E17).
- Grenfell Cloth: tightly woven Egyptian cotton, windproof and breathable, uncoated; developed at Lodge Mill, Burnley by Walter Haythornthwaite after Sir Wilfred Grenfell's 1922 talk, 1923.
- Everest expeditions of 1933 and 1936 used the cloth (see existing Haythornthwaite note).
- Who weaves the cloth today is not stated. [gap]
