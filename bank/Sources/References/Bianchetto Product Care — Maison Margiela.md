## Summary
The care page is the only house text found that describes how the paint is applied and how it ages. Read via WebFetch (a model summary of the page), so second-hand.

## Key Claims
- Bianchetto is a handmade technique "part of the DNA of the house"; the paint is applied to objects and clothes "in the same way as walls are painted".
- The effect "indicates the passage of time and recalls purity".
- Cracking is intended: "the hand-made paint can crackle with use".
- Care: garments with the effect should be washed inside out; shoes and bags with the paint "should not be washed".
- Not stated on the page: paint chemistry, heat setting, number of coats, drying.
