## Summary
V&A catalogue record (created 5 Aug 2025) for a Derby-shoe Tabi; the object is later than the debut, but the record text states the museum's account of the first show. Read via WebFetch (a model summary of the page), so second-hand.

## Key Claims
- "The Tabi shoe was first shown by the house in its debut catwalk show in 1988"; the house itself was founded in 1988. The record never names 1989 or Spring/Summer 1989.
- The debut finale showed calf-length black leather Tabi boots with soles dipped in red ink, leaving hoof-like prints on the canvas floor; the canvas later became a waistcoat held with masking tape.
- The design came from Margiela's trip to Japan, where the split-toe tabi sock dates to the 15th century; Margiela, short of money, showed the same Tabi shoes season after season.
- The V&A object itself is a Derby split-toe shoe, leather and rubber, Paris, dated 2000-2015 and probably from the Galliano period (2014-2024); the colour is given as brown in one field and black in another.
