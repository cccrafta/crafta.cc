## Summary
Exhibition page for the Palais Galliera retrospective (3 March to 15 July 2018, curator Alexandre Samson). Museum evidence for how the house's output is periodised. Read via WebFetch (a model summary of the page), so second-hand.

## Key Claims
- The retrospective follows Margiela's career "from spring-summer 1989 to spring-summer 2009"; the page gives no date for the first show.
- Describes the "cloven" shoe inspired by traditional Japanese tabis, with the big toe set apart.
- Notes Margiela's use of white "in a multitude of shades" and a plain white label.
- Describes the Artisanal collection as made from vintage garments and recovered materials, one-of-a-kind hand-sewn pieces. Bianchetto is not mentioned.
- Over 100 silhouettes, runway videos, house archives and installations.
