## Summary
The maker's own account of its Walthamstow denim factory, its founder and its production approach. T1 for who makes what and where.

## Key Claims
- Founded 2016 by Bilgehan "Han" Ates, a tailor who had run London factories for about 30 years.
- Jeans are made on site in a 1920s Walthamstow (E17) factory building that Ates has owned since 1995.
- Denim comes from Japanese selvedge, Italian and Turkish mills; Kurabo (Japan) is named on the brand's channels.
- Offers lifetime free repairs and a Wash Lab for finishing.
