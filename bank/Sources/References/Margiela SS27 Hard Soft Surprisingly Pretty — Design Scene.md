## Summary
Mixed-positive SS27 review (published 2026-09-30), read directly via curl. The writer credits the idea more than the result.

## Key Claims
- "The collection takes its starting point from the traditional organization of Parisian ateliers. Tailoring handles structure, while Flou deals with dressmaking and draping"; Martens "deliberately disrupts that separation."
- Trench coats receive rigid horizontal or vertical panels; jackets and waistcoats turn inward, hiding closures while fortified creases keep the evidence of the altered position; enormous reinforced darts on fluid dresses.
- Verdict: "more convinced by the proposition than by much of what it produced"; volumes "surprisingly controlled" next to FW26 (jersey, leather, metal wire, antique dresses, wax, porcelain, organza).
- Florals, polka dots and bright colour pull attention from construction; Mark Colle's flower installation is described as an homage to Raf Simons' 2012 flower-filled couture debut.
