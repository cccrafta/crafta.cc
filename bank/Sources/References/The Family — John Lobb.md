## Summary
London family firm's own page (distinct from Hermes-owned John Lobb).

## Key Claims
- John Lobb Ltd, 9 St James's Street SW1A 1EF, 'the original British bootmaker since 1849', run by one family for more than 170 years.
- Founder John Lobb (1829-1895) was a Cornish farm boy who won awards at Victorian international exhibitions and held a Royal Warrant from Edward, Prince of Wales.
- William Lobb (1902-1963) created the Double Monk; John Hunter Lobb (b. 1940) is chairman and managing director and a highly influential last maker.
- Fifth generation: William John Lobb (director 2002), Jonathan Hunter Lobb (Director of Production), Nicholas David Lobb (strategy). Apprentices rotate through finishing, closing, clicking, making and last making.
- The page does not say where current production takes place.
