## Summary
Early Permanent Style visit to Albam covering product choices and why English production costs more.

## Key Claims
- Ventile Alpine jacket with taped seams; Ventile about £20/m versus £1-2/m for typical hiking fabrics.
- RiRi zips; shirts made in runs of about 70 per colour.
- Trousers cut by a trouser cutter, with a higher darted waist.
- Fisherman's cagoule iterated season by season.
- English production costs more than overseas.
