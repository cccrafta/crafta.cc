## Summary
Fred Perry's own 70th-anniversary piece (October 2022) on the 1952 shirt and how it moved from the tennis court to mod, skinhead, rude boy and rave wardrobes. A maker source for the 1952 origin and the leisure/subculture shift; contributors' recollections are first-person memory.

## Key Claims
- "The first Fred Perry Shirt was worn in 1952 - an all-white shirt with a Laurel Wreath on the left side."
- About ten years after the original, the brand added "Twin Tipping" to the collar and released more colours.
- Contributor recollections: mod revival wearers "religiously wore different coloured Fred Perry polo shirts" (first one bought 1978, Oxford Street); worn with Sta-Prest trousers, braces and DMs.
- "Mods, Skinheads, Rude Boys and Ravers had their differences" but the shirt is described as "a uniform that unites the working class and music scenes of these periods."
- Not on this page: Tibby Wegner, the M3 model name, Wimbledon, or fabric.
