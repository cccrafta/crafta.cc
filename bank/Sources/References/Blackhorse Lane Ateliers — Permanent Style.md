## Summary
Simon Crompton's review of Blackhorse Lane jeans, covering construction choices and how the price was held down.

## Key Claims
- Seams are felled rather than overlocked.
- One-piece fly taken from an 1896 jeans pattern found by Han Ates.
- Waistband chain-stitch integrates the back patch and ends on a rivet; belt loops are tacked to seams; pocket bags are heavier.
- The price was kept down by using Turkish denim.
- Method reported: the team analysed a set of well-regarded jeans (Rudy Budhdeo of Son of a Stag's top 12) before drafting.
