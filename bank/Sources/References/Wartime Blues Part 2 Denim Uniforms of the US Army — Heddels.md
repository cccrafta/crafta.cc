## Summary
Heddels history of US Army denim work clothing, used here to show that denim predates the jungle jacket as an Army work cloth but was never the jungle jacket's cloth. Fetch summary only.

## Key Claims
- The US Army used blue denim for work clothing from 1908-09.
- The M1940 denim coat was 100% cotton, with a five-button placket, two large waist patch pockets and a point collar.
- The M1940 denim coat replaced a pullover jumper that reportedly tore down the middle.
- In 1941 the Army moved to olive HBT, judged better for tropical theatres.
- Denim therefore predates the jungle jacket as a US Army work cloth, but was never the jungle jacket's cloth (the report's synthesis of items from this article and the Warehouse article).
