## Summary
7 September 2020 guide by Simon Crompton. T2 on what the shop carries and why; he discloses paid styling work for Clutch's Instagram.

## Key Claims
- Clutch Cafe, Fitzrovia, was set up by the Japanese magazine Clutch as a UK outlet for brands the magazine covers; cafe upstairs; one of a handful of London shops Crompton regularly recommends to visitors.
- Named brands: Buzz Rickson (faithful recreations; duffle coat wool 'bulletproof'), The Real McCoy's, Himel Bros (Canadian horsehide), Warehouse, Cushman, Jelado, Coherence, Stevenson Overall.
- Japanese vintage-style tees are circular-knitted with no side seam, hence boxy and short; Crompton finds Full Count denim narrow in the thigh and points to Blackhorse Lane or Levi's made-to-measure.
- Epperson Mountaineering and Rocky Mountain Featherbed are the climbing-inspired lines, 'a little synthetic' for his taste.
- Complaint: 'too much in there'; verdict: 'full of treasures'.
