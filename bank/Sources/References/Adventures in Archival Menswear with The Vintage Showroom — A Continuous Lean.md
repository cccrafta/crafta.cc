## Summary
6 January 2016 blog piece on the archive and its second book. Corroborates founders and shop opening; blog-grade.

## Key Claims
- Douglas Gunn and Roy Luckett run the archive: 'thousands upon thousands of mint condition garments and accessories' tracing modern menswear; they opened a Covent Garden shop in 2009.
- Books: Vintage Menswear: A Collection from the Vintage Showroom (2012) and The Vintage Showroom: An Archive of Menswear (Laurence King), with four sections: Aviation & Motorsports, Tailoring & Dress Uniforms, Utility & Denim, Sportswear & Weatherwear.
- Archive pieces include a bearskin bomber jacket, fur-lined flying trousers, the original US Navy peacoat and British Antarctic Survey waterproofs.
- Writer's favourite piece: a dead-stock 1970s Belstaff parka and mittens set designed in collaboration with the mountaineer Chris Bonington.
