## Summary
Maker's own story. T1 for founding and production site; names no tannery.

## Key Claims
- Started as The General Leather Company, founded in 1971 in London by Savile Row graduate Alan Sprooles and Peter Goodall.
- Pauline Harris became director in 2015 (35 years in leather tailoring, 20 at General Leather) and renamed it for Cromford, Derbyshire, 'birthplace of Britain's industrial revolution'.
- Garments are made at its own atelier at 56 Chiltern Street, Marylebone.
- It produced garments for Margaret Howell and Mr Porter, makes leather and suede pieces for film and TV, and made coats for the Queen.
- Ready-to-wear is designed by Harris in sizes S-2XL; the range can be made to measure and bespoke.
