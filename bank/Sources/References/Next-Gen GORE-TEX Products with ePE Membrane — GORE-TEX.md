## Key Claims
- The membrane is "ePE (expanded polyethylene)", called "a key milestone" by Gore; the laminate is described as "PFAS-free* with a reduced carbon footprint**".
- Footnote *: "Made without intentionally added per- and polyfluorinated substances; may contain trace amounts." The PFAS-free claim covers the laminate including its durable water repellent (DWR) treatment.
- Footnote **: the reduced carbon footprint is "through the laminate's innovative membrane and select textiles (per Higg MSI)", via "a new lower-mass membrane".
- Performance wording is qualitative only: "Durably Waterproof", "Very Breathable", "Totally Windproof"; no hydrostatic head, Ret or MVTR figures on the page.
- The membrane is paired with "carefully selected textiles including recycled, solution or undyed textiles".
- Awards named: GearJunkie 2023 Gear of the Year and The Great Outdoors 2024 Gear Award for Best Innovation (Brand).
