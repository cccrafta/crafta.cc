## Summary
Hypebeast report on the FW26 Shanghai show (published 2026-04-01), read directly via curl. Independent corroboration of Daily Front Row's construction details, in less depth.

## Key Claims
- The show presented "couture" Artisanal pieces alongside global ready-to-wear, "a nostalgic nod to the house's founding years".
- Flea-market-found tailoring archetypes were altered: tailcoats "chopped or painted with bianchetto white paint", double-breasted jackets fused with second-skin jersey.
- "Garment memory": unwearable vintage dresses glued to a fabric base and ripped off to leave an imprint; a six-metre Edwardian dress restored and its layers moulded together with beeswax.
- Dresses made from actual shattered porcelain, heavy beadwork and "impossible draping"; every look paired with an Artisanal mask.
- Accessories: "Level Cut-Out" boots, the men's "Float" shoe (upper resting on a much smaller sole), "Tabi-claw" boots with a stiletto heel.
