## Summary
Museum's own service page. T1 for how to see fashion and textile objects in person.

## Key Claims
- Free service: pick up to 5 objects from the same venue via Explore the Collections and book an appointment of 1-4 hours; the first 15 minutes are a safety briefing.
- Appointments take place at V&A South Kensington, V&A East Storehouse (Hackney Wick) or the David Bowie Centre in the Storehouse building.
- 16 and over can use it unaccompanied; one guest allowed.
- Textile examples listed include Spitalfields silk shoes (T.446&A-1913) and Lucienne Day textiles.
