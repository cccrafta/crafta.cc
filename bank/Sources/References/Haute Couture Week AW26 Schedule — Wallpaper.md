## Summary
Preview of the July 2026 couture week, read directly via curl. Relevant because it names Margiela only for an auction, not a show.

## Key Claims
- Haute Couture Week A/W 2026 ran 6 to 9 July in Paris; the FHCM page confirms Monday 6 to Thursday 9 July 2026.
- Debuts highlighted: Pierpaolo Piccioli at Balenciaga and Duran Lantink at Jean Paul Gaultier on Wednesday 8 July; Olivier Theyskens relaunching with Boloria.
- The only Margiela item in the preview is an auction on 9 July at 2 p.m. in Paris of 200 pieces from Martin Margiela's personal collection, not a house show.
- Wallpaper notes couture week takes place each year in January and June or July.
