## Summary
Group history page; thin on production.

## Key Claims
- Swaine founded 1750 as a whip maker; Brigg began with plumassier Charles Brigg moving into umbrellas (walking sticks from 1838); Herbert Johnson founded 1889 as hatmaker with a royal warrant from Edward VII in 1901.
- Brigg merged with Swaine & Adeney; Swaine Adeney Brigg acquired Herbert Johnson in 1996.
- Footer says 'Made in the UK'; mentions a workshop and atelier on New Bond Street and offers servicing and restoration; does not say where each product is made.
