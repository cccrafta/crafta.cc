## Summary
FashionUnited, 16 February 2026. Read as page text via curl. Trade report of the company's announcement.

## Key Claims
- The Ortviken plant in Sundsvall is scheduled to resume making recycled pulp in the fourth quarter of 2026, with a stated aim of 60,000 tonnes a year of Circulose.
- Renewcell, acquired by Altor in June 2024, was renamed Circulose; the company dropped open-market sales for a partner-led model tied to direct volume commitments from brands.
- Eleven brands had committed as of the announcement; December 2025 additions were Bestseller, John Lewis, C&A, Filippa K, Reformation, Faherty, Bobo Choses and Zero, joining H&M Group, Mango and Marks & Spencer.
- CEO Jonatan Janmark: the restart was aligned with confirmed demand rather than spot-market sales.
