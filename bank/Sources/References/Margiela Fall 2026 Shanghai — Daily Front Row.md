## Summary
Review of Margiela's Fall 2026 show in Shanghai (published 2026-04-02), a hybrid of Artisanal and ready-to-wear. Describes construction of several signature pieces. Read via WebFetch summary, so second-hand.

## Key Claims
- Opening gown imitates porcelain by layering glass organza, with no traditional waist seams and no set-in sleeves.
- Ceramics dress: about 500 ceramic pieces on an organza base, 300+ hours; toiled in calico, ceramics fired to the pattern pieces, glazed, fired again, broken by hand, rounded off and attached.
- Bianchetto: an Edwardian tailcoat was painted white, baked and cracked open.
- Tuxedo variant bonded to a layer of second-skin jersey; an upside-down shawl collar with raw edges.
- Gold star gown: 150,000+ hand-applied stars, 34 artisans, 2,975 hours.
- Waistcoat from a 19th-century tapestry that once covered a canapé sofa found in Chartres; dress from vintage crochet shawls.
- Finale gown: 5 m flea-market painting, stabilised by restorers for a week, draped over a double corset without being cut so it can be removed and hung.
