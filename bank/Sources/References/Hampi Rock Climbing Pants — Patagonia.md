## Summary
Patagonia's climbing-specific hemp-blend pant, the climbing counterpart to its Iron Forge work line. Only a search-result summary of the EU page and retailer listings was readable, hence stub; re-fetch the Patagonia page to confirm wording.

## Key Claims
- Women's fabric: 53% organic hemp, 44% recycled polyester, 3% elastane (per Patagonia EU / REI / Worn Wear listings via search summary).
- Patterned for climbing movement with a gusset and articulated knees for high-stepping, stemming and heel hooking.
- Soft internal elastic waistband that lies flat under a harness; right-side pocket for a bouldering brush; tapered lower leg with half-elastic cuffs; 28 in inseam (women's).
- Fair Trade Certified sewing.
- Men's listings (retailers only) describe an articulated, gusseted pattern; leg shape and waist system vary by model year.
- Shows that hemp, a workwear-adjacent fibre, is already used in climbing pants, but blended with polyester/elastane rather than as canvas.
