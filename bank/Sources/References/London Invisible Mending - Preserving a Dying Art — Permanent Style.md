## Summary
August 2024 piece by Manish Puri.

## Key Claims
- Michael Norman runs London Invisible Mending as a second business; he found skilled weavers by calling two old colleagues who still practised the technique.
- Invisible (French) reweaving rebuilds warp and weft with threads harvested from internal seams or hems; woven fabrics only, not synthetics; about 75% of repairs are moth holes.
- A cigarette-sized hole costs from GBP 90 and takes about six hours; turnaround about three weeks; drop-off at Graham Browne, 4 Bow Lane, or collection across London.
- Context: cheaper ready-to-wear and synthetics made repair uneconomical; a US reweaver suspended service when its weaver retired. Alternatives: British Invisible Mending Service (Thayer Street), The Valet (Piccadilly Arcade).
