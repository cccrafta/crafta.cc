## Summary
Maker's founders page.

## Key Claims
- Deborah Carre and James Ducker met as handsewn-shoemaking apprentices under master shoemaker Paul Wilson; joined forces as Carreducker in December 2004.
- James apprenticed at John Lobb in St James's and became a full shoemaker there; also taught handsewn shoemaking at Cordwainers College. Deborah won a QEST scholarship.
- Studio at Unit 1.20 Oxo Tower Wharf, Bargehouse Street, London SE1 9PH.
- Menu lists classes (shoemaking, closing, pattern cutting) and made-to-order boots and clogs; the page itself does not describe a repair service.
