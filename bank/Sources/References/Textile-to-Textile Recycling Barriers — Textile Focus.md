## Summary
Textile Focus (2026-10-17) summarising the Textiles Intelligence report 'Textile-to-textile recycling: the business case'. Read via WebFetch summary.

## Key Claims
- Under 1% of global fibre production comes from recycled pre- and post-consumer textiles; most recycled fibre comes from plastic bottles.
- About 11% of post-consumer textile waste was collected and sorted into streams suitable for recycling last year.
- Recycled polyester is estimated at 2.6 times the cost of virgin polyester.
- Europe reaching 15% textile-to-textile recycling by 2035 would need capex of EUR 8–11 bn and annual opex of EUR 5–6.5 bn (report estimate).
- National textile EPR schemes should be operational by April 2028 under the existing timetable; ESPR adds pressure.
- Named developers: Ambercycle, Circ, Circulose, Syre. Report concludes scale needs investment, feedstock, demand and policy, not only technology.
