## Summary
Heddels piece on a Buzz Rickson's tropical combat jacket release. For Crafta it documents that a Japanese reproduction maker copies the first-pattern Vietnam jacket, and it supports the first-pattern feature description.

## Key Claims
- Buzz Rickson's model BR12247 copies the first-pattern Tropical Combat Jacket (the 1963-64 model).
- It is made in 5.5-oz cotton (OG-107 weight), made in Japan.
- The first-pattern features it reproduces: two slanted chest bellows pockets and two lower pockets, exposed pocket buttons, epaulets, waist take-up tabs, pen pockets behind the chest pockets, inner gas flap (feature list is corroborated by VietnamGear and Rare Gear USA).

## Notes
- The report records no Bronson Shop (7.5 oz) or Clutch Cafe (BR 5.5 oz) detail, so none is recorded here; any such retailer figures need to be sourced separately.
