## Summary
Goldwin press release via PR Newswire, 24 March 2023 (T1). Read as page text via curl.

## Key Claims
- Five brands (The North Face, Goldwin, nanamica, The North Face Purple Label, Woolrich) launched Fall/Winter 2023 products with Brewed Protein™ fibre; joint development with Spiber began in 2015.
- The first product was The North Face's Planetary Equilibrium Tee (August 2019); all earlier products were sold in limited quantities by lottery because of limited fibre.
- Mass production of the polymer at Spiber's Thailand plant began in early 2022.
- "We have set an activity goal of shifting 10% of our newly developed products to those using Brewed Protein™️ by 2030."
- The North Face Nuptse Jacket listing: outer 60% Brewed Protein fibre, 25% wool, 15% nylon, with a PTFE waterproof/breathable laminate on the reverse layer.
