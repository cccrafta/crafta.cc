## Summary
The house's own definition and origin of Bianchetto. T1 for the maker's account. Read via WebFetch (a model summary of the page), so second-hand.

## Key Claims
- Defined as "our white overpaint technique that leaves visible brush strokes and transforms surface into a blank canvas", an extension of white as a house code.
- Introduced in 1989: Martin Margiela "first overpainted a collection of Salvation Army furniture for the Maison's design studio"; the same year the treatment was extended to the Tabi shoe. This is the house's origin, not the "first show" origin repeated in press coverage.
- A matt white was chosen "to record the story of each object as it naturally marks and shows signs of use"; because it goes on garments, objects and interiors it "will naturally crack and fade over time".
- Used today on bags, Replica Sneakers, Tabi and ready-to-wear.
- Links the paint to the blouse blanche (white work coat) as a neutral framework.
