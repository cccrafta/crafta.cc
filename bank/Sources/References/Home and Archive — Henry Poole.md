## Summary
Maker's homepage: workroom, archive ledgers and heritage claims.

## Key Claims
- Every item is handmade by master craftsmen at the Savile Row premises (15 Savile Row, W1S 3PJ), from a pattern cut for the individual client.
- The archive room holds historic customer ledgers 'dating from 1846'.
- James Poole began by making military uniforms during the Napoleonic Wars; his son Henry is regarded as a founding figure of Savile Row, and the family has run the business across generations.
- Over 6,000 fabric choices; trunk shows in Europe, the Americas and Japan.
- Has made a Travel Trench coat with GORE-TEX, inspired by historic garments in its archives.
- The page gives no founding year; it says 'over 200 years' (1806 comes from other sources).
