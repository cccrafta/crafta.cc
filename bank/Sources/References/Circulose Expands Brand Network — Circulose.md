## Summary
Circulose media release, 24 June 2026 (T1). Read as page text via curl. Announces partnerships, not purchase volumes; no financial terms.

## Key Claims
- Four new brand partnerships announced 24 June 2026: EILEEN FISHER, Marimekko, Samsøe Samsøe and King Louie, joining H&M Group, Mango, BESTSELLER, Reformation and Marks & Spencer "among others".
- CIRCULOSE® is described as "a regenerated material made from 100% discarded textiles"; Circulose is owned by Altor and says it operates the world's first commercial-scale chemical textile recycling facility in Sundsvall, Sweden.
- Commercial strategy: "close brand collaboration, a licensing-based model, and dedicated implementation support". No volumes or prices stated.
- The pulp is a dissolving pulp from end-of-life cotton-rich textiles that fibre makers turn into viscose and lyocell, replacing virgin forest-based inputs.
