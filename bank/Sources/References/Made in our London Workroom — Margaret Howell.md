## Summary
Maker's page on its shirtmaking. T1 for the workroom, stitch and time figures.

## Key Claims
- The London Workroom is newly opened in Harringay, North London; the first Workroom was set up in 1973 in Blackheath, South London, after several years of making shirts from her bedroom.
- A team of cutters, machinists and finishers makes each shirt; buttons are horn or mother-of-pearl; shirts are hand-pressed before delivery.
- A single shirt can contain 12,000 stitches (six stitches per cm, up to 20 m of seaming) and takes 3-4 hours.
- The team has over 175 years of combined experience.
