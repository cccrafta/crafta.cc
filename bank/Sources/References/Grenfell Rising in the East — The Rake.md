## Summary
Feature on Mo Azam and the Grenfell factory in east London. Brand-sourced quotes.

## Key Claims
- Managing Director Mo Azam's family has run London outerwear factories since 1984 (Whitechapel, then Bow).
- Those factories made coats for Katharine Hamnett, Casely-Hayford, Burberry (about 400 a week), Aquascutum and Daks.
- Grenfell's current factory is a former Met Police forensics lab in Lea Bridge, E17.
- Everest 1953 and Admiral Byrd use claims come via Azam; [single-source, brand-sourced].
