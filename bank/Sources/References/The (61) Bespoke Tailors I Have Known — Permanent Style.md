## Summary
Crompton's overview of London bespoke houses (Oct 2024); page read to first 100,000 characters only.

## Key Claims
- Henry Poole: strong English cut with less shoulder and chest padding than Huntsman, more flexible; described as the founder of Savile Row.
- Dege & Skinner: traditional, military-influenced, strong-shouldered and longer; family owned and 'remarkably consistent'.
- Anderson & Sheppard: drape cut, soft shoulders, fuller chest and back, closely cut waist; among the cheaper Row houses.
- Timothy Everest no longer offers bespoke; Edward Sexton's house carries on after his death with part of an offshore service made in China.
- Whitcomb & Shaftesbury cut in London with much making done in a workshop they own and trained in India (out of scope for Crafta: Indian production).
