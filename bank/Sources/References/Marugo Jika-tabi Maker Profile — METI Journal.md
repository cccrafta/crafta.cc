## Summary
Japanese Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) Journal profile of Marugo (11 Jul 2022), with quotes from chairman Fujiki Shigehiko and president Fukuda Masahiko. Read in full via curl, translated here; T1/T2 (government feature built on maker interviews).

## Key Claims
- Marugo was founded in 1919; founder Fujiki Itaro devised jika-tabi by gluing rubber to the sole of tabi. Chairman Fujiki: "Converse started making rubber-soled sneakers in 1917. It is not so much that Marugo developed jika-tabi as that using rubber for the soles of footwear began to spread simultaneously around the world" (translation).
- Marugo's jika-tabi protected workers in farming, civil engineering and construction; at festivals such as Sapporo's YOSAKOI Soran (from 1992) its festival tabi hold about 60% share. Demand for jika-tabi itself "gradually tapered".
- In response Marugo launched the colourful ASSABOOTS in 2009 in Europe, answering overseas users' wish for fashionable jika-tabi, and in Japan from 2012; in 2014 it developed Tabirira, which drops the metal clasps (kohaze).
- Performers in the 2021 Tokyo Olympics opening ceremony pictograms wore Marugo tabi shoes; Marugo also made the marathon tabi for the 2019 NHK drama Idaten.
- Marugo opened MARUGO TOKYO (Aug 2019) and MARUGO KURASHIKI (Apr 2022); turnover 4.7 billion yen; address Chayamachi, Kurashiki.
- President Fukuda says split-toe shoes give a "barefoot feel", help balance and reduce falls; the claims are the maker's.
