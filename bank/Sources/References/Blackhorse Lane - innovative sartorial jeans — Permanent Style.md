## Summary
14 February 2018 review by Simon Crompton. T2 for construction detail; the fit and price data are dated.

## Key Claims
- Fly is cut from a single strip (Levi's 501s use two stitched pieces, a weak point); the idea came from an 1896 jeans pattern Han found.
- Felled stitching replaces the 'big yellow overlock' of Momotaro-type jeans; chain stitching runs round the whole waistband, belt loops are tacked to seams, pocket bags are heavier and rear pockets are lined at the bottom.
- Design started when BLA asked Rudy Budhdeo of Son of a Stag for his top 12 jeans, analysed them and combined the best features; Han ran London garment manufacturing for 30 years, mostly tailored garments not workwear.
- Turkish-woven denim keeps cost below Japanese; the reviewer's 18oz NW1 cost 290 GBP, E5 started at 155 GBP.
- BLA offers free repairs, 'They might never sell you a second pair'.
