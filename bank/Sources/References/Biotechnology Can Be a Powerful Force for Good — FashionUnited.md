## Summary
FashionUnited written Q&A with Spiber, August 2025 (T2 channel, maker answers). Read as page text via curl.

## Key Claims
- Spiber has collaborated with 45 brands on 193 products to date, "over 80 companies" including mills have worked with the fibre, and 45 brands have launched "more than 190 products".
- Available forms: staple fibre, tops/sliver and spun yarn, with a silk-like sheen; partners highlight softness.
- Spiber says end-of-life breakdown into feedstock has been demonstrated at lab scale only.
- Spiber claims its fibre is the only man-made protein fibre to be industrialised (maker claim).
