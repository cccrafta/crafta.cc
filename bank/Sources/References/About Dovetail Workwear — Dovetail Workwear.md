## Summary
Maker page for a Portland women's workwear brand whose Maven pants add a gusset and double-front panels to canvas trades pants. Summary via WebFetch plus search results; founding year disputed between sources.

## Key Claims
- Founded in 2018 per the About page by landscapers Kate Day and Kyle Marie Summers (also cited as Kyle Marie Begley) with apparel developer Sara DeLuca; an Amazon brand blurb says 2015.
- Origin: women landscaping and in trades found jeans failed under physical work, and women's workwear fit badly; the Maven pant was the first product.
- Stated process: patterns drafted from scratch for women's proportions rather than adjusting men's, with input from thousands of working women; "Fit is our superpower."
- Maven X features (search results): contoured mid-rise waist, 10 pockets, reinforced front pockets, hidden elastic waistband, crotch gusset, double-front leg panels, hammer loop.
- Page cites military-grade fibres, waterless dyeing and recycled materials, and a "Rewear + Repair" collection.
- Each pocket's size and placement is stated to be tested.
- Relevance: a workwear brand independently arrives at the gusseted crotch that climbing pants use; the gusset is a shared element.
