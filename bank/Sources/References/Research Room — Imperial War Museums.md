## Summary
Museum's own access page. Does not say uniforms can be viewed; ask the team.

## Key Claims
- Research Room is on the second floor of IWM London (Lambeth Road), free, 10:00-16:30 Monday-Friday; appointments essential.
- Tuesday-Friday for documents, photographs, library; Monday is reserved for 3D objects or artwork, booked by emailing researchroom@iwm.org.uk, for up to four researchers.
- Standard requests need at least 7 days; Monday 3D-object requests needing a condition check 'may take at least a month'.
- The page does not mention uniforms; whether a uniform qualifies as a 3D object is not stated.
