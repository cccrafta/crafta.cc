## Summary
Maker's craft page. Clarifies that hats are finished in the shop but made by outside manufacturers; the page gives no 1849 bowler date.

## Key Claims
- Shop dates to 1676; hats are 'hand-prepared in our London shop' at 6 St James's Street by one of its Hatters.
- It uses outside manufacturers in Spain, Belgium and the UK, and mills in the UK and Ireland for wool and cashmere; couture pieces are handcrafted in store.
- Escorial wool felt has a natural 'memory' that returns it to a curled state after stretching.
- Repair services include steam and brush, full re-block on a wooden block, brim re-block, sweatband and lining replacement, and a conformateur fitting for hard hats.
- It claims to have 'created the world's first bowler hat'.
