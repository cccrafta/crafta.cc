## Summary
Lenzing press release, 16 October 2024 (T1). Read as page text via curl.

## Key Claims
- SAISEI Collection debuted at Kingpins Amsterdam, 23-24 October 2024: Kaihara Denim weaving with LENZING™ ECOVERO™ fibres with REFIBRA™ technology and ROICA™ EF recycled stretch yarn.
- Three fabrications: super stretch (Monster Stretch by Kaihara Denim), comfort stretch, and low stretch (selvedge denim); the fabric is said to have "a subtle bulkiness and reduced drape".
- Footnote: ECOVERO with REFIBRA contains a minimum of 20% recycled material from pre- and post-consumer cotton waste and meets the Recycled Claim Standard; the body text says "up to 20% of post-consumer textile waste".
- ROICA™ EF is made with pre-consumer recycled content and is said to be 30% lower in CO2 emissions than regular yarns (Asahi Kasei claim).
- The release says nothing about Kaihara's share of Japanese denim.
