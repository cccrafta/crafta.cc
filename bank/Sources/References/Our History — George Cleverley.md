## Summary
Maker's history page.

## Key Claims
- George Cleverley, born 10 August 1898, spent 38 years at Tuczek (Clifford Street) before founding G.J. Cleverley on Cork Street, Mayfair, in 1958.
- In 1978 he picked pupils John Carnera and George Glasgow to take over; died 1991 almost 93, still working.
- Now at 13 The Royal Arcade, 28 Old Bond Street; still a family business led by George Glasgow Jr (CEO and creative director).
- Signature 'Cleverley shape' is a chisel-toed shoe. The page does not say where shoes are made.
