## Summary
Interview around Martens' first ready-to-wear show (SS26, October 2025). Primary-source quotes on intent. Read via WebFetch summary.

## Key Claims
- Martens: "I wanted to do something democratic"; ready-to-wear should be "never precious".
- "I want people to wear Margiela" and "I want my staff to wear it on the street."
- On ready-to-wear vs Artisanal: "I don't have the urge to do these crazy artistic things in my ready-to-wear."
- He says he designed the collection himself before his appointment was confirmed.
- On Martin Margiela: "he was really creating clothes that were ready to wear", which Martens calls close to the founder's principles.
- Show details per the writer: metal mouthpieces (four-stitch motif), tuxedo jackets over ripped shirts, children's orchestra in oversized suits.
