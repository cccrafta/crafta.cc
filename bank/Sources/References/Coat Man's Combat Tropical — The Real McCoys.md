## Summary
Maker's product page for The Real McCoy's reproduction of the Vietnam tropical combat coat. The claims are the maker's own, and it shows the design still being made as a faithful copy in Japan; other makers' reinterpretations are recorded below from different sources.

## Key Claims
- Maker's claim: The Real McCoy's MJ22006 is made in poplin.
- Maker's claim: it uses urea buttons and is sewn in-house in Japan.
- Maker's claim: it copies the early models of the Tropical Combat Coat.
- Also reported (other sources, stockist listings): Engineered Garments makes a "Jungle Fatigue Jacket" in ripstop and sheeting, listed as USA made with a hidden button closure.
- Also reported (Nigel Cabourn, https://www.cabourn.com/en-us/blogs/shop-info/flagshipstore20260502): the Cabourn Utility Jacket is "based on the Jungle Fatigue", with four gusseted pockets, tailored lapels and Ventile.

## Notes
- The Real McCoy's reasoning behind the design, in its own words, was not found; nor for Buzz Rickson's, RRL, Kapital, visvim or Fullcount.
- Cabourn's Heritage Post interview calls Vietnam the "most modern vintage military clothing I use" but never mentions the jungle jacket.
