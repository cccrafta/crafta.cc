## Summary
Kurashiki city feature (published 8 Mar 2017) on Marugo. Municipal promotional writing, based on company interviews; translated here. Read via WebFetch (a model summary of the page), so second-hand.

## Key Claims
- Chayamachi grew cotton from the Edo period, which fed a textile industry including indoor tabi (座敷足袋).
- Around 1917 (Taisho 6) founder Fujiki Itaro shaped rickshaw tyres and sewed them to footwear, a sewn-on jika-tabi the page calls "one of the origins of jika-tabi"; the company was set up in 1919 (Taisho 8) as Marugo Tabi; the name expresses a hope to reach the world's five continents.
- Factory once had as many as 600 employees.
- Overseas customers saw jika-tabi as comfortable and as martial-arts shoes and asked for fashionable versions; ASSABOOTS launched in Europe in 2009, were first made at a Marugo factory abroad in 2011, then moved to Kurashiki; Japanese sales began 2012; Tabirira development began 2014.
- Products include versions with Kojima denim and Kurashiki canvas.
