## Summary
House page explaining the techniques in the FW26 line (Bianchetto, Tapestry, Fusion, Assemblage). Read via WebFetch (a model summary of the page), so second-hand.

## Key Claims
- Bianchetto is "our white overpaint technique"; it "originated from our first studio furniture, overpainted in white" (no year on this page).
- The paint is said to crack and fade over time, showing signs of use and age.
- Products named Bianchetto on this page: Heel-less Knee Boots ($2,790) and Heel-less Pumps ($1,750); application method is not described.
- Other FW26 techniques: Tapestry (reused furnishing fabrics, since the 1990s), Fusion (a material bonded over a garment, deliberately longer and wider so the edge shows), Assemblage (disparate materials joined).
