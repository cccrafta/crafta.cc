## Summary
Visit piece on James Smith & Sons.

## Key Claims
- James Smith & Sons began in 1830; the New Oxford Street shop opened in 1857; described as the last in London to make umbrellas on site; fifth generation of the Smith family still owns it.
- The workshop is in the basement with 19th-century tools on wooden benches; a bell rings each time an umbrella is finished, about three a day.
- Most umbrellas now use nylon twill; silk on request; parts come from specialist suppliers across Europe, many of whom have closed, so the shop keeps adapting.
- Lightweight steel frames arrived in 1851; accounts still kept by hand in ring binders; manager Phil Naisbitt. The article does not describe a repair service.
