## Summary
Interview with nanamica founder Eiichiro Homma, a former Goldwin technical-outerwear designer, on bringing performance-wear logic into everyday clothing. Page dated 7 July 2026. WebFetch summary with short quotes.

## Key Claims
- Homma: "I've worked at Goldwin as a designer of technical outerwear for more than 18 years."
- "Function begins with protection from rain, wind, and cold", and is "about how naturally a garment fits into your everyday life".
- "When talking about style, we don't mean decoration."
- Method: "We transform modern technology into real life garments"; combines contrasting qualities such as natural with technical, soft with durable, with cotton GORE-TEX as the example.
- Stated aim: "a hybrid of fashion and function"; "the main character must always be the human being."
- Background via search results (secondary): nanamica founded 2003 with Takashi Imaki; the same year Goldwin and Homma launched The North Face Purple Label; keywords "Utility" and "Sports".
- The interview does not mention workwear or military references; workwear lineage is not part of his stated framing.
