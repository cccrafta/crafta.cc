- Reported as comparable to ePTFE in waterproofness and stronger and thinner (maker claim relayed).
- Brands named: The North Face, Rab, Arc'teryx, Mammut, Mountain Hardwear; Arc'teryx Beta AR first jacket with Gore-Tex Pro ePE.
