## Summary
2018 directory; read to first 100,000 characters; addresses may be stale.

## Key Claims
- British Invisible Mending (32 Thayer St, Marylebone) costs roughly GBP 80-300 and mended moth holes in a flannel jacket per a reader.
- Denim and workwear: Blackhorse Lane (Coal Drops Yard) repairs other brands' jeans; Clutch Cafe, Son of a Stag and Bryceland's have chainstitch machines for hemming.
- Cromford Leather (56 Chiltern St) repairs and alters leather clothing; Steven Skippen at Gaziano & Girling does shoe patina and polishing.
- No London hat-repair or waxed-jacket specialist is listed; for hats the author says go back to the maker and cites Lock's service.
