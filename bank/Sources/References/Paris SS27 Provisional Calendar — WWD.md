## Summary
WWD summary of the provisional Paris spring 2027 schedule (published 2026-07-24). Read directly via curl. Useful as a dated statement on where Margiela showed last season.

## Key Claims
- "Back in the City of Light this season are Maison Margiela on Sept. 29, after opting for Shanghai last season."
- Margiela's SS27 show is slotted at 5:30 p.m. on the second day of Paris Fashion Week.
- Valentino, which shows couture in January, is also described as returning to the Paris schedule after showing fall 2026 in Rome.
- Gives no sign of a Margiela couture presentation in July 2026 (inference from silence in this article; see Wallpaper couture-week preview).
