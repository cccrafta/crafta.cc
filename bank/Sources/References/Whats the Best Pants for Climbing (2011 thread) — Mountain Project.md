## Summary
Forum thread dated 19 January 2011 (page 2 of 4) in which climbers recommend workwear canvas, painter pants and technical fabrics. T4 anecdotal evidence; usernames omitted. Summary via WebFetch.

## Key Claims
- Several posters favour Carhartt: "Carhartt double duck pants are the only way to fly"; "durable, affordable".
- Canvas shorts praised as "Good heavy material that takes a beating"; bare legs stick better in knee jams but "you do end up bloody after a long day".
- Carhartt Relaxed Fit Khakis bought for about $25 on sale and called "perfect for bouldering".
- Arborwear tree-climber pants described as similar to Carhartts but with a gusseted crotch that aids movement.
- Old early-1980s Chouinard/Patagonia double-layer canvas pants remembered as "Big, bulky, sturdy", cut-protective, and "dam hot in summer".
- For wide cracks, cheap or thrift jeans are suggested; thick fabric with some nylon and stretch "seems to last the longest".
- Schoeller-fabric pants (Mammut Courmayeur) called highly abrasion resistant and fast-drying but too hot at about 80 F and pricey.
- Original poster found martial-arts pants "rip too fast" and planned to try Dickies.
