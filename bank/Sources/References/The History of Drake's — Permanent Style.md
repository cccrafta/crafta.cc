## Summary
2012 history; later ownership details come from search summaries (Wikipedia citing Permanent Style).

## Key Claims
- Founded 1977 by three ex-Aquascutum staff: Michael Drake (head of menswear design), Jeremy Hull and Isabel Dickson; began with oversized lambswool scarves.
- Mid-1980s ties: Hill & Drake line with Charles Hill, sold on to Turnbull & Asser; Drake's then hired tiemakers across the country and moved them into its Clerkenwell office, still a working factory in 2012.
- 2007 website launch; 2011 Clifford Street shop.
- The article gives no detail on how ties are made and names no mills; Holliday & Brown (where Drake once had ties made) is now owned by Mantero in Como.
