## Summary
2016 visit: basement workroom, military trade, house style.

## Key Claims
- Family-run Savile Row tailor about 150 years old; in 2012 it took over the workrooms beneath the shop to fit far more people on site.
- Military tailoring is still about 30% of the work and all cutters are trained in it; weekly visits to Sandhurst; made uniforms for Princes William and Harry (Blues & Royals).
- Per head cutter Nicholas De'Ath there were probably 22 regiments 20 years ago and 8 now.
- House style is strong-shouldered, 'clean and close', little chest drape or skirt flare, little rope to the shoulder.
- April 2016: first ready-to-wear (from GBP 995), half-canvassed, made by Wensum in Mauritius; alterations in-house.
