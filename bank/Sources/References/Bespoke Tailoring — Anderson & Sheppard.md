## Summary
Maker's bespoke page: location, year, circular-tweed initiative. Thin on workroom detail.

## Key Claims
- Established 1906; bespoke business at 32 Old Burlington Street, London W1S 3AT; holds a royal warrant as tailors to the King.
- The homepage links to a gallery of garments from 'the workrooms' and to Process and Team pages at the Old Burlington Street address; it does not itself say who makes the garments.
- Promotes an off-cut house tweed as 'a circular initiative promoting recycled waste in bespoke tailoring'.
- Displays B Corp and Planet Mark badges.
