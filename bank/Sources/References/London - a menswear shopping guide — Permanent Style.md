## Summary
August 2024 city guide (read to character 100,000 of about 141,000). T2 curated list; one-line verdicts only.

## Key Claims
- Blackhorse Lane (Coal Drops Yard): 'superb, both for their own jeans and for the repairs and alterations they do'.
- Clutch Cafe (Fitzrovia): 'the biggest range of Japanese workwear brands in London'; Son of a Stag (East End): early popularizer of selvedge denim; Bryceland's (Chiltern St): workwear and tailoring mix.
- Outsiders (Coal Drops Yard): 'perhaps the best outdoors shop in London'.
- Cromford Leather (Chiltern St): coats still made downstairs, with leather repairs; Bennett Winch (Savile Row): UK-made canvas and leather casual bags; Lewison's and House of Vintage (Brick Lane): the vintage options, Lewison's the purist.
- Lock & Co (St James's St): 'the most famous hatter in the country'; Universal Works at Coal Drops Yard listed as worth popping into.
