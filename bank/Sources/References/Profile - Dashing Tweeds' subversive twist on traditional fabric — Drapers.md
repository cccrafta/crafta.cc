## Summary
11 April 2016 trade-press profile. Best source for how the cloth is actually made and where.

## Key Claims
- McDougall (RCA-trained) develops swatches on a 24-shaft sample handloom in the Dalston studio, designing two collections a year.
- Production is by commission weavers across the UK, mainly Drove Weaving in Langholm (part of Lochcarron of Scotland); runs are 60-600 metres.
- A reflective 3M yarn woven with British and Shetland wool was made for Fred Perry; the idea came from Hills scraping up double-yellow-line paint to match the colour.
- Anderson & Sheppard received its first delivery in 2006; most ready-to-wear is made in Tottenham.
- A Converse co-branded high-top sold 30,000 pairs in 2011; the brand was marking its 10th anniversary in 2016.
