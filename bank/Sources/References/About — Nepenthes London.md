## Summary
Maker/retailer's about page. Tokyo origin; no London opening date or address on this page.

## Key Claims
- Nepenthes was conceived in 1988 in Tokyo 'on grounds sourcing something wasn't readily available in Japan'; founded by Keizo Shimizu, with Daiki Suzuki joining around the first shop.
- It began with a strong focus on US-made goods, moved to collaborating with obscure labels and then to making its own lines.
- The London range lists Needles, Engineered Garments, South2 West8, Workaday, tActi, RhodolirioN, Woburn Walk and Shiden.
