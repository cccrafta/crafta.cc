## Summary
Hypebeast (Petar Kujundzic, 23 Jul 2016, crediting GQ). Notable because it dates Margiela's first use to the early 2000s, against the SS1999 date in other sources. Read via WebFetch (a model summary of the page), so second-hand.

## Key Claims
- Says the shoe's "first foray into high fashion was made possible by Maison Margiela in early 2000s"; no season, no Artisanal line named.
- Margiela bought vintage pairs and updated them: embossed tongue with the house's numeric branding and hand-painted midsoles.
- Staff were given pairs to doodle on, and each pair came with a card reading "Feel free to add your own graffiti".
- Says it "remains unclear which label actually made" the shoes and that the Dassler brothers were asked to produce it in the 1970s; only a handful of originals remain.
