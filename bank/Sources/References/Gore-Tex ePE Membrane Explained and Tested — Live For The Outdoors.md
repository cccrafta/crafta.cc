## Summary
Chris Williams, updated 10 December 2024. Read as page text via curl. Hands-on impressions (T3), affiliate-supported; not a lab test.

## Key Claims
- Tested in waterproof gear from Patagonia and Mountain Equipment in Scotland: "you don't really notice any difference. It's a bit thinner"; the writer says a blind test would struggle to detect a change.
- Gore says ePE is lighter and thinner than ePTFE, durable like its original fabric, and PFC-free of the compounds "of environmental concern", agreed in partnership with Greenpeace; the page itself notes Gore "isn't eliminating PFCs completely".
- Gore aims to transition to majority ePE "by sometime in 2025" (reported, not a Gore document).
- No breathability or waterproofness figures; the verdict is purely about perceived performance.
