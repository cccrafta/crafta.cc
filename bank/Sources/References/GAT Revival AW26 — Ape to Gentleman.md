## Key Claims
- The GAT "is enjoying a renaissance, and it shows no signs of slowing down"; it was first made for the West German military and is "quiet, understated and slim-fitting, with leather uppers, suede overlays and gum rubber soles".
- Brands are now trying new materials, exaggerated proportions and hybrid shapes after years of keeping to the original design.
- "Maison Margiela has championed the design for decades with its iconic Replica"; shown with the Margiela Replica Leather and Suede Sneakers (Mr Porter link) and the adidas BW Army (END. link).
- Cheaper makers named: Oliver Cabell (GAT), Novesta (GAT Leather Court Sneaker), Reproduction of Found; the only colourway named is "classic white, grey and gum", "the one to beat". (The earlier stub also listed Puma Palermo, Collegium and Todd Snyder; those do not appear in the fetched text.)
- Not on the page: prices (the stub's "about $120" is unsupported), Kith x adidas BW Army, specific new colourways.
