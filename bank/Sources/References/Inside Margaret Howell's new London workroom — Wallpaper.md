## Summary
Design-press tour of the Harringay workroom; corroborates and extends the maker's page.

## Key Claims
- Workroom is in Florentia Village, Harringay, a former textile manufacturing hub, set up summer 2026; first shirts arrive in A/W 2026 in 12 designs.
- Every stage happens on site, patterns are made in-house and pattern pieces are still cut by hand; 17 people work there.
- Howell's earlier 'Unit 7' workroom in Edmonton, north London, opened in 2000; each shirt was stitched by one machinist from up to 25 components.
- Other Margaret Howell shirts are made in and around Florence, Italy, and in other British factories.
- Early cotton came from David & John Anderson, a Scottish supplier of men's dress shirt fabrics.
