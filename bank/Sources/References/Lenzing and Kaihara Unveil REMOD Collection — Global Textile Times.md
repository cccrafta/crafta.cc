## Key Claims
- REMOD Collection: a denim range built around three TENCEL™ Lyocell fibres (Standard, HV100, dtex 2.2), woven at Kaihara Corporation's integrated mill in Hiroshima (per the Japanese release: 広島県にあるカイハラ株式会社の一貫生産工場).
- HV100 uses Variocut technology, which introduces deliberate variations in fibre length for a subtle surface irregularity and a matte, cotton-like look; dtex 2.2 is described as linen-inspired.
- Range: heavyweight men's jackets, mid-weight wide-leg jeans, stretch jeans and cotton-free casual shirting, with constructions both with and without cotton.
- Lenzing's Dennis Hui: TENCEL fibres "have been part of Kaihara's collections since the 1990s". Kaihara's Akihiro Maehara frames the collection around texture: fabrics that "balance authentic denim aesthetics with softness, drape and comfort".
- These are vendor claims; no fibre ratios, weights or wear data are given.
