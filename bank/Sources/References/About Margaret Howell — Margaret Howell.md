## Summary
Maker's about page. Gives the design philosophy and the British cloth lineage but no founding year or factory detail.

## Key Claims
- Howell dates her start to finding an old pinstripe shirt at a jumble sale in 1970 (this is the story's date, not a company registration date).
- She cites Harris Tweed, the 'irregular slub of Irish linen', Scottish knitting factories and Mackintosh's part hand-made raincoats as British traditions she works with.
- She admires mid-20th-century British design: Anglepoise lamps, Ercol furniture, Robert Welch cutlery.
- She describes herself as a hands-on designer wanting clothes 'relaxed and lived in', with a natural look.
