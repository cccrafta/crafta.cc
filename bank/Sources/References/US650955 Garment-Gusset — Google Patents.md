## Summary
Earliest gusset patent found in this research: a built-up elastic insert for the crotch and front of a bifurcated lower garment, giving stretch along diagonal lines. The text never names drawers, trousers or overalls, so its garment type is uncertain. OCR was garbled; quotes are fragments.
## Key Claims
- Inventor Jeremiah A. Scriven of New York; filed 28 August 1899, granted 5 June 1900; CPC class A41B 9/04 (knickers).
- Describes "bifurcated nether garments" with an "elastic insert or panel in rear garment".
- Aim is stretch and strength where needed: "great elasticity strength at desired places".
- Built from layered stretch fabric whose ribs run at different angles; a front insert with lengthwise ribs and a crotch insert with sideways ribs limit each other's distension, giving expansion along diagonals.
- The text does not say whether it is underwear or outerwear, so it cannot be cited as workwear or sport evidence by itself.
- Use as evidence only that a crotch gusset was a patented idea by 1900 and that its purpose was stretch plus strength in one place.
