## Summary
Percival states plainly that production is global; only a capsule is made in England.

## Key Claims
- Founded in East London in 2010; first store in Soho.
- "Our garments are made across the world with skilled craftspeople we know and trust"; organic cotton from Portugal, recycled blends from Italy, specialist wool from the UK.
- Made in England capsule: outerwear cut and sewn at a 150-year-old Manchester factory, 6-ply lambswool knitwear in Leicestershire, military Derby shoes in Northamptonshire; runs of 100 or fewer.
- Factory not named.
