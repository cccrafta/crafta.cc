## Summary
March 2026 update; read to first 100,000 characters only.

## Key Claims
- Canons (North London, owner Simon Bolzoni) is the top recommendation: 'the most comfortable bespoke shoes I've ever had'.
- Foster & Son is described as the 'old business' (Jermyn Street shop closed); the name is now used for accessories and ready-made shoes built in Northampton.
- Carreducker (London, Deborah Carre and James Ducker) boots via remote fitting with some machine stitching: 'pretty good', not top-maker finishing.
- Cleverley: three pairs over 13 posts; fit problems at outside joints and little toe; still keeps the black pair 'because they are beautifully made'.
- Masaru Okuyama has moved from Hong Kong to London; Nicholas Templeman left for Vancouver. Author says Lobb St James's style never appealed to him.
