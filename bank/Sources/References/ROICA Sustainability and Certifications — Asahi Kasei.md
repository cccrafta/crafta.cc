## Summary
Maker's sustainability page (T1), read as page text via curl. It lists an ISCC PLUS "mass balance certification for circular materials" among plant certifications, with no certificate number or site attribution in the text; a search summary of the same site attributes it to the Taiwan plant (not verified here). The ISCC certificate database was not queryable.

## Key Claims
- ROICA™ lists "International Sustainability & Carbon Certification (ISCC) PLUS - Mass balance certification for circular materials" alongside ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001 for its plants.
- ROICA™ EF carries Recycled Claim Standard certification (certificate numbers shown for Japan, Thailand and Taiwan).
- The page has a dedicated "Mass Balance Approach" initiative under raw-material procurement.
- The page itself notes certifications differ by plant and yarn.
