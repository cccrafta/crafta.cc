## Summary
Maker's heritage timeline.

## Key Claims
- Miller Christy and Joseph Storrs set up a hat business at Whitehart Court, London, on 1 March 1773; the Christys took over the Stockport felt works in 1824; Bermondsey factory became one of the largest bowler makers.
- 1990s: Stockport closed and all operations moved to Witney, Oxfordshire, alongside Compton Webb; 2011: Liberty acquired Christy & Co and Compton Webb.
- 2026: Laird Hatters acquired Christys', 'bringing production back to London' (no detail on where or when).
- Timeline mentions the first British police headwear and a 2013 fedora for Harrods.
