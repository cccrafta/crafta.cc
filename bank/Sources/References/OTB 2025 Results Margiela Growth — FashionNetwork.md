## Summary
FashionNetwork report (Laura Galbiati, translated by Nicola Mira, published 2026-02-17) on OTB's 2025 results. Read via WebFetch, so the extraction is a model summary (second-hand); the headline figures are corroborated by Jing Daily and TheIndustry.fashion. OTB does not report Margiela revenue.

## Key Claims
- OTB 2025 revenue €1.7 billion, "down 4.8% at constant exchange rates"; net sales €1.6 billion, down 5%; EBITDA €237.3 million, a 15.1% margin on net sales.
- Maison Margiela grew 8.4% (headline); CEO Ubaldo Minelli said "in excess of 8%". Diesel reached "the highest profitability level of the last 10 years".
- Wholesale revenue fell 14.7%; retail, outlet and e-tail fell 2.6% combined but rose to 60% of sales. The article gives no wholesale target (the 25-30% target is in the Jing Daily report).
- 49 stores opened, 58 closed, 600 single-brand stores at year end; new Margiela flagships in Canada, the Middle East and Mexico.
- Three creative-director changes in 2025: Glenn Martens (Maison Margiela), Simone Bellotti (Jil Sander), Meryll Rogge (Marni).
- 2026 outlook: Minelli speaks of ambitious growth goals and continued investment in product and supply chain; no 2026 figures. Japan is stable at more than 27% of revenue.
- Claims previously in this note and not found in the article (Margiela +4.6% in 2024, acquisition in 2002, a possible 2026 listing) are removed as unverified.
