## Summary
Marugo's own centenary history page (T1, maker). Japanese text; translated here. The page did not carry a fashion-line entry. Read via WebFetch (a model summary of the page), so second-hand.

## Key Claims
- 1919: Marugo Tabi Co. (丸五足袋株式会社) was founded; the entry credits Fujiki Itaro (藤木伊太郎) with inventing the sewn-on jika-tabi (縫い付け式地下足袋) by sewing reshaped rickshaw tyres onto tabi, at a time when most people wore straw sandals or wooden clogs outdoors.
- 1924: a German engineer was hired and leather shoe production began; 1925: athletic shoes and short rubber boots.
- 1932: patented "everlasting work gloves" (万年軍手), called Japan's first rubber-coated gloves; 1954: the Kurashiki plant was spun off as Marugo Rubber Industry for auto parts.
- 1995: launched Pro-Guard Mannen, jika-tabi with resin toe caps, alongside a safety sneaker.
- 2012: the company "restarted jika-tabi production in Japan to keep the craft alive" (translation); production had meanwhile been placed in factories abroad (China from 1968, Korea from 1986, the Philippines from 1992).
- 2019: group centenary.
