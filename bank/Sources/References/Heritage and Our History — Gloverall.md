## Summary
Gloverall's own history and current production statement. T1, maker claims.

## Key Claims
- Founded in London 1951 by Harold and Freda Morris; name contracts "gloves and overalls".
- Began with surplus WWII Royal Navy duffle coats; by 1953 Harold, son of a Savile Row master tailor, redesigned the coat (slimmer, leather loops instead of jute rope, horn toggles, check Italian lining).
- Own factory in Northamptonshire by 1962; today the Heritage Collection is "cut, stitched and finished in England at a factory outside London" (town unnamed).
- The Original Monty still follows the 1951 pattern (maker claim).
- Fabrics: Italian wool blends, Melton, Harris Tweed; knitwear made in Britain; outdoor range and accessories made elsewhere.
