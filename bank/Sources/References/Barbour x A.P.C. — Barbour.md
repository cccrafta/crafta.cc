## Summary
Barbour's own collaboration page for Barbour x A.P.C., listing a Trucker Denim Jacket among the pieces. Everything on it is the maker's claim; no denim weight or mill is given and the launch date is not stated.

## Key Claims
- The Barbour x A.P.C. Trucker Denim Jacket is style MCA1168IN71 and is 100% cotton (maker's claim).
- The price is about $525 or EUR 390 (maker's page).
- The collection is described by Barbour as combining "Barbour's wax with A.P.C.'s denim" (maker's claim).
- It is described as the first large collection between the two brands (maker's claim).
- No denim weight or mill is given, and the launch date is not stated on the page.

## Notes
- A banner file name containing "AW26" is inference only; do not treat it as a launch season.
