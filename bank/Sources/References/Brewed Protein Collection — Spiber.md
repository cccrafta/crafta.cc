## Key Claims
- Global release on 29 September 2023 of the 2023 Fall/Winter collection from four labels: The North Face, Goldwin, nanamica and Woolrich; 17 products in total.
- Described as the first commercial-scale, mass-produced collection to go on sale since operations started at Spiber's Thai plant.
- Sold in Japan and abroad simultaneously; a Goldwin-only pop-up in London on 29-30 September and a collection pop-up in the Marunouchi Building, Tokyo, running to late January 2024.
- The page calls it "next-generation Brewed Protein™ fiber" and does not explain the process or give a plant capacity.
- Not on this page: Goldwin's 2030 target (10% of newly developed products), the 2026 vehicle application and the 45 brands / 193 products figure; see the linked notes.
