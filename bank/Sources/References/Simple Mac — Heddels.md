## Summary
Short product write-up of Margaret Howell's waxed cotton Simple Mac.

## Key Claims
- Howell had been working for almost four decades at the time of writing (2019).
- Simple Mac is water-resistant waxed cotton with raglan sleeves, internal storm placket, internal pockets, press studs, flap welt pocket and metal eyelet vents under the arms.
- Offered in khaki and black.
- Says nothing about where it is made.
