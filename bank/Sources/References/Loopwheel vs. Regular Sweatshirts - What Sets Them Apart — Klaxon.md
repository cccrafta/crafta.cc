## Summary
Retailer blog post comparing loopwheeled and sinker-knit sweatshirts. The retailer sells loopwheel products and the article offers no testing or data, so every claim is a seller's assertion. Read through a fetch summary.

## Key Claims
- Retailer says low-tension loopwheel knitting means the fabric "retains its texture, remains soft" and traps air, adding breathability.
- Retailer says loopwheel fabric "gradually conforms to the body" and develops "a natural patina that's hard to replicate" with wear.
- Retailer says regular sweatshirts are knitted at high speed under high tension and are "unlikely" to develop the same texture, and "often said to lack the same durability".
- Retailer gives loopwheel output as about 16 metres of fabric per day against over 200 metres on sinker machines (Loopwheeler's own page gives about 1 m per hour; figures differ).
- The page gives nothing on washing, shrinkage or fading.
