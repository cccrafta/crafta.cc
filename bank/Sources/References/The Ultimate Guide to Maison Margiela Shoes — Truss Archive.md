## Summary
Resale-archive guide (T3, commercial). Useful because it names a season for the Tabi and the Replica; some of its dates are contested by other sources. Read via WebFetch (a model summary of the page), so second-hand.

## Key Claims
- The Tabi appears "in altered forms every season from spring 1989 onwards"; 1988 is given as the founding year of the house.
- The Replica GAT "was first introduced in Spring 1999", "originally released as part of the 'Artisanal' line, '0,10: Garments remodelled by hand for men'".
- "It was in 1996 that Margiela discovered the German Army Trainer while in Austria"; the label reads "Men's sports shoes, lambskin and calf-split, Austria, 70's".
- Says the original trainer was made by Adi and Rudolf Dassler in the 1970s for army fitness training; this conflicts with the other sources (see Hypebeast 2015 and German Wikipedia notes) and is not relied on.
