## Summary
Albam's account of its founding and early British supply chain.

## Key Claims
- Founded 2006 by Alastair Rae and James Shaw (Mr Porter says 2007; own page says 2006).
- Started with a Loughborough knitter, a Derbyshire factory and a Leicester dye house for the Classic T-shirt.
- First shop on Beak Street, Soho, opened October 2007 (Spitalfields Life, secondary).
- Search summaries say T-shirts and shirts are now made in Portugal and outerwear in British factories; not confirmed on the page. [single-source on current split]
