## Summary
August 2019 profile of Paynter's first batch. Useful for the workwear lineage (French chore coat) and the batch model; says nothing on HQ location or repair.

## Key Claims
- Batch No.1 is a nod to the classic French chore coat, recut tailored rather than the 'boxy, baggy' vintage originals.
- Production limited to 300 pieces, three times a year (the maker's own page later says four), each jacket cut to order.
- Jackets are assembled by hand in a mountainside factory in Guimaraes, Portugal; reverse bull denim comes from Berto, Italy, made bespoke for the brand.
- A wash house garment-dyes the jackets; corozo buttons are handmade in the UK from nuts grown in Ecuador.
- Batch No.1 sold out in 14 minutes (article published 19 August 2019).
