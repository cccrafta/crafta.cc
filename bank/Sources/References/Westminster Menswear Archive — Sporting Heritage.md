## Summary
Collection directory entry. Only one source found so far; access terms not stated.

## Key Claims
- Founded 2016, held at the University of Westminster, Northwick Park, Harrow; 'currently the only collection in the United Kingdom dedicated exclusively to men's clothing, fashion and design'.
- More than 3,000 garments and accessories from the 1780s to the present, covering workwear, military uniform and current men's fashion.
- Sportswear is 'a key strength': cycling, football, skiing, hiking, hunting and motorcycling; a large Umbro group followed a 2024 centenary exhibition.
- Focus is on the technical and functional aspects of menswear design, serving students, researchers and designers; contact via mensweararchive.com.
