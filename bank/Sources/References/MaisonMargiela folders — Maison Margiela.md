## Summary
The house's own page for MaisonMargiela/folders, its archive programme tied to the FW26 Shanghai show. Read directly via curl. Promotional in tone; treat descriptions as the maker's account.

## Key Claims
- Four "house codes" were exhibited across China in April 2026: Artisanal ("our couture line"), Anonymity (masks), Tabi ("our split-toe shoe collected by a global community") and Bianchetto ("our white overpaint technique").
- Shanghai: 58 Artisanal looks from 1989 to today, in a container installation on Yan Dang road; key pieces run "from the AW1989 porcelain plate waistcoat to pieces from our FW2026 show", sorted by upcycling, deconstruction, trompe l'oeil and archetypes.
- The process was shown from concept to finished piece, including show invitations made from unexpected materials and "detail logs recording each piece's intention, materials and construction."
- Chengdu: personal collections of 9 Tabi collectors, with each collector's wardrobe reconstructed full-scale.
- Shenzhen: participants transformed an item from their own wardrobe with the Bianchetto white overpaint technique, guided by the Maison Margiela Atelier team.
- The material is shared "globally through our internal Dropbox folders" (links on the page; the Dropbox contents were not opened).
