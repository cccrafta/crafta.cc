## Summary
T4 lead only. Establishes London base and work-uniform theme; no production countries.

## Key Claims
- London-based; debut collection January 2017; work 'centered on modern-day uniform and contemporary workwear'.
- Creative director of Mackintosh 0001 from November 2017 (no longer); ASICS footwear from SS2018 and the Novalis line announced 2023.
- Other collaborations: Stussy 2015, Camper hiking boots 2018, Levi's 2024, Fox Racing Fox Lab capsule 2025.
- Production countries are not stated.
