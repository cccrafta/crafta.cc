## Summary
Yvon Chouinard's Let My People Go Surfing (Penguin, 2005; revised 2016) is the primary text for Patagonia's design philosophy, but only secondary quotation pages were reached (Goodreads, AZQuotes, Medium summaries). Verify each line and page number against the book before publishing. Patagonia.com philosophy pages failed to load.

## Key Claims
- Quoted passage: "I believe the way toward mastery of any endeavor is to work toward simplicity; replace complex technology with knowledge. The more you know, the less you need." (Goodreads quote page; AZQuotes cites p. 90 of the Penguin edition, single source).
- Context for that line: Shackleton's carpenter on the James Caird carried only three hand tools (secondary summary).
- Patagonia's design checklist (secondary write-ups) asks: functional? multifunctional? durable? fits the customer? repairable? as simple as possible? is the line simple? is it chasing fashion?
- Reasoning reported: a product's weakest component sets its lifespan, so repairability and fewer parts matter.
- Chouinard cites Dieter Rams, "good design is as little design as possible", for functionally driven design being minimalist (secondary).
- Shorthand "Buy less; buy better" is attributed to the book by secondary summaries.
- Not verified: any phrase "Does it need to be made?" attributed to Patagonia.
