## Summary
Trend outlook (Dec 2025) citing market-research firms. Page text garbled; second-hand. Use for direction, not figures.

## Key Claims
- Theme: fabrics move from niche sustainability efforts to integrated systems of bio-based, recycled and smart textiles.
- Focus shifting from bottle-derived recycled polyester to textile-to-textile closed-loop recycling; Textile Exchange CEO cited a record 132 million tons of fibre produced in 2024.
- Large-scale automated sorting needed for recycling to be economic.
- NILIT and Asahi Kasei showed a biomass-balanced nylon 6.6 concept; Bcomp AmpliTex is a 100% natural-fibre biocomposite fabric.
- Mycelium leather described as moving toward the mainstream (MycoWorks Reishi).
