## Summary
Highsnobiety trend piece (Jake Silbert, 2025) on the GAT resurgence. Read via WebFetch summary.

## Key Claims
- Resurgence began roughly two years before the article; 2024 hype was a stream, by 2025 "Niagara Falls".
- Partly a reaction against Samba overexposure: buyers want a flat shoe less obvious than the Samba.
- TikTok micro-trend "Margiela GATs Summer"; menswear fans already knew the shoe.
- Margiela's GAT started in the late '90s as a dressed-up Bundeswehr trainer (military original about the '70s).
- Kim Jones's last Dior menswear collection (Fall 2025) revived a GAT-like silhouette; adidas relaunched BW Army earlier in the year.
- Margiela GAT about $750, over seven times comparable low-tops (writer's claim).
- Born of Margiela's desire to reclaim the quintessential normal shoe; now worn by "quite literally everyone and their dad".
