## Summary
Klättermusen's maker statement on its climber-founded workshop origin and a repair-first durability stance. WebFetch summary with short quotes; warranty and repair pages were linked but not read.

## Key Claims
- Founded "by a tightly-knit band of local climbers in the city of Umeå in 1975" as a small DIY workshop; footer lists Åre as the current location.
- Stated approach: "repair equipment until it falls apart and to continually strive to improve constructions".
- Goal: "to make the most durable and long-lived equipment imaginable".
- Approaches durability "from a lifecycle point of view"; started a recycling system in 2009.
- Claims to be "the first outdoor brand in history to achieve a 100% fluorocarbon-free collection" in September 2017 (maker claim).
- The page contains no explicit guarantee terms.
- Retailer copy (not the maker) describes the products as long-lasting and repairable.
