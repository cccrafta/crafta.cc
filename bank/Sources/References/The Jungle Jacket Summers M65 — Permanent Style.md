## Summary
Permanent Style's 2021 piece on the jungle jacket as a summer alternative to the M-65. It is the best source found on why heavier reproduction cloth changes the jacket, and also says how vintage olive cotton versions age. Read as a fetch summary (first 100k chars); opinions are the author's.

## Key Claims
- Author's view: vintage ripstop jungle jackets are "as light as a linen overshirt".
- Author's view: reproductions often keep the large pocket size but use heavier cloth, so they look bulky.
- The M-65 is described as only "two layers of cotton".
- Permanent Style says the jungle jacket has 3 versions (RareGearUSA says 5 patterns; numbering is contested).
- Aging: "natural fading at the hems and seams, and the little repairs where the jacket has been caught or worn through" are part of the appeal of vintage; real vintage character needs "lots of wear over a few years".

## Notes
- The aging quote concerns vintage olive cotton, NOT denim. Do not transfer it to denim or to any Barbour jacket.
- Fetch summary only; the summariser often returned partial text, so quotes are short.
