## Summary
Reference table of standard moisture regain by fibre, the number behind why cotton dries slower than nylon or polyester. Table values were read; the page gives no test conditions or standard, so it supports ranking but not drying times.
## Key Claims
- Standard moisture regain (%): cotton 8.5, nylon 4.0, polyester 0.4, wool 16.0, hemp 12.4, flax 12.4.
- Moisture content (%): cotton 7.34, nylon 3.1, polyester 0, wool 13.8, hemp and flax 10.4.
- Definition: regain is the weight of water as a percentage of the oven-dry weight, R = (W / D) x 100; moisture content uses total weight, C = W / (W + D) x 100.
- The page lists one nylon row and does not split nylon 6 from nylon 6,6, and gives no temperature, humidity or ISO/ASTM reference.
- Practical use: cotton holds about twice nylon's water by this measure, so cotton canvas should dry slower than nylon; no per-fibre drying times were found in the sources checked, and the claim is inference from regain.
