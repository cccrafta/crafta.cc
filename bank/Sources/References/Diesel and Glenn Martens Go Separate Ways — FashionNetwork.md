## Key Claims
- Martens steps down as Diesel creative director after the SS27 show at Milan Fashion Week on 22 September, and stays in the OTB Group as head of design at Maison Margiela, where he has been creative director since 2025, immediately after Galliano's departure.
- Diesel's statement: "The next chapter in Diesel's creative direction will be announced in due course"; no successor named.
- Born 1983 in Bruges; began at Jean Paul Gaultier; won the ANDAM Prize in 2017 with Y/Project; guest designer on the Diesel Red Tag capsule in 2018; Diesel creative director from late 2020.
- Renzo Rosso (Diesel founder, OTB chairman): "Glenn Martens' contribution to Diesel has been extraordinary... he has transformed the perception of the brand."
