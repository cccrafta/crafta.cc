## Summary
Gear Patrol piece (17 Feb 2026, headline begins "Barbour Used Levi's As a Springboard...") on Barbour's non-collaboration Denim Bedale. It frames denim against Barbour's usual waxed canvas and lists trade-offs, but the verdicts are the author's opinion and no mill or denim weight is given.

## Key Claims
- Dated 17 Feb 2026 (fetch summary only; the page was not read in full).
- Barbour's non-collaboration Denim Bedale was first sold Japan-only and is now sold in the US via Huckberry for $660.
- Gear Patrol calls the denim "medium-wash denim".
- Gear Patrol notes Barbour's usual cloth is 6oz Sylkoil waxed canvas.
- Trade-offs per the author: denim resists weather less (it is "just cotton", soaks through and blocks wind less well), but the owner avoids rewaxing.
- Author's opinion: it patinas, "matched only by leather". This is the author's view, not tested; do not transfer denim-aging claims from other sources onto this jacket.
- Design details: brown corduroy collar and cuffs, brass-toned hardware, two-way zip.

## Notes
- Fetch summary only. No mill or weight is given.
- It is the only source found that ties Barbour and denim patina, and it is an opinion piece. Useful for the wax-versus-denim framing.
