## Summary
V&A record of a boutique garment from London's Mr Freedom, a football jersey translated into fashion in 1970. A dated, museum-held example of the sport-kit-as-fashion stage. Also see V&A LOAN:AMERICASFOUNDATION.1361-2024, a Champion hooded sweatshirt made for David Bowie's 2003 Reality tour. Retrieved via V&A API.

## Key Claims
- Accession T.203-1974; made Great Britain, September 1970; retailer Mr Freedom; machine-knitted wool; donated by Mr Freedom to the Cecil Beaton Collection.
- Description: scarlet machine-knitted wool jersey football jumper with "15" knitted into the front, short sleeves, round neck, cream cuffs and neck band, smaller "15" on sleeves in blue and white. Label: "Mr Freedom, Made in England".
- It is a fashion shop's version of a team shirt (number as graphic), not a playing kit, 1970.
- Related: V&A LOAN:AMERICASFOUNDATION.1361-2024, sweatshirt by Champion for David Bowie's Reality tour, 2003: black hooded, cotton and polyester, label "Champion AUTHENTIC ATHLETIC APPAREL MADE IN CAMBODIA", embroidered "Bowie".
- Related: V&A T.155-1997, white wool cricket sweater with blue trim, Kent & Curwen, Great Britain, 1997.
