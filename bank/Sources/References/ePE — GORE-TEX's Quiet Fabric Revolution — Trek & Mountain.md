## Summary
Jon Doran, 25 March 2024. Independent field test (T2), no lab data. Read as page text via curl for the key passages, plus a WebFetch summary. The only independent test found that says anything about breathability against the old membrane.

## Key Claims
- Gore's plan, as reported: by autumn 2025 the decades-old ePTFE technology (in use since the 1970s) is replaced by ePE; Gore says it keeps a minimum hydrostatic head of 30,000mm and is "just as durable as current fabrics".
- On breathability Gore "were slightly coy about this, offering no figures or commitments"; the writer concludes "the breathability jury is still out".
- Field test of a Mountain Equipment Makalu jacket over several months of autumn/winter use in the Cairngorms and Peak District: reliably dry and fine for steady-paced walking, but "clearly not quite as breathable as GORE-TEX PRO".
- The PFC-free DWR "rapidly suffered from contamination in regularly handled areas like the cuffs, and simply stopped beading water", described as standard for PFC-free finishes.
- Brands with ePE products in the UK at the time: Mountain Equipment, Patagonia, Rab and adidas Terrex; the standard ePE jackets use a 75D polyester face fabric with a solution-dyed backer, and Pro/Active versions were not yet available (Arc'teryx had launched a Pro ePE shell).
