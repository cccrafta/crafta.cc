## Summary
Association's own scheme page. T1 for structure and durations.

## Key Claims
- Scheme launched 2007; every SRBA member must 'employ at least one salaried apprentice at all times'; over 100 young people have graduated.
- Training durations vary by house: cutting 4-6 years, coats 3-5 years, trousers 2-3 years; assessment by a panel of master tailors every June leads to the SRBA Apprenticeship Diploma.
- The London Academy of Bespoke is official training partner and has run a pre-apprenticeship course since September 2024.
- Apprentices may enter the Golden Shears competition, held every two years at Merchant Taylors' Hall.
