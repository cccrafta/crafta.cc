## Summary
Construction breakdown of the A&S drape chest.

## Key Claims
- Frederick Scholte originated the draped style (soft shoulder, high armhole, large sleevehead), which became the A&S house style.
- Body canvas is wool or horsehair, loosely woven and cut on the bias at about 30 degrees so it can stretch; most tailors cut it at right angles for rigidity.
- Chest stitches are an inch or more long, larger than peers; the shoulder seam is sewn by hand to give more movement, where even some of the most expensive tailors machine-sew it.
- The article does not say whether the work is done in-house.
