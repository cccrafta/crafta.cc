## Key Claims
- Circulose turns cellulose-rich pre- and post-consumer textile waste into dissolving pulp using a patented process; others spin it into viscose, lyocell or viscose filament.
- Spinnova (Jyväskylä, Finland) uses a mechanical fibre process that, per the release, needs no harmful chemicals or dissolving during spinning; Circulose pulp can be used at 100%.
- Spinnova's CEO says the pulp becomes cotton-like fibres rather than viscose.
- Spinnova trialled Circulose pulp in 2023; partners since tested yarn spinning and weaving.
- No capacity, volume, financial terms or scale-up timeline given; integration is described as intended.
