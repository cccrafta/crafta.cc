## Summary
Opinion piece by Chris Erik Thomas (published 2026-06-29), read directly via curl. Argues the split-toe silhouette has lost its strangeness.

## Key Claims
- Margiela's Tabi Daily Mule borrows the open back, wide strap and suede-soft leather of a Birkenstock Boston and keeps the split toe; priced $1,290, or $1,850 in polished leather.
- The writer says Martin Margiela designed the tabi "for his 1989 debut collection" as a take on the traditional Japanese work shoe (the house itself says 1988; see Maison Margiela Tabi page and the tabi research note).
- Argument: the Tabi now ranges across ballet flats, slingbacks, pumps, Mary Janes, loafers and lace-ups, and split-toe sneakers have made the form ordinary.
