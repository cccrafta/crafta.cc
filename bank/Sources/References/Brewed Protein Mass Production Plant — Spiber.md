## Summary
Spiber news, 10 July 2019 (T1). Read as page text via curl. A 2019 plan, not an operating figure.

## Key Claims
- Plant at the Eastern Seaboard Industrial Estate, Rayong, Thailand; "maximum capacity of several hundred tonnes per year".
- Expected at the time to be the world's largest structural protein fermentation facility (Spiber's own independent-research claim, 2019).
- Commercial production was expected to start in 2021; Goldwin's 2023 release puts the start of polymer mass production at early 2022.
- The 500 tonnes per year figure appears in secondary sources only (an Issey Miyake interview and The Robin Report, via search summary).
