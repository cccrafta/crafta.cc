## Summary
February 2019 interview with Shimizu and staff; independent-ish (Japanese editorial) but built from the company's own statements.

## Key Claims
- Nepenthes London is the brand's second overseas store after New York, at 8 Woburn Walk, WC1H 0JL, in a corner of what the article calls London's oldest arcade (about 200 years old, attributed to Thomas Cubitt).
- Two floors, eight staff; range is mostly Nepenthes' own labels (Engineered Garments, Needles), with Needles already distributed to about 40 European stores.
- Shimizu: 'I have been coming to London every year for the past 15 years'; 'We do not intend to grow too large in scale'; 'I still want to do things that look British' and a London label is under consideration.
- An in-house catalogue, INPRINT, plans a London issue covering the arcade area.
