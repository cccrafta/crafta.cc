## Summary
Fall 2025 interview. Good on Howell's own account of the early years, the archive and the Japan licence; silent on where clothes are made.

## Key Claims
- The archive is in the basement of her shop on Margaret Street, Marylebone, kept by a full-time archivist, garments racked under Tyvek.
- She found French shirt patterns fitted better than English ones and bought fine English fabrics from street vendors to recreate the softness of worn vintage shirts.
- First London store opened in 1977; early buyers were Maxfield (Los Angeles) and Joseph (New York); the article says she started the business 55 years ago.
- She licensed the brand to a Japanese businessman who built it to 80+ stores in Japan while she kept creative control.
- On her designs: 'They're real clothes... They're not fantasy.' and 'You must have a feel for the quality of cloth.'
