## Summary
V&A Dundee article on a Nicholas Daley object. T1/T2 for the object's existence and framing; Dundee link is the museum's reading.

## Key Claims
- V&A Dundee shows 'a jute and cotton bomber jacket by designer Nicholas Daley' from his 2017 Juteopolis collection, lent by Daley.
- The collection 'points to his Dundonian family and their connection with Dundee's jute mills'.
- The piece is used in a decolonisation series by dress historian Dr Sally Tuckett, tracing Dundee's jute industry to the 18th-century linen trade and coarse osnaburg cloth shipped to the colonies for clothing enslaved people.
