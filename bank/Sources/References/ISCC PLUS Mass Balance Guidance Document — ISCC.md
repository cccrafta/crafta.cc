## Summary
Scheme owner's guidance (T1), read from the PDF text layer. Caveat: the document says it was published 17.12.2025 and is "currently under public consultation. Not valid yet", so cite it for the scheme's stated concept, not as final rules.

## Key Claims
- ISCC PLUS has three chain-of-custody options: physical segregation, controlled blending and mass balance.
- Mass balance: "certified and non-certified materials can be physically mixed during storage, transport or processing, but are kept segregated on a bookkeeping basis"; entities may not claim more certified product than they sourced.
- The sustainability characteristics stay assigned on a bookkeeping basis; physically the certified material may be mixed with materials of different characteristics and with non-certified (fossil) material.
- Bookkeeping must be site-specific and limited to periodical, spatial and legal boundaries.
