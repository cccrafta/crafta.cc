## Summary
Maker's heritage page. T1 for the founding story and current production claim; the maker's own framing of its role should be attributed.

## Key Claims
- Founded in London in 1951 by Harold and Freda Morris; the name contracts their earlier wholesale trade in gloves and overalls.
- They first sold surplus Royal Navy duffle coats left from the post-war period, then had new coats made to the same military pattern: 'Gloverall didn't reinvent anything. It made the original accessible.'
- Every Heritage Collection coat is made in England 'at our factory outside London'; the page says Gloverall had its own factory in Northamptonshire by 1962 but does not say whether that is the current site.
- No mills are named; Harris Tweed is cited for the overcoats and the Original Monty is still made to the 1951 pattern with wooden toggles on jute rope loops.
- The toggle-and-loop closure was 'simple to operate with gloved hands in extreme cold'; replacement toggles and anchor buttons are sold under Care & Repair.
