## Summary
January 2017 profile. Corroborates single-site London production and the no-overlock construction.

## Key Claims
- Han Ates acquired the Walthamstow warehouse in 1995 ('I've been in this building for 21 years'); every pair is made on site and, apart from fabric, 'we don't source anything from the outside'.
- Han says Japanese jeans often use overlock stitching for speed; Blackhorse Lane uses none, instead tucked edges and a one-piece fly, taking inspiration from tailoring after studying Oni and Momotaro jeans.
- Denim has moved to Italian and Turkish mills to cut environmental impact: the Italian mill buys Egyptian cotton and quality is 'comparable to Japanese denim' and cheaper; Japanese fabrics still used on some garments.
- The site is shared with about 30 artists and craftsmen including leather workers and weavers.
- London's textile industry 'died off in the latter half of the 20th Century'; the brand aims to rekindle it.
