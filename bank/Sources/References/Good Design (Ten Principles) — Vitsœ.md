## Summary
Vitsœ's page reproducing Dieter Rams' ten principles of good design. Headings quoted as seen via WebFetch; no original-publication citation on the page. Verified wording for the principles, not for the broader "less but better" book citation.

## Key Claims
- The ten headings: innovative; makes a product useful; aesthetic; understandable; unobtrusive; honest; long-lasting; thorough down to the last detail; environmentally-friendly; as little design as possible.
- Principle 7: good design "avoids being fashionable and therefore never appears antiquated"; "Unlike fashionable design, it lasts many years - even in today's throwaway society."
- Principle 6: it "does not make a product more innovative, powerful or valuable than it really is" and does "not attempt to manipulate the consumer with promises that cannot be kept."
- Principle 10: "Less, but better" because it "concentrates on the essential aspects"; "Back to purity, back to simplicity."
- Rams began a storage system in 1957 that became the basis of Vitsœ, founded 1959; "the ideology behind my design is engrained within the company".
- Context (search results, secondary): Rams was head of design at Braun 1961 to 1995; the book Weniger, aber besser first published 1995.
- Chouinard cites Rams in Patagonia's product philosophy (secondary, see Chouinard quotes note).
