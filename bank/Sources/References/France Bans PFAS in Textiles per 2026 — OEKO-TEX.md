## Summary
OEKO-TEX news item on the French PFAS law. Read as page text via curl. Short; defers details of limit values to later regulation.

## Key Claims
- In February 2025 France passed a law banning PFAS in several consumer products, including clothing, cosmetics and ski waxes.
- The law prohibits manufacture, import, export and sale of PFAS-containing clothing by 2026, with a broader ban on PFAS in all textiles set for 2030.
- Some exemptions may apply based on limit values to be defined in future regulations.
- The law also adds financial accountability measures for companies emitting PFAS into the environment.
