## Summary
Heddels (12 Jan 2021) on a US-made pant that explicitly combines climbing-pant and workwear construction. A specialist-press statement of which features each tradition contributes, though the assignment of features is partly the reader's inference. Writer had not tried them on. WebFetch summary.
## Key Claims
- The Easy Pant is 'inspired by a pair of climbing pants from the '90s' and made to order in Grease Point's US workshop.
- Climbing-style features listed: elastic waistband with built-in webbing belt, gusseted crotch 'for ease of movement', loose fit with tapered leg.
- Workwear-style features listed: 'double-kneed', reinforced knee panels, double-stitched patch pockets front and back, triple-stitched felled seams, mil-spec hardware.
- Heddels describes climbing pants as 'comfortable, mad durable, aggressively non-restrictive, thoughtfully detailed, and almost never tight' and calls the result 'a menswear crossover episode' and 'genre interpolation'.
- Fabric is pique cotton from Yoshiwa Mills in a 10oz black, 12oz tan and 12oz olive; priced $259.
- Limit: the article never states which feature comes from which tradition; that mapping is inference from its framing.
