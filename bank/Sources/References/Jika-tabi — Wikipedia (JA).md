## Key Claims
- Glued-on rubber-sole tabi (貼付式) is attributed to Ishibashi Tokujiro (石橋徳次郎), utility model no. 80594, registered October 1923; his company Nippon Tabi (日本足袋) in Kurume, Fukuoka launched it in 1922, and it spread via miners at Miike colliery.
- Ishibashi Shojiro (正二郎), Tokujiro's younger brother, ran Nippon Tabi and founded Bridgestone from its rubber know-how; the article credits the invention to Tokujiro, not Shojiro.
- Stitched-on rubber soles began to appear in the late Meiji period (no inventor named).
- Market: Rikio (founded 1948, Tokyo) about 60-70% of the Japanese market, Marugo (1919, Kurashiki) about 30%; most production is overseas.
