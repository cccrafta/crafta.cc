## Summary
UKC forum thread on wearing workwear for climbing; the page returned HTTP 402 to a direct fetch, so only a search-result summary was seen (stub; thread date not confirmed). T4 anecdotal.

## Key Claims
- Original poster wears the cheapest polyester tracksuit bottoms so tearing them on rock costs little.
- Replies: work trousers are heavy compared with climbing trousers and often lack an elasticated waist, so a belt is needed, which is awkward with a harness.
- One poster notes climbing in Carhartts or Dickies is common in the US, as cotton with cleaner lines and fewer pockets.
- A Dickies Redhawk user: heavy, lack of stretch most annoying, zips uncomfortable when chimneying, "stay wet once wet"; preferred Craghoppers Kiwi stretch trousers.
- Alternatives suggested: army surplus combat trousers (hard-wearing, cut for movement) and cheap stretch jeans (about 10 GBP).
- A poster points out climbing trousers normally have a diamond gusset which ordinary trousers lack.
- Related UKC thread "Workwear instead of expensive kit" recommends Snickers and Helly Hansen workwear, mainly for hillwalking outerwear.
