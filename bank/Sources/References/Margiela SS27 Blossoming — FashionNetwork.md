## Summary
FashionNetwork review of Margiela SS27 (Tuesday 2026-09-29, Élysée Montmartre). Read directly via curl. Quotes Martens' own show notes explaining the tailleur/flou framing, which makes it the primary-level source for that claim.

## Key Claims
- 62 womenswear and menswear silhouettes, Élysée Montmartre, floral installation by Mark Colle; soundtrack Hole's "Violet" (1994).
- Show notes by Martens: "Our research is rooted in the traditions of Parisian ateliers, divided between the 'tailleur', responsible for more rigid, structural garments, and the 'flou', dedicated to the creation of dresses and drape. Here, we are reclaiming the haute couture practices of the 'tailleur' and the 'flou' for this Maison Margiela ready-to-wear show."
- Also from the notes: "rigidity is introduced into archetypal garments... tailoring is enriched with new structural elements; draping is given unprecedented foundations from which it can flow."
- Construction: rigid split darts at the waist on a crêpe de Chine dress; floral-print pieces coated in transparent resin then unpleated so the rigid surface fractured along the old fold lines, giving a vitrified front over a fully supple back.
- Trench coats were the clearest case of the hard/soft treatment; new 5AC bag in softened leather, a Ghost line of vanity cases, Heel-less offer extended to men.
- Martens now works fully for Margiela (managing director Gaetano Sciuto, chairman Stefano Rosso); brand growth "exceeded 8 per cent at constant exchange rates" in 2025, about 100 branded stores and some 500 retailers.
