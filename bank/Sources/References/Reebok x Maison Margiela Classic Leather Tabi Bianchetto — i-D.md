## Summary
i-D staff, 29 Jan 2021, labelled "Created with Reebok" (partner content, so the maker's and partner's framing). Only shoe-specific description of Bianchetto on leather found. Read via WebFetch (a model summary of the page), so second-hand.

## Key Claims
- The shoe uses "the house's signature Bianchetto technique": black nappa leather is hand-painted with a white coating that "intentionally chips off over time".
- Adding the split toe required "an entire re-design of the sneaker in order to accommodate it - sole, upper and all".
- Reebok's Classic Leather was introduced in 1983; the earlier Tabi Instapump Fury was first seen on the Artisanal SS20 runway.
- No year is given for the origin of Bianchetto.
