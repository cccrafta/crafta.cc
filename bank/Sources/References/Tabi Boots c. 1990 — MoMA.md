## Summary
MoMA catalogue record and wall text from the exhibition Pirouette: Turning Points in Design (2025). Read via WebFetch (a model summary of the page), so second-hand.

## Key Claims
- Object dated c. 1990, leather, gift of Linda Loppa, object number 87.2018.a-b; not on view.
- Wall text: tabi are traditional Japanese split-toe socks "at least the 1400s", worn with geta or zori thonged sandals.
- Margiela "debuted in 1988 at his first runway show"; the white boots were dipped in red paint and left footprints on the white canvas-lined runway, and the marked cloth was later made into garments.
- The label reads the Tabi as an example of reworking existing forms, materials or techniques into unusual garments that "eventually become leitmotifs within his practice".
