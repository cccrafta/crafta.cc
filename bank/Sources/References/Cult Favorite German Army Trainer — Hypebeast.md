## Summary
Hypebeast (Gavin Yeung, 14 Jul 2015) summarising Luke Leitch's Wall Street Journal report. This, via Leitch, is the origin of the adidas-versus-Puma question; every later piece found traces back to it. Read via WebFetch (a model summary of the page), so second-hand.

## Key Claims
- An adidas spokesperson confirmed the company "designed a trainer, called the BW-Sport", issued by the German Army in the 1980s and 1990s; the design is "generally attributed to Adidas".
- A spokesperson for the Bundeswehr History Museum in Dresden said they "only had records that the design was made by Puma", "at least in the testing phase".
- Puma told the reporter it "has no records of a shoe made for the government".
- Because the shoes were made for a government agency, "there is zero branding"; Leitch's opening line is that the shoe "has more conflicting histories than the Caucasus".
- Margiela is listed with Dior Homme and adidas as labels producing their own versions; no Margiela date is given.
