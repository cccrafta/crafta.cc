## Summary
Retailer guide to identifying the successive patterns of the Vietnam Tropical Combat Jacket. It is the main source for how the design changed between patterns, but pattern numbering and dating are collectors' conventions, not official.

## Key Claims
- Exposed pocket buttons snagged in vegetation, so the 2nd pattern covered the buttons (retailer; the report also cites What Price Glory, another retailer, and Heddels).
- The 3rd pattern dropped the epaulets, waist tabs and gas flap.
- Ripstop (to stop tears spreading) came with the 4th and 5th patterns, around 1967-68.

## Notes
- Date conflicts: the 2nd pattern is given as Aug 1964, Jan 1965 or 1966; poplin weight is given as 5.5 or 6 oz. Collectors' numbering is not official.
- Snagging on vines, parachute lines and M1956 load-bearing straps is retailer copy only.
- Also supports the first-pattern feature list recorded in the VietnamGear note.
