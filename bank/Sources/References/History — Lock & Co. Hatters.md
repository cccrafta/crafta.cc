## Summary
Maker's history page; silent on where hats are made.

## Key Claims
- Dates to 1676 (Robert Davis); at 6 St James's Street since 1765; billed as the world's oldest hat shop and still family-owned.
- 1849: Edward Coke commissioned a hard hat for gamekeepers; Lock designed it and Thomas Bowler & Son produced it, hence 'Coke' or 'bowler'.
- Ledgers were rehomed at the London Metropolitan Archive in 2014 and can be viewed by the public; a conformateur dating from 1852 is still used to fit hard hats.
- The page does not say where hats are made.
