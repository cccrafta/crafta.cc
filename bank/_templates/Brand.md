## In One Line
What the brand makes, for whom, and why it matters to Crafta, in one or two sentences.

## Lineage
Origin: who founded it, when, where, for what use. Footnote every claim, or mark it `[unsourced]`.

## Making and Material
Where and how it is made; named mills, cloths, weights, finishes, construction details.

## Patina and Care
How the product ages; rewaxing, repair, resoling or reproofing offers. Write "No source" if none.

## Today
What the brand makes now, who wears it for what, collaborations, where it is sold.

## Design
What the pieces look like and why they work: the eye behind them, signature pieces and details, what others copied from it.

## Depth
